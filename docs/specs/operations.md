# Operations (`operations`) - specification

Master prompt section 13. Phase 1 builds one operations feature: the nightly downtime pack with the
back-entry workflow (section 13.4, fire safety and PEEPs row: "a nightly downtime pack per location
(MAR, care plan summaries, allergies, PEEPs, contacts) printed or cached on the kiosk for power cuts
and IT outages, with downtime forms and a back-entry workflow"). The rest of section 13 (occupancy,
domiciliary scheduling, supported living, facilities, catering, front door, day services) arrives in
Phases 3 and 4. Status per feature: [feature register](feature-register.md).

## 1. Purpose and register rows

When the power, the network or Harbour fails, staff still know who is in the building, what they
are allergic to, whether to attempt resuscitation, what texture of food and drink is safe, how each
person communicates and how to get them out; and when systems return, the care written on paper
goes into the record with its true time, marked so nobody mistakes it for a contemporaneous entry.

| Phase 1 rows | Later rows |
| --- | --- |
| OPS-028 nightly downtime pack with back-entry workflow | Fire risk assessment actions and PEEPs (OPS rows for 13.4, Phase 3), which replace the PEEP placeholder; the eMAR (Phase 2), which replaces the medicines placeholder; every other section 13 row |

## 2. Schema (for the schema and API step)

Forced RLS on every table (ADR-0002). An outage is visible to staff at its location and to
tenant-wide roles; a downtime pack follows its location, and its content follows the people in it
(a user sees a pack only if they may see every person on it, which `operations.downtime.read` holders
at the location can).

| Table | Kind | Key columns | Retention class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `downtime_pack` | append-only | location, pack id, source (`server_nightly`, `server_on_demand`, `kiosk_cache`), `generated_at`, `as_at`, `valid_until`, generated by (system or user), content snapshot (JSON from `assembleDowntimePack`), rendered PDF document, warnings summary (counts and opaque ids only) | `downtime_pack`; a pack in force during a declared outage moves to `downtime_record` |
| `outage` | mutable, versioned | location, kind (`OUTAGE_KINDS`), description, `started_at`, `ended_at`, `declared_at`, declared by, declared late (flag), `closed_at`, closed by, `back_entry_due_at`, workflow instance (`OUTAGE_WORKFLOW`), reconciliation (by, at, statement, reviewed duplicate entry ids), cancellation reason | `downtime_record` |
| Back-entry columns on the append-only care events (`care_note`, `observation`, `body_map_mark`, `activity_record`, `visit_log`) | expand only | `late_entry_reason` (`downtime` or null), `outage_id`, `outside_outage_reason`, `paper_reference` | `care_record` (unchanged) |

Prints, downloads, kiosk caching and opens of a pack are audit events, not a table. Field mapping:
[data-standards.md](data-standards.md#packs-and-governance-phase-1-slice-11); purposes:
[records of processing](../compliance/records-of-processing.md).

## 3. Permissions

From `packages/domain/src/platform/permissions.ts` (matrix: [permissions.md](permissions.md)).

| Permission | Allows | Held by (built-in roles) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `operations.downtime.read` | Print or open the location's downtime pack; a kiosk signed in by a holder caches it | Owner, Registered Manager, deputy, clinical lead, team leader |
| `operations.downtime.manage` | Declare, close, reconcile and cancel outages | As above |
| `care.note.write` and the other care write permissions | Back-entering a paper record (the same permission as writing it at the time) | As for the record kind |

Care workers and agency staff read the printed pack the nurse or senior in charge hands out; they
do not hold location-wide read access in Harbour, because their scope is the people assigned to
them.

## 4. Rules that are easy to get wrong

Every rule has a table-driven test in `packages/domain/src/operations/` named with its hazard ID.
Figures come from `rate_table` by key, resolved with `resolveOperationsRates`; the downtime figures
are policy defaults (marked to verify), never statutory.

1. **Content** (`DOWNTIME_PACK_SECTIONS`). For each person: name, date of birth, NHS number, room
   and photo reference; allergies (most severe first); resuscitation status; IDDSI food and drink
   levels with number, name and colour; communication needs; the PEEP; medicines due while the pack
   is in use; key contacts; the active care plan summarised by need (domain order, with
   contingencies); active risk assessments. The first seven are safety-critical (HZ-021): never
   blank. A missing one reads "not recorded" in words, with what to do ("Allergies not recorded.
   Do not assume there are none."); a positive "none" is shown as recorded. Drafts and superseded
   care plans never print (HZ-024).
2. **Freshness** (HZ-021). Every pack carries `generated_at`, `as_at` (when the information was
   read: the server's read time, or a kiosk's last sync) and `valid_until` = `as_at` plus
   `downtime_pack.valid_hours`. Every page's header is worked out when the page is printed or shown
   (`downtimePageHeader`): the location, then "Information as at ... Use until ..., then use a newer
   pack."; once `valid_until` has passed, "Out of date: this information is from ... (N hours ago)
   and should have been replaced by ... Use a newer pack if there is one, and check allergies,
   resuscitation status and diet with the nurse in charge before acting on it." A page printed from
   an out-of-date pack carries those words for ever; a kiosk view recomputes them every time it is
   shown. Every page also carries the print time and the back-entry instruction.
3. **Who is in it.** Everyone receiving care at the location: active, on leave and in hospital
   (they may come back during the outage; their pages say "On leave" or "In hospital"). Discharged,
   deceased, archived and not-admitted people, and people at another location, are left out with
   the reason in the internal record. A person is never left out because their record is
   incomplete; a missing date of birth is a warning. Pages follow the building: room in natural
   order (Room 2 before Room 10), then name; people with no room last.
4. **Wrong person** (HZ-001). A section whose data belongs to another person is withheld, never
   printed: "Not included: this information did not match the person. Check the record when
   Harbour is back." Another person's contact is dropped and the person's own contacts still print.
   The whole pack is never refused for one person's problem: an outage without any pack is worse.
5. **Resuscitation** (HZ-015). The decision in force is printed as the care module computes it
   (`resuscitationStatus`); records that disagree print a warning ("Resuscitation records disagree.
   Check the signed ReSPECT or DNACPR form before acting.") and a decision past its review date says
   it still applies until changed. Harbour does not choose between conflicting records.
6. **Placeholders** (typed ports). Until PEEPs (Phase 3) and the eMAR (Phase 2) exist, the PEEP and
   medicines sections say where to look: "Not kept in Harbour yet. Use the person's PEEP in the fire
   file." and "... Use the paper MAR chart." (`FIRE_FILE_EVACUATION_PORT`,
   `PAPER_MAR_MEDICINES_PORT`). The medicines port lists doses due from `as_at` up to `valid_until`,
   in time order with time-critical medicines first at each time (HZ-003).
7. **Generation.** The worker builds every location's pack nightly and replaces the kiosk cache;
   a holder of `operations.downtime.read` can build one on demand. A kiosk builds one from its own
   cache when the server cannot be reached, with `as_at` its last sync, so it may be out of date
   from the start, and says so. Where the printed pack is kept and who prints it is a deployer
   control in the business continuity plan.
8. **Declaring an outage** (`checkOutageDeclaration`). A kind (power, network, Harbour
   unavailable, devices, planned maintenance, other) and a description of at least
   `MIN_OUTAGE_DESCRIPTION_LENGTH` characters. The start may be before the declaration (it is often
   noticed late) and an outage may be declared after it ended, so that back-entries have a window;
   a declaration more than `care_note.late_entry_hours` hours after the start is flagged. The start
   cannot be in the future beyond `sync.clock_drift_flag_minutes`; a location has at most one open
   outage and outage windows do not overlap.
9. **Closing** (`checkOutageClosure`). The end is not before the start and not in the future. The
   back-entry deadline is the closure plus `downtime.back_entry_due_hours`; overdue back-entry
   escalates to the Registered Manager through the task engine (the closed state's SLA in
   `OUTAGE_WORKFLOW`).
10. **Back-entry** (`checkBackEntry`; HZ-017). A record written on paper is entered with the paper
    time as its event time, the late-entry reason `downtime` and the outage it belongs to; the
    outage must be at the person's location and not cancelled. A paper time outside the outage
    window (start to end, or to the time of entry while the outage is open) is refused unless a
    reason of at least `MIN_BACK_ENTRY_REASON_LENGTH` characters is given, and the reason is shown
    with the entry. A future paper time is refused (`checkEntryTime`). The usual late-entry flag
    (care.md rule 3) still applies, and every back-entry carries a label wherever it appears:
    "Written on paper 11/10/2026 15:05 during the outage (power cut) from 11/10/2026 14:40. Entered
    in Harbour 11/10/2026 19:20." Back-entries refresh the handovers of the shift they happened in
    and the shift that received them (care.md rule 8, late syncs). Corrections supersede, as for
    any care event.
11. **Duplicates and reconciliation** (`possibleDuplicateBackEntries`,
    `checkOutageReconciliation`; HZ-017). Entries for the same person, kind and minute are listed
    as possible duplicates unless their paper references show different forms. A manager confirms
    the outage reconciled only after reviewing each possible duplicate (kept, or corrected by
    supersession) and writing what was checked ("All 14 forms entered and checked"). The summary
    counts back-entries, entries outside the window and people.
12. **Cancelling** an outage declared in error needs a reason and is refused by the API once any
    back-entry cites it.

## 5. API, audit events and outbox events

For the schema and API step (none of this is built in slice 11):

- `GET /v1/locations/{id}/downtime-pack` (latest pack with its freshness; `?format=pdf` renders it
  with the page header from rule 2 on every page), `POST /v1/locations/{id}/downtime-pack`
  (build now). Worker job: nightly per location, idempotent per location and night.
- `GET` and `POST /v1/locations/{id}/outages` (declare), `POST /v1/outages/{id}/close`,
  `/reconcile` (statement and reviewed duplicate ids), `/cancel` (reason).
- Back-entry uses the existing care write routes with a `backEntry` object (`outageId`, `reason`,
  `paperReference`); the server runs `checkBackEntry` and stores the label inputs.
- Audit events: `operations.downtime_pack.generated`, `.printed`, `.opened`, `.cached` (with the
  device), `operations.outage.declared`, `.closed`, `.reconciled`, `.cancelled`; back-entries are
  audited as care writes with the outage id.
- Outbox events: `operations.outage.declared` (the notification centre tells the Registered
  Manager), `operations.outage.closed` (raises the back-entry task), `operations.outage.reconciled`,
  `operations.downtime_pack.generated` (kiosks fetch it).

## 6. Offline behaviour (section 17.1)

| Data | On the device | Writes offline | Conflict handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Latest downtime pack (JSON and PDF) for the location | Kiosk devices at the location; replaced, never evicted, when a newer one arrives | Printing only | The page header is recomputed at print time from the device clock; a pack built from the cache shows its last sync as `as_at` |
| Outages at the location | Devices at the location, read only | Declaring needs the server | Not applicable |
| Back-entries | As the care event | Yes, queued with the outage id | Append-only; possible duplicates are reviewed at reconciliation |

## 7. Clinical safety

Hazards: HZ-021 (no access to essential information during an IT outage, extended with the
out-of-date pack cause and the freshness controls), HZ-017 (late or retrospective entry mistaken for
a contemporaneous record, extended with back-entry from paper), HZ-001 (wrong-person sections
withheld), HZ-003 (medicines due), HZ-007, HZ-015, HZ-016 and HZ-024. See the
[hazard log](../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md). Medical-device boundary: the pack copies recorded
information and says what is missing or where to look; it shows disagreeing resuscitation records
as a warning without choosing between them; nothing scores, predicts or recommends
([medical-device-boundary.md](../compliance/medical-device-boundary.md)).

## 8. Data protection and data standards

A downtime pack holds special category data for everyone at a location, on paper as well as on
screen (records of processing: downtime packs and outages, purpose B1). Printed packs are a
deployer control: kept where the business continuity plan says, and superseded packs shredded.
Retention: `downtime_pack` for nightly packs, `downtime_record` for outages and the packs in force
during them ([retention.md](retention.md)); back-entries stay `care_record`. Field mapping:
[data-standards.md](data-standards.md#packs-and-governance-phase-1-slice-11).

## 9. Acceptance criteria and tests

- Domain (built, slice 11): `packages/domain/src/operations/downtime-pack.test.ts` (content and
  section order; everyone receiving care, nobody else; never left out for an incomplete record;
  not recorded in words; positive none; withheld sections; resuscitation warnings; placeholders;
  medicines window; freshness words on every page; kiosk packs) and
  `packages/domain/src/operations/outage.test.ts` (workflow and SLA; declaration and closure;
  back-entry inside and outside the window, other location, cancelled, future, before the outage;
  labels; duplicates; reconciliation).
- Database: RLS and HZ-011 isolation for `downtime_pack` and `outage`; append-only grants for
  `downtime_pack`; the back-entry columns on each care event table; the cancel refusal once cited.
- API: `hazard HZ-021 the downtime pack contains every required section for every current
  resident`; the PDF has the header on every page; back-entry through each care route; the
  nightly job is idempotent; audit events.
- Web and mobile: Playwright journey (declare, print, back-enter, reconcile) with axe; Maestro
  kiosk journey printing the cached pack offline with the freshness words.

## 10. Open questions

- The policy defaults `downtime_pack.valid_hours` and `downtime.back_entry_due_hours` (24 each)
  need the tenant's business continuity lead to confirm them (H-98).
- The CSO reviews the extended HZ-021 and HZ-017 controls and the section list (H-95).
- The PDF renderer is the Phase 1 PDF decision (phase-1.md); the API step records it in an ADR.
- Paper downtime forms: whether the pack should print blank forms with references per person
  (which would make duplicate detection exact) is left to the web slice.
