# How to set a line manager and keep staff teams

Record who manages each member of staff, and keep teams of staff with a lead. A line manager
and a team lead can then read the training, competence and supervision records of the people
they manage or lead. They never sign off competence or record for them.

**Who can do this**: HR, or the owner. Only someone whose staff file permission covers every
home changes line managers and teams. Others who may open the staff file see the screens but
cannot change them.

**Register row**: WORK-009.

## Set a line manager

1. Open **Staff** in the menu and choose the line manager link for them, for example **Line
   manager for Ada Example**.
2. Under **Name a line manager** (or **Change the line manager**), choose the **Line manager** and
   enter **From**, the first day they are responsible. Choose **Name line manager** (or **Change
   line manager**). Any current line manager stops that day.
3. To end the line with no one in their place, under **End the line**, enter **Ends from**, the
   first day they are no longer the line manager, and choose **End line**.

The page shows the line manager now, since when, and earlier line managers marked **Ended**.

## Keep staff teams

1. Open **Staff teams** in the menu.
2. Under **Add a team**, enter the **Team name** and choose its **Home**, or leave it across every
   home. Choose **Add team**.
3. Open the team. Under **Add a member**, choose the **Member of staff**, tick **They lead the
   team** if they do, enter **Joins from** and choose **Add member**.
4. To change who leads, choose **Make Ada Example the team lead**, or **Ada Example no longer leads
   the team**.
5. When someone leaves the team, under **Ada Example leaves the team**, enter **Leaves from** and
   choose **End Ada Example's membership**.
6. To stop using a team, under **Archive the team**, tick the confirmation and choose **Archive
   team**.

## Important

- A line manager or team lead sees only what their own role already lets them read. Being someone's
  line manager gives no access on its own.
- A line manager at another home can read a report's records but never signs off their competence,
  records their training or supervision, or completes their reminders.
- An archived team gives its lead no access and cannot be used again.
- If someone else saved a change first, reload the page and make your change again.
