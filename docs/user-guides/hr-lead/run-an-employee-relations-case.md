# How to run an employee relations case

As a case's case manager, name the people taking part, move the case through investigation and
hearing, arrange meetings, handle suspension, record referral decisions and close the case.

**Who can do this**: the people named on the case as **Case manager**. Naming new people needs the
employee relations caseworker role as well.

**Register rows**: WORK-010.

## Steps

1. Open **Employee relations** in the menu (or the case's link) and choose the case. "Your part"
   says what you are on the case.
2. Under **Name someone on the case**, choose the **Person** and **Their part** (investigator,
   hearing chair, appeal chair, note taker, HR adviser or another case manager). If the same person
   must take more than one of investigator, hearing chair and appeal chair, tick **No other manager
   is available for this part**. Choose **Name on case**.
3. Under **Run the case**, move it on when it is ready:
   - **Start the investigation**: enter the **Date** and choose **Start investigation**. An
     investigator must be named.
   - **Move to a hearing**: enter the **Date** and choose **Move to hearing**.
4. To arrange the investigation meeting, hearing or appeal hearing, use its form, for example
   **Arrange: Hearing**. Choose who it is **Led by**, enter the **Date the invitation was sent**,
   the **Date of the meeting** and the **Time** (24-hour, for example 14:30). Tick **The issue has been set out in
   writing**, **The evidence has been shared** and **They have been told they may be accompanied**.
   Choose **Arrange**.
5. After the meeting, you or the person who led it records it under **Meetings and hearings**:
   choose **What happened**, and enter the **Date held** and whether **The employee was
   accompanied**, or the **Reason for postponing**. Choose the save button.
6. If the employee appeals after the outcome, under **Record an appeal** enter the **Date** and the
   **Grounds for the appeal**, and choose **Record appeal**.
7. If a referral decision is needed, use the **Record the DBS barring referral decision** or
   **Record the Professional regulator referral decision** form. Choose the **Decision**, with the
   **Date referred** or the **Reason no referral is needed**, and choose **Save decision**.
8. To close the case, under **Close the case** enter the **Date**, choose the **Reason**, tick **I
   understand this case cannot be opened again** and choose **Close case**.

## Important

- Where you can, the investigator, the hearing chair and the appeal chair are different people.
  The member of staff the case is about is never named, and nobody names themselves.
- A hearing can be arranged only once the employee has the issue in writing, the evidence in a
  disciplinary or capability case, and knows they may be accompanied. Who accompanied them is never
  recorded.
- Suspension is only for a disciplinary case: a precaution while the facts are established, never
  a sanction. Use **Suspend** with a **Next review date**, then **Review the suspension** (**Save
  review**) or **End the suspension** (**End suspension**). A review not done shows **Suspension
  review overdue**.
- Thapsus Care refers no one. It asks for the DBS or regulator decision when the outcome needs one,
  and the case cannot close while a decision is missing or a suspension is in force.
- A closed case cannot be opened again. The page says how long it is kept.
- To remove someone, choose the form named for them, for example **Remove Ada Example from the
  case**, tick the box and confirm. They no longer see the case. The last case manager cannot be
  removed.
- Anyone named on an open case can add to the **Timeline** under **Add a note**, with the **Date of
  the note** and the **Note**, then **Add note**.
