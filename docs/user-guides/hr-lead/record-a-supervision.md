# How to record a supervision, appraisal or spot check

Record a supervision, appraisal or spot check you held with a member of staff, and what was
discussed. Thapsus Care then shows when the next supervision and appraisal are due.

**Who can do this**: owner, group executive, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment
or training lead. It is recorded in your name as the person who held it. Nobody records their own.

**Register row**: WORK-009.

## Steps

1. Open **Training** in the menu and choose the member of staff.
2. Under **Supervision and appraisal**, check **Next supervision** and **Next appraisal**: each
   shows **Not due yet**, **Due now** or **Overdue**, and the date.
3. Under **Record a supervision, appraisal or spot check**, choose **What was held** and enter the
   **Date held**.
4. If you want, write **What was discussed** and **Actions agreed**. Write only what the
   supervision needs.
5. Choose **Save supervision record**.
6. To add or correct the note later, choose the link under the record, for example **Add a note:
   Supervision on 03/03/2026** or **Correct the note: Supervision on 03/03/2026**. Write it and
   choose **Save note**. Only the person who held it can do this.

## Good to know

- The note is seen only by supervisors and by the member of staff, and is kept with the staff
  file.
- A correction keeps the earlier note, struck through.
- Spot checks are recorded but do not count as supervisions.
