# How to set the training each role needs

Say which training each role needs and how often it is refreshed. The training matrix and each
member's training record follow these requirements.

**Who can do this**: owner, group executive, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment
or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-005.

## Steps

1. Open **Training** in the menu and choose **Training requirements**.
2. Check the requirements already set, listed by role.
3. Under **Set a role's requirement**, choose the **Role** and the **Topic**. If the role already
   has a requirement for that topic, it is changed.
4. To refresh on a different cycle from your organisation's default, enter **Refresh every
   (years)**. Leave it empty for the default.
5. For the Oliver McGowan Mandatory Training, choose the **Oliver McGowan tier**. Tier 1 is used
   when you leave it empty.
6. Keep **This role needs this training now** ticked. Untick it to stop requiring it.
7. Choose **Save requirement**.

## Good to know

- A requirement that is switched off shows **Not required now**.
- A tier 2 Oliver McGowan record counts for a tier 1 requirement too; a tier 1 record never counts
  for tier 2.
