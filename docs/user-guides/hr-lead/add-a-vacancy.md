# How to add a vacancy

Add a job that is open for applications, and change its status as recruitment goes on.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-001.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu. If there is no job template yet, add one first under **Job
   templates**.
2. Under **Add a vacancy**, choose the **Job template** and enter the **Vacancy title**.
3. Choose the **Location**, or **Across the organisation**, and enter the **Number of positions**.
4. Enter the **Opening date** and, if there is one, the **Closing date**.
5. Tick **Open it for applications now** to open it at once. Otherwise it is saved as a draft.
6. Choose **Add vacancy**.
7. To change its status later, open the vacancy, choose the **Status** under **Change the
   vacancy's status** (**Draft**, **Open**, **Closed**, **Filled** or **Withdrawn**) and choose
   **Save status**.

## Good to know

- Applicants' details are seen only by the recruitment team.
- Applications can be added only while the vacancy is **Open**.
- Once the vacancy is no longer open or a draft, **Equality monitoring** shows how many forms came
  back and counts for each question. Small counts are never shown, and nobody sees anyone's
  answers one by one. Equality answers are kept apart from the applications.
