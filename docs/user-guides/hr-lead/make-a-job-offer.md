# How to make a conditional job offer

Make an offer after the interview, then record whether the applicant accepts. The offer depends on
the pre-employment checks the role needs.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-001, WORK-002.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu, choose the vacancy and choose the interviewed applicant.
2. Under **Make a conditional offer**, enter the **Proposed start date** and tick **They have
   worked in health or social care before** if they have.
3. Choose **Make offer**. **Record the applicant's response** shows the checks the offer depends
   on.
4. When they answer, choose the **Response**: **Accepted**, **Declined** or **Withdrawn by us**.
5. If they accepted, invite them as a member of staff first, then choose them under **Their member
   of staff record**.
6. Choose **Save response**.

## Good to know

- Accepting starts their staff file with the job, the start date and the checks the role needs,
  listed as outstanding. Interview notes and scores stay with the recruitment.
- Declining or withdrawing the offer closes the application.
