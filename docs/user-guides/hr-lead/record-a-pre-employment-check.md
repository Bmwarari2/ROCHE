# How to record a pre-employment check

Record each Schedule 3 check on a member of staff's file: proof of identity, the DBS check, conduct
in earlier care work, references, qualifications, fitness to work, right to work and professional
registration.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.
Never on your own staff file.

**Register rows**: WORK-002, WORK-012.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff files** in the menu and choose the member of staff.
2. Under **Pre-employment checks**, find the check and choose its button, for example **Record
   the DBS check** or **Record a right to work check**.
3. Fill in the fields for that check:
   - **Proof of identity with a photo**: the **Document seen**.
   - **DBS check**: the **Level of check**, **Date of issue**, **Certificate number** (12 digits),
     **Result**, and if they start before the certificate arrives, **Adults' barred list checked
     clear on (optional)**. Tick **They subscribe to the DBS Update Service** if they do.
   - **Conduct in earlier care work**: **Earlier care employers contacted**.
   - **References**: **References received**.
   - **Relevant qualifications**: **Qualifications**, one on each line.
   - **Fitness to work**: tick **They have declared they are fit to do the job** and, if agreed,
     **Reasonable adjustments have been agreed**.
   - **Right to work**: **Date checked**, **How it was checked**, **Permission to work** and, for
     time-limited permission, **Permission ends (time-limited only)**.
   - **Professional registration**: **Register the role needs**, **Part of the NMC register** (NMC
     only), **Registration number (PIN)**, **Renewal due** and **Revalidation due (optional)**.
4. Tick **I have verified this today** if you checked it yourself today. This records you as the
   person who verified it.
5. Choose the save button, for example **Save the DBS check**.

## Important

- Never upload or copy a DBS certificate, and never record what it disclosed. Record only the
  level, date, number and whether it was clear.
- Never record a right to work share code. Each right to work check is added to the ones already
  recorded, so a follow-up check for time-limited permission keeps the earlier one.
- A check verified by the member of staff themselves does not count: someone else must verify it.
- Notes under each check say what still needs attention, for example "Working only under
  supervision until the DBS check is back." or "The right to work was checked after the start
  date."
- Only the nurse part of the NMC register counts as a registered nurse on a shift.
- Some things cannot be recorded on this screen yet: the employment history, the DBS risk
  assessment signature and HR-only documents. The page shows where the employment history stands.
