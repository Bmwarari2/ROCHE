# How to export an employee relations case

Download a whole case to keep with your organisation's records, for example when it closes.

**Who can do this**: the case's case managers only. Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is you
first, with your passkey or authenticator app.

**Register rows**: WORK-010, WORK-011.

## Steps

1. Open **Employee relations** in the menu and choose the case.
2. Under **Export this case**, choose **Download the case (JSON)**.
3. If Thapsus Care asks, confirm with your passkey or authenticator code. You come back to the
   download.
4. Keep the file somewhere only the right people can open it.

## Important

- The export is recorded.
- Cases are never in the organisation's full export, so this is the only way to take a case out.
- If it says **Only the case's case managers export it.** or **The case could not be exported. Try
  again.**, nothing was downloaded.
