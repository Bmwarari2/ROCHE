# How to record a probation decision

Record whether a new member of staff has passed probation, or extend it.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.
Never on your own induction.

**Register row**: WORK-004.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff** in the menu and choose the induction link for them, for
   example **Induction for Ada Example**.
2. Make sure the induction plan has a **Probation end date**. **Decide probation** shows only once
   it has.
3. Under **Decide probation**, choose the **Decision**: **Passed**, **Extended** or **Not passed**.
4. Enter the **Date decided**. If you are extending it, enter the **New end date**.
5. Choose **Save decision**.

## Important

- Not passing probation is a decision to dismiss. Make it through an employee relations case for
  probation; the induction only notes it.
- An extended probation is decided again when the new end date comes.
- The decision is made by someone other than the member of staff, never before their start date.
