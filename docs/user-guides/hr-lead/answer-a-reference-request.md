# How to record and answer a reference request

Record a request for a reference about a former or current member of staff, then record whether
you sent it or declined.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.
Never about yourself.

**Register rows**: WORK-013.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff** in the menu and choose the leaving link for them, for
   example **Leaving for Ada Example**. A leaving must be
   recorded first.
2. Under **Record a reference request**, enter the **Date asked for** and the **Organisation
   asking**, never a person's name.
3. Choose the **Kind of reference**. It starts as **Conduct reference**: in care, this covers
   dates, role, reason for leaving, and any sanction in force or safeguarding concern that led to
   action or a referral. Choose **Factual reference** only on purpose.
4. Choose **Record request**.
5. When you have answered, under **References** open the form named for the organisation, for
   example **Answer Example Care Homes Ltd**. Choose the **Answer**: **Sent**, with the **Date
   sent**, or **Declined**, with the **Reason for declining**. Choose the button of the same name.

## Good to know

- The reference itself is not kept in Thapsus Care, only that one was sent, when and by whom.
- A reference is sent once, in your own name.
