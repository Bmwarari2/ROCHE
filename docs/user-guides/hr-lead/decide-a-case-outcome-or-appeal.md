# How to decide an employee relations case outcome or appeal

As the hearing chair, record the outcome after the hearing. As the appeal chair, record the appeal
outcome, which closes the case.

**Who can do this**: the person named on the case as **Hearing chair** (the outcome) or **Appeal
chair** (the appeal). They may be any colleague, such as a Registered Manager, named by the case
manager.

**Register row**: WORK-010.

## Steps

1. Open the case from **Employee relations** in the menu, or from its link if you do not have that
   menu item.
2. For the outcome, once the case is at **Hearing**, go to **Decide the outcome**:
   - Enter the **Date decided** and choose the **Outcome**.
   - For a written warning, enter the **Date the warning stops counting**, as your policy says.
   - Tick **The right of appeal has been given in writing**.
   - Only if the employee did not attend twice without good reason, tick **Decided without a
     hearing: the employee did not attend twice without good reason**.
   - In a disciplinary case, tick **This takes the employee out of regulated activity** only with an
     action short of dismissal such as removal from care work.
   - Choose **Save outcome**.
3. For an appeal, once the case is at **Appeal**, go to **Decide the appeal**. Enter the **Date
   decided**, choose the **Appeal outcome** and choose **Save and close the case**.

## Important

- Decide only after the hearing, and confirm the right of appeal in writing.
- Some outcomes, such as dismissal, need a DBS or regulator referral decision before the case can
  close. The case manager records it.
- Deciding an appeal closes the case. It cannot be opened again.
