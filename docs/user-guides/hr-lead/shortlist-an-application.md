# How to shortlist an application

Check an applicant against each essential criterion in the job template. An applicant who meets
them all is shortlisted for interview.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register row**: WORK-001.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu, choose the vacancy and choose the applicant.
2. Under **Shortlisting**, tick the "Meets:" line for every criterion the applicant meets.
3. Choose **Save shortlisting**.

## Important

- If any criterion is not ticked, the application is rejected with the reason "An essential
  criterion was not met". A rejected application cannot be opened again.
- The applicant is told nothing by Thapsus Care.
