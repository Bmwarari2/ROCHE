# How to close an application

Close an application that will not go further, with the reason.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-001.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu, choose the vacancy and choose the applicant.
2. Under **Close the application**, choose the **Reason**.
3. Tick **I understand this application cannot be opened again**.
4. Choose **Close application**.

## Important

- A closed application cannot be opened again. If the person applies again, add a new
  application.
- The applicant is told nothing by Thapsus Care.
- An application with an offer waiting is closed by recording the response to the offer instead.
