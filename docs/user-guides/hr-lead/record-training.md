# How to record training

Record training a member of staff has completed, so their training record and the matrix are up to
date. You can also correct a training record.

**Who can do this**: owner, group executive, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment
or training lead. Nobody records their own training.

**Register rows**: WORK-005.

## Steps

1. Open **Training** in the menu and choose the member of staff.
2. Under **Record training**, choose the **Topic**.
3. For the Oliver McGowan Mandatory Training, choose the **Oliver McGowan tier**. Otherwise leave
   **Not applicable**.
4. Enter the **Date completed**. If the certificate has its own end date, enter the **Certificate
   end date**.
5. Enter the **Training provider** if you want, and choose **Record training**.
6. To correct a record, find it under **Training records** and choose the link that names it, for
   example **Correct Fire safety on 03/03/2026**. Change what is wrong and choose
   **Save correction**.

## Good to know

- A correction keeps the earlier version in the record's history.
- Training is valid until the certificate's own end date or, without one, until the refresh set for
  the role.
- Courses finished in **E-learning** are added to the training record without anyone recording
  them.
