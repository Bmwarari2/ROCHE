# How to check the training matrix

See every member of staff's required training as it stands today, and find who needs training.

**Who can do this**: owner, group executive, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment
or training lead, clinical leads and nurses, and team leaders or senior carers.

**Register row**: WORK-005.

## Steps

1. Open **Training** in the menu.
2. Under **Filter the matrix**, choose a **Location** and a **Role** if you want, and tick **Only
   staff with training not done, lapsed or due soon** to see only those needing attention.
3. Choose **Show matrix**. The line above the matrix counts the topics not done, lapsed and due
   soon.
4. To find one person, type part of their name in **Find a member of staff**.
5. Choose a name to open their training record.

## Good to know

- Each topic shows as an icon and a word: **Up to date**, **Due soon**, **Lapsed** or **Not done**,
  or **Not needed for their roles**. Up-to-date topics show "Until" and the date.
- If more staff match than can be shown at once, the page says so: choose a location or role to see
  them all.
- Staff appear only once their roles have training requirements. Set them under **Training
  requirements**.
