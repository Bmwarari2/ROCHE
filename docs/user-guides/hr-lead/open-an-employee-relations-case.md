# How to open an employee relations case

Open a grievance, disciplinary, capability, sickness absence or probation case about a member of
staff. You become its first case manager, and only the people you name on it can see it.

**Who can do this**: the employee relations caseworker. No other role can open a case or see one,
not even the owner or Registered Manager, unless they are named on it.

**Register row**: WORK-010.

## Steps

1. Open **Employee relations** in the menu. You see only the cases you are named on.
2. Under **Open a case**, choose the **Kind of case** and the **Member of staff it is about**.
3. Enter the **Date opened**.
4. Under **Summary**, say briefly what the case is about. Everyone named on the case will see it.
5. Choose **Open case**. It is listed with its stage, **Open**.

## Important

- Cases are kept apart from everything else in Thapsus Care. Only the people named on a case see
  it, and it is left out of the organisation's full export.
- The member of staff the case is about is never named on it and does not see it in Thapsus Care.
  Tell them in writing, as your procedure says.
- You need a second sign-in step (passkey or authenticator app) to use cases. What is done on a case
  is recorded, but only which case and what kind of action, never what was written.
- Next, name the people who will take part. See [How to run an employee relations
  case](run-an-employee-relations-case.md).
