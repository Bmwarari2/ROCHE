# How to record a member of staff leaving

Record a leaving date and reason, work through the leaving checklist and record the exit
interview. Their access ends after their last day.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.
Nobody records their own leaving.

**Register rows**: WORK-013.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff** in the menu and choose the leaving link for them, for
   example **Leaving for Ada Example**.
2. Under **Record a leaving**, enter the **Last day of employment** and choose the **Reason for
   leaving**. Choose **Record leaving**.
3. Under **Leaving checklist**, for property returned and final pay, open the item, for example
   **Mark as done: Property returned**, enter the **Date done** and choose the button of the same
   name.
4. Under **Record the exit interview**, choose the **Exit interview** state: **Offered**,
   **Declined** or **Held**. If it was held, enter the **Exit interview date** and a **Summary** of
   what they said, in your words. Choose **Save exit interview**.

## Important

- Their access ends at the end of their last day. If they work a shift that starts that day, it
  ends at the end of that shift, so nobody is cut off mid-shift. To end it at once, see "How to end
  a leaver's access now".
- Leaving items not done by the day after the last day show **Overdue**.
- The leaver may see the exit interview summary.
- The page says how long the staff file is kept. With no date of birth on the file, it is kept
  until someone decides, and HR is asked to record the date of birth.
- The leaving date is recorded once.
