# How to add an e-learning course

Add a SCORM or xAPI course for staff to take in Thapsus Care. Finishing it adds the topic to their
training record.

**Who can do this**: owner, group executive, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment
or training lead.

**Register row**: WORK-006.

## Steps

1. Open **E-learning** in the menu.
2. Under **Add a course**, enter the **Course title**.
3. Choose the **Training topic** that finishing the course records. For the Oliver McGowan
   Mandatory Training, also choose the **Oliver McGowan tier**.
4. Under **Package**, choose the course's zip file (SCORM 1.2, SCORM 2004 or xAPI, up to 100 MB).
5. Choose **Add the course**. While it is checked, the button says **Checking the package**. When it
   is done, **Course added** shows and the course is listed under **Courses**.
6. To stop offering a course, choose the button under it that names the course, for example
   **Retire Fire safety for new starters**.

## Good to know

- Thapsus Care checks every file in the package and scans it for malware before staff are offered
  it.
- Retiring a course happens at once, with no confirmation. A retired course is still listed but
  cannot be opened.
