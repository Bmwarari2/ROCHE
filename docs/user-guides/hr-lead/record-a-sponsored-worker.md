# How to record a sponsored worker

Keep the records your sponsor licence asks for: the certificate of sponsorship, visa, hours, pay,
English language evidence and the Appendix D records you hold. Thapsus Care keeps the records and
the reporting dates; it never decides anyone's immigration status.

**Who can do this**: owner and HR, recruitment or training lead only. Registered Managers and deputy
managers do not see sponsor records. Nobody sees or keeps their own sponsor record.

**Register rows**: WORK-003, WORK-011.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff files** in the menu and choose **Sponsored workers**. (If you only handle sponsor
   records, **Staff files** opens **Sponsored workers** directly.)
2. Under **Record a sponsored worker**, choose the **Member of staff**.
3. Enter the **Certificate of sponsorship number**, **Date the certificate was assigned** and
   **Occupation code (SOC 2020)**, as on the certificate.
4. Choose the **Route** and enter the **Visa end date**.
5. Enter the **Contracted hours a week** and **Guaranteed basic pay a year, in pounds** (before tax,
   without allowances).
6. Choose **How English language was shown**. If they switched to this route in the UK, enter
   **Switching evidence checked on (optional)**.
7. Under **Appendix D records held**, tick each record you hold.
8. Choose **Record the sponsored worker**.

## Good to know

- The record shows, each as an icon and a word, whether the visa is valid, ending soon or expired,
  whether pay **Meets the salary rules** or is **Below the salary rules**, and which Appendix D
  records are missing.
- To change the record, open it and use **Change the record**, then **Save the record**. When
  sponsorship ends, enter **Sponsorship ended on (optional)** there, and record it as a change to
  report too.
- Sponsor records are HR only. Every time someone opens them it is recorded.
