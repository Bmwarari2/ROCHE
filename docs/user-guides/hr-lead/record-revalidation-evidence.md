# How to record a nurse's revalidation evidence

Track a registered nurse's evidence for their next NMC revalidation against the NMC's figures. HR
records it from the nurse's evidence; the nurse revalidates with the NMC themselves.

**Who can do this**: owner, group executive, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment
or training lead. Nobody records their own.

**Register row**: WORK-012.

## Steps

1. Open **Training** in the menu and choose the nurse.
2. Go to **NMC revalidation**. If evidence is recorded, it shows the revalidation date, the days
   left, and each requirement as **Met** or **To do**.
3. Under **Record revalidation evidence**, enter the **Revalidation date**.
4. Enter the totals so far: **Practice hours so far**, **Continuing professional development
   hours so far**, **Participatory learning hours so far**, **Pieces of feedback so far** and
   **Reflective accounts so far**.
5. Tick each that is done: **The reflective discussion has been held**, **The health and
   character declaration is made**, **Professional indemnity is in place** and **A confirmer has
   signed the NMC confirmation**.
6. Choose **Save evidence**.

## Good to know

- There is one record per revalidation date. Saving again updates it.
- When every requirement is met it shows **Ready to revalidate**. Once the date passes without it,
  it says the revalidation date has passed.
- Thapsus Care only tracks revalidation. It does not revalidate anyone with the NMC.
- The registration number and renewal date are kept on the staff file's professional registration
  check.
