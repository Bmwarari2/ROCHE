# How to start a staff file

A staff file holds a member of staff's pre-employment checks under Schedule 3 and shows where each
one stands today. Start one for each new member of staff before you record their checks.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.
Nobody can start or change their own staff file.

**Register row**: WORK-002.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff files** in the menu.
2. Under **Start a staff file**, choose the **Member of staff**. Only current staff without a file
   are listed, and never you.
3. Enter the **Job title** and choose **Where they mainly work**, or **Across the organisation**.
4. Fill in what applies:
   - **Care Workforce Pathway category (optional)**, as your organisation names it.
   - **Register the role needs**: choose the register for a nurse or other registered role. This
     adds the professional registration check. Choose **None** otherwise.
   - Tick **They have worked in care before** if they have. This adds the check on their conduct in
     earlier care work.
   - **Start date (optional)**.
   - **Date of birth (optional)**: only needed if the file is kept to their 75th birthday or they
     are under 18.
5. Choose **Start the staff file**. The file opens with each check and its status.

## Good to know

- Each check shows as an icon and a word: **Done**, **Due soon**, **Expired**, **Not recorded** or
  **Not needed**. The file says **Complete** only when every check it needs is done and none has
  expired; otherwise it says **Checks outstanding**.
- To change the job, location, register, dates or leaving date later, open the file and use
  **Change the profile**, then **Save the profile**.
- Staff files hold health and criminal records information. You need a second sign-in step
  (passkey or authenticator app) to use them. Every change is recorded with who made it.
- On the file, anyone who can see staff files also sees whether the member of staff has confirmed
  they read the staff privacy notice, and their answers about photos.
- A member of staff who opens their own file sees "This is your own staff file. Someone else
  records your checks."
