# How to record absences and report changes for a sponsored worker

Some changes for a sponsored worker must be reported on the Home Office sponsor management system
within a set time. Record the absence or change here, report it on the sponsor management system,
then mark it as reported.

**Who can do this**: owner and HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-003.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff files** in the menu, choose **Sponsored workers** and choose the worker.
2. To record an absence, under **Record an absence**, enter the **First day away** and **Last day
   away**, answer **Was the absence authorised?** and choose **Record the absence**.
3. If **Unauthorised absence to report** shows at the top, unauthorised absence has passed the
   limit. The form under **Record a change to report** is filled in for you.
4. Under **Record a change to report**, choose the **Change** and the **Date it happened**, then
   choose **Record the change**. It shows under **Reportable changes** with the date to report it
   by.
5. Report it on the sponsor management system.
6. Under **Reportable changes**, choose the button that names the change, for example **Mark
   change of hours as reported**. Enter the **Date reported** and the **Sponsor management system
   reference**, then choose the button of the same name.

## Important

- Each change shows its state as an icon and a word: **To report**, **Overdue**, **Reported** or
  **Reported late**.
- The date to report by is worked out for you from the day it happened, skipping weekends and bank
  holidays. Report by the date shown.
- Thapsus Care does not report to the Home Office for you.
