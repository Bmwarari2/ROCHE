# How to add an application

Add an application to an open vacancy as it comes in, so you can shortlist, interview and make an
offer.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-001.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu and choose the vacancy.
2. Under **Add an application**, enter the applicant's **First name** and **Last name** as they
   gave them.
3. Enter their **Email address** and **Phone number** if you have them.
4. Choose **Add application**. It is listed under **Applications** with its stage, **Applied**.

## Good to know

- Applicants may never be employed, so only the recruitment team sees their details.
- Once an application closes without a job, the applicant's details are kept until the date shown
  as "Kept until" on the application, then removed. Their name then shows as **Applicant details
  removed**.
- Interview notes and scores are never copied into the staff file.
