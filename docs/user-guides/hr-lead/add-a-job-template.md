# How to add a job template

A job template sets the essential criteria checked at shortlisting and the values-based questions
asked at interview, for a Care Workforce Pathway role. Every vacancy is based on one.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register row**: WORK-001.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu and choose **Job templates**.
2. Under **Add a job template**, enter the **Job title** and choose the **Care Workforce Pathway
   role**.
3. If the role needs a register, choose it under **Professional registration needed**. Otherwise
   leave **None needed**.
4. Under **Essential criteria**, write at least 2 criteria, one on each line.
5. Under **Values-based interview questions**, write each question on its own line, starting with
   the value it explores and a colon, for example "compassion: Tell us about a time you comforted
   someone."
6. Choose **Add template**.

## Good to know

- Each essential criterion is checked for every applicant before shortlisting.
- The values are care, compassion, competence, communication, courage and commitment.
