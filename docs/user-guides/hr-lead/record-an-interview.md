# How to record an interview

Record a values-based interview: the panel, a score for each answer and the panel's
recommendation.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.

**Register rows**: WORK-001.

## Steps

1. Open **Vacancies** in the menu, choose the vacancy and choose the shortlisted applicant.
2. Under **Record an interview**, enter the **Interview date**.
3. Choose **Panel member 1**, and panel members 2 and 3 if there were more.
4. Score each question from 1 (weak) to 4 (strong).
5. Choose the **Panel's recommendation**: **Proceed** or **Do not proceed**.
6. Add **Notes** if you want, and choose **Record interview**.

## Important

- The total score is shown with the scores, never used to decide. The decision is the panel's.
- An offer can be made only after an interview whose panel recommended **Proceed**.
