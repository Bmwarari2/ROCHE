# How to run a new starter's induction

Track a new member of staff's induction: the checklist, the induction plan, shadow shifts and
probation reviews.

**Who can do this**: owner, Registered Manager, deputy manager, HR, recruitment or training lead.
Nobody records on their own induction.

**Register row**: WORK-004.

## Steps

1. Open **Staff** in the menu and choose the induction link for them, for
   example **Induction for Ada Example**.
2. Under **Induction checklist**, check each item's status: **Done**, **To do**, **Overdue** or
   **Not needed**.
3. For an item you tick by hand, open it, for example **Mark as done: Contract issued**, enter the
   **Date done** and choose **Mark as done: Contract issued** again.
4. Under **Induction plan**, enter the **Probation end date**. Tick **They already hold the Care
   Certificate** if they do. Choose **Save plan**.
5. After each shadow shift, under **Record a shadow shift**, enter the **Shift date**, choose the
   **Colleague shadowed** and choose **Record shadow shift**.
6. After each probation review, under **Record a probation review**, enter the **Review date**,
   choose **How it is going** (**On track** or **Concerns**), add **Notes** if you want, and choose
   **Record review**.

## Good to know

- You tick these by hand: contract issued, system access set up, uniform and badge given, induction
  at the location, fire safety briefing and shadowing signed off.
- Thapsus Care works out the rest: **Pre-employment checks complete** from the staff file,
  **Policies read and signed** from their policy signatures, **Care Certificate** from the 16
  standards, and **Probation passed** from the probation decision.
- An item still to do after the start date shows **Overdue**.
