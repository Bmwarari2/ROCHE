# How to read updates from the home

The home can share short everyday updates about your relative or friend, such as activities they
joined.

**Register row**: COMMS-001.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Harbour with the email address the home has for you.
2. The first time, Harbour asks you to confirm it is you. Choose
   **Set up a passkey or a phone code** and follow the steps. You can use a passkey, or an
   authenticator app on your phone.
3. You see **Updates**. The newest updates come first.

## Good to know

- These are everyday updates. They are not the full care record.
- **The home will phone you about anything important**, such as a fall, a hospital visit or illness.
  These are never posted here.
- If an update was not right, it may be taken down. You see "Taken down".
- If you see "Your account has no access yet", ask the home to link your account to the updates.
- If there are no updates, the home may not have shared any yet. Ask the home if you are not sure.
