# How to run a staff survey

Write a short anonymous survey for staff at a home or across the organisation, open it, and close
it.

**Who can do this**: people who run staff wellbeing (Registered Manager, deputy manager, owner).
Only someone who manages every home can ask the whole organisation.

**Register row**: SCHED-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Wellbeing** in the menu, choose **Staff surveys**, then **Write a survey**.
2. Under **Who is asked?**, choose a home or **The whole organisation**.
3. Enter a **Title**, the **Opens on** date and the **Closes after** date (the last day staff can
   answer), as day, month and year.
4. For each question, write the **Question** and choose **Answered with**: **A number from 1 to 5**
   or **Written words**. Choose **Add a question** for more, up to 20. Ask about the work, never
   about a named person.
5. Choose **Save draft**.
6. When it is ready, open the survey and choose **Open survey**, then confirm. Staff see it under
   **Surveys for you**.
7. The survey closes by itself within the hour after its last day. To close it early, choose
   **Close survey** and confirm.

## Important

- Its questions and dates cannot change once it opens. Closing cannot be undone.
- Answers are not linked to anyone: Thapsus Care keeps that a person took part, never who gave
  which answer or when.
- You cannot answer a survey you wrote, or one at a home where you run surveys.
- Results show only after the survey closes. See [how to read staff survey
  results](read-staff-survey-results.md).
