# How to prepare a statutory notification

Fill in what the regulator's form asks, so it is ready to enter on their portal.

**Who can do this**: people who manage incidents (Registered Manager, deputy, nurse or clinical
lead, owner). Only the Registered Manager and the owner can then record it as submitted.

**Register rows**: SAFE-003, SAFE-004, SAFE-005.

## Steps

1. Go to **Incidents**, then **Statutory notifications**, and open the notification. Or open it
   from the incident page, or from the overdue alert.
2. Read **Deadlines**, **Why Harbour drafted it** and **What it must include**.
3. Under **Prepare the notification**, fill in each box as you will enter it on the regulator's
   portal.
4. Choose **Save as a draft** as you go.
5. When every answer is there, choose **Save and mark ready to submit**.
6. If you are not the Registered Manager or the owner, tell them it is ready. They record the
   submission.

## Good to know

- Harbour does not send anything to the regulator. It helps you prepare and keeps the record.
- The legal wording ("What it must include", "Where the duty comes from") is in English only for
  now.
- To change a notification that is ready, choose **Move back to draft**.
- A deputy manager can prepare but cannot record a submission, unless the organisation gives
  them an acting-up grant. Acting-up grants have no web screen yet.
