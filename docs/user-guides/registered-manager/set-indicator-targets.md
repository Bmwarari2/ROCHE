# How to set indicator targets

Set your organisation's own targets for the quality dashboard: an organisation default for each
indicator, and a home's own target where it needs one.

**Who can do this**: a home's own target: the Registered Manager and the deputy manager for
their homes, and the owner, the group executive and the quality and compliance lead. The
organisation default, and a home's target looser than it: only the owner, the group executive
and the quality and compliance lead, who manage quality for the whole organisation.

**Register row**: QUAL-005.

## Steps

1. In the menu, choose **Quality dashboard**, then **Set targets for** and the home's name.
2. The page **Indicator targets** has a section for each indicator Thapsus Care counts: **Falls**,
   **Pressure ulcers**, **Medication incidents**, **Overdue reviews** and **Incidents reviewed in
   time**.
3. Under **Organisation default** or the home's own target, see the current target or **No
   target set**.
4. Fill in the two numbers. For an indicator where lower is better:
   - **Within target at or below**;
   - **Close to target up to**.
   Where higher is better, the fields say **Within target at or above** and **Close to target down
   to**. The hint says the unit, for example a percentage from 0 to 100, or a rate per occupied
   bed-days.
5. Choose **Save** with the indicator and level named, for example **Save Rose House's target for
   Falls**. The page says "Target saved."

## Clear a target

1. Read the note that says what the indicator will then be rated against, for example "Falls at
   Rose House will then be rated against the organisation default, if there is one."
2. Choose **Clear** with the indicator and level named, for example **Clear Rose House's target for
   Falls**. The page says "Target cleared."

## Good to know

- Targets are your organisation's own, never a Thapsus Care score. Thapsus Care does not suggest one.
- A home's own target wins over the organisation default. On the dashboard, the organisation
  default is shown beside it.
- A home's target cannot be less strict than the organisation's, unless someone who manages
  quality for the whole organisation sets it.
- With no target, a figure is shown but not rated.
- The rules are in the [quality specification](../../specs/quality.md#22-quality-dashboard-and-pir-prefill-slice-47-qual-005-qual-006-migration-0108).
