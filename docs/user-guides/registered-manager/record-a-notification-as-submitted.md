# How to record a statutory notification as submitted, or as not required

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager and the owner only. You must confirm it is you with
your passkey or authenticator app (step-up).

**Register rows**: SAFE-002, SAFE-003, SAFE-004, SAFE-005.

## Record it as submitted

1. Make sure the notification shows **Ready to submit**. If it is still a draft, prepare it first
   ([How to prepare a statutory notification](prepare-a-statutory-notification.md)).
2. Submit it on the regulator's own portal or form. Harbour does not send it for you.
3. In Harbour, open the notification and go to **Record that it was submitted**.
4. Under **When was it submitted?** choose **Just now**, or **Earlier** with the date and time.
5. Enter **The regulator's reference**: the confirmation number the portal gave you.
6. Choose **Record as submitted**.
7. If Harbour shows **Confirm it is you**, choose **Sign in again**, then sign in with your
   passkey, or your password and a code from your authenticator app. You come back to the page.
   Enter what you typed again: it is not kept.
8. The page says "Recorded as submitted". The overdue alerts stop.

## Record it as not required

Only if the law does not need it. If in doubt, notify. This cannot be undone.

1. Open the notification and go to the not-required section.
2. Under **Why is it not required?**, say which facts mean the law does not need it.
3. If Harbour says "Harbour found this notification is needed", write at least 20 characters and
   tick **I have checked the facts, and the law does not need this notification**.
4. Choose **Record as not required**, and confirm it is you if asked.

## Good to know

- Step-up asks you to have signed in with a passkey or code in the last 10 minutes. A password
  alone never counts.
- The reason is kept with the notification and in the audit trail.
- You cannot upload the submitted form on the web yet.
