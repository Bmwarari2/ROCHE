# How to name the lone-working on-call route

Choose who is told, and in what order, when a lone worker asks for help or stops checking in.
Nobody can start a lone-working session until a valid route is saved.

**Who can do this**: people who manage lone working (Registered Manager, deputy manager, owner).

**Register row**: SCHED-012.

## Steps

1. Go to **Lone working** in the menu and choose **On-call route**.
2. Under **Lone working**, check whether it is **Switched on**, and read anything under **What to
   fix**.
3. Under **Steps**, for **Step 1** choose **Who is told**: the on-call person or role, told at once
   when an alarm is raised.
4. Under **How they are told**, tick **Email**. You can also tick **Phone notification** or **In
   Thapsus Care only**, but every step must include email.
5. Choose **Add a step** and, for the next step, enter **Minutes after the alarm** and choose the
   Registered Manager under **Who is told**. Add more steps if you need them; each comes later than
   the one before.
6. Choose **Save route**. The page shows **Switched on** once the route is valid.

## Important

- The worker is reminded first. When the grace period ends the alarm is raised: step 1 is told at
  once and later steps follow by their minutes. The alarm is sent again to everyone on the route
  until someone acknowledges it on the **Lone workers now** board.
- Text messages are not available at launch, and phone notifications cannot reach staff until
  their phones are registered for them. Email is how people away from Thapsus Care are told.
- You can name only roles and people who can see and acknowledge alarms. If nobody but the worker
  could be told, a session cannot start.
- Thapsus Care tells people; it does not send help. Make sure whoever is on call knows to act, and
  to call 999 if a worker's safety cannot be confirmed.
