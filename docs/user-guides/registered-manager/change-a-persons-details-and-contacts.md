# How to change a person's details and contacts

Keep a person's personal details, their national data opt-out, and their contacts and legal
representatives up to date.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers and the owner. Everyone who can open
the person's record can read the tab.

**Register rows**: PEOP-001, PEOP-002.

## Change personal details

1. Open the person's record and choose **Personal details**.
2. Under **Who they are**, choose **Change personal details**.
3. Change what is needed, for example the **Preferred name**, which is shown first on every
   screen, or the **NHS number** (10 digits; spaces are fine). Use the **Local identifier** when
   there is no NHS number.
4. Choose **Save personal details**. The page says "Personal details saved".

## Record the national data opt-out

1. Under **National data opt-out**, the page says **Opted out** or **Not opted out**.
2. Choose **Record that they have opted out**, or **Record that they have not opted out**. The page
   says "Opt-out recorded" or "Opt-out removed".

## Add or change a contact or representative

1. Under **Contacts and representatives**, choose **Add a contact or representative**, or **Change**
   on a contact's details.
2. Choose **Who they are**, for example **Next of kin**, **Attorney for health and welfare (LPA)**
   (lasting power of attorney) or **Court of Protection deputy**, and enter their name and
   details.
3. For an attorney, deputy or appointee, fill in **Their authority**: the Office of the Public
   Guardian, court or Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) reference, the evidence (a copy
   already filed in the person's documents) and **The end date**, if there is one.
4. Choose **Add contact** or **Save contact**.

## Important

- **An attorney, deputy or appointee acts only within their authority, and only while it lasts.**
  Nothing is shared on an authority until its reference or evidence is recorded: the contact says
  **Evidence not recorded**.
- If you change an authority so it no longer covers what was shared, or it ends sooner, the
  sharing decisions that rest on it are suspended at once, until someone reviews them on the
  **Sharing** tab (see [How to record a sharing decision](record-a-sharing-decision.md)).
- Changing the email address of a family member who has a portal account needs you to confirm it
  is you with your passkey or authenticator app.
- Thapsus Care does not check the opt-out with the NHS national service yet: it shows what the
  home records.
- Every change is kept in the record's history (see
  [How to see the history of a record](../all-staff/view-a-records-history.md)).
