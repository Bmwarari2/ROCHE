# How to decide a leave request

Approve or decline a member of staff's request for leave, and see which shifts then need cover.

**Who can do this**: people who approve rotas and timesheets (Registered Manager, deputy manager,
owner). People who build rotas, such as a care coordinator, can see the home's absences.

**Register row**: SCHED-010.

## Steps

1. Choose the home, then go to **Absences** in the menu. Requests **Waiting for a decision** come
   first, then absences that leave shifts needing cover.
2. Read the request: who, the kind of leave and the dates.
3. Under **Your decision**, choose **Approve** or **Decline**.
4. Under **Reason (optional when approving)**, write a reason. The member of staff sees it. A
   decline needs a reason of at least 3 characters.
5. Choose **Save decision**.
6. If the absence shows, for example, "2 shifts need cover", go to the rota and take the person
   off those shifts so someone else can be put on.

## Important

- You cannot decide your own request.
- Once leave is approved, the person stays on their shifts but each one is marked as needing cover,
  so it never empties without anyone noticing. The rota check counts those shifts as open, and
  people who build rotas at the home get a task for each one.
- Nobody can be put on a shift on a day they have approved leave.
- Carer's leave and parental leave can only be postponed as the law allows, not declined. A
  decline of these is refused, and postponing is not on the web yet.
- An absence copied from your HR platform shows **Managed in the HR platform**. Change it there.
- Recording sickness, fit notes, return to work meetings and the Bradford factor are not on the web
  yet.
