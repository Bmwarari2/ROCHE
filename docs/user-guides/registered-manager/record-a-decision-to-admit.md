# How to record a decision to admit

Decide, with reasons, whether the home can meet a person's needs after their pre-admission
assessment, and confirm any conditions before they move in.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers and the owner.

**Register row**: PEOP-005.

## Steps

1. Open the person's **Pre-admission** tab. The assessment must say **Ready for a decision**. If
   it says "Finish every required section, the sources and how the person took part before
   recording a decision.", it is not finished yet.
2. Under **Decision to admit**, **Record the decision**, answer **Can the home meet this person's
   needs?**: **Can meet needs**, **Can meet needs with conditions** or **Cannot meet needs**.
3. Write the **Reasons for the decision**: what the assessment found and why it leads to this
   decision.
4. For **Can meet needs with conditions**, list each of the **Conditions**: what must be in place
   before the person moves in, such as equipment or training. Use **Add a condition** for more.
5. If known, enter the **Planned admission date (optional)** and, for a trial placement, **Trial
   ends (optional)**.
6. Enter **Who was told about the decision (optional)** and **When they were told (optional)**:
   both, or neither.
7. Choose **Record the decision**. The page says "Decision recorded".

## Conditions

When each condition is in place, choose its button, for example "Confirm condition 1 is met".
The page says "Condition confirmed as met". Once every condition is met, the page says "The decision
is to admit and every condition is met." Choose **Record the admission** (see
[How to record an admission and a room](record-an-admission-and-a-room.md)).

## Good to know

- To change a decision, choose **Record a new decision** and say **Why the earlier decision is
  being replaced**. The earlier decision stays on the record, struck through, under **Earlier
  decisions**.
- If the decision is **Cannot meet needs**, close the enquiry as not admitted (see
  [How to record an enquiry](record-an-enquiry.md)), or record a new decision if something has
  changed.
