# How to run the safeguarding log

Take a safeguarding concern from raised to closed: refer it or record why not, record the
enquiry and its outcome, and keep the protective measures up to date.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager and the owner. Nurses, senior
carers and care workers can raise a concern but cannot run the log.

**Register row**: SAFE-006.

## Open the log

When a concern is raised about someone at your home, you are told straight away in Harbour: an
urgent notification, "A safeguarding concern has been raised", and a task, "Decide on a
safeguarding referral", due at the referral target. Harbour does not send it to your phone or
email yet. If no manager opens the concern, the next person in your organisation's escalation
chain is told (by default the owner). Once a manager other than the one who raised it opens the
concern, nobody further is told. Referring the concern, or recording why not, completes the task.
If you raised the concern yourself, the other managers at your home are told, not you; if you are
the only one, managers at your organisation's other homes are told.

1. Choose **Safeguarding** in the menu. The **Safeguarding log** lists open concerns first.
2. Each concern shows its state (for example **Concern raised** or **Referred**), its referral
   clock in words, who it is about with their communication needs, and the kinds of abuse.
3. Open a concern by its title, **Concern raised** and the date and time.

## Steps

1. Read **Who it is about**, any warnings at the top, and **What happened**.
2. Under **Update the record**, fill in only what has happened, then choose **Save**. Sections
   left empty keep what is already recorded:
   - **Referral to the local authority**: **Local authority**, **How it was referred**, **When it
     was referred** and **Their reference**.
   - **Enquiry**: **Kind of enquiry** and **When the enquiry opened**.
   - **Outcome and the person's wishes**: **Finding**, **Risk from staff**, **Was the person asked
     what outcomes they wanted?**, **The outcomes they wanted** or **Why they were not asked**, and
     **Were their outcomes achieved?**
   - **Disclosure and Barring Service**: **Referral to the Disclosure and Barring Service**
     (**Referred** or **Not required**) and **Why**.
   - **Protective measures**: tick **Change the protective measures** and write what staff must
     do now.
   - **What will happen to the protective measures when the concern ends?**: choose **Carried
     into the care plan or risk assessment** or **No longer needed**. Changing the measures clears
     this answer, so answer it again when you change them. The answer shows under **What happens
     to the measures when the concern ends**.
3. Then take the next step. Each step has its own section, for example **Mark as referred**,
   **Decide not to refer** (with **Why**), **Open the enquiry**, **Record the outcome** or **Close
   the concern** (with **Why**).
4. If a step lists **Before you can do this:**, record those things under **Update the record**
   first. The step's button appears when nothing is missing. For example, before you mark a
   concern referred or decide not to refer, answer **Was the person asked what outcomes they
   wanted?**; before the outcome, answer **Risk from staff**.
5. Each step saves with "Saved".

## When a concern with protective measures will not end

Before a concern with protective measures can be closed, or not referred, Harbour asks whether the
measures were carried into the care plan or risk assessment, or are no longer needed, so they are
never lost. If the step lists this under **Before you can do this:**, make sure the measures are in
the care plan or risk assessment if they are still needed, then answer **What will happen to
the protective measures when the concern ends?** under **Update the record** and choose **Save**.

## Good to know

- The referral clock is your organisation's target, not a legal deadline: the law says without
  delay. An overdue concern alerts the managers of the log urgently, and again each day until it
  is referred or a reason not to refer is recorded.
- Harbour never refers a concern, decides an enquiry or sends anything to the local authority, the
  Care Quality Commission or the Disclosure and Barring Service. You do that, then record it here.
- Recording the referral here does not record the s.42 notification as submitted. If the concern
  came from one, a warning says the notification is still open. Record it as submitted (see
  [How to record a notification as submitted](record-a-notification-as-submitted.md)).
- Whoever raised a concern cannot decide alone that it is not referred, or close it. Ask another
  manager.
- Closing is final: the concern cannot be reopened. Closing needs whether the person's outcomes
  were achieved and, where a member of staff was or would have been removed, the DBS decision.
- What the raiser wrote in **What happened** cannot be changed.
- Carers see only the protective measures, on the person's **Safeguarding measures** tab (see
  [How to check a person's safeguarding measures](../care-worker/check-safeguarding-measures.md)).
- The rules: [safety specification, safeguarding log](../../specs/safety.md#safeguarding-log-plan-slice-13-safe-006-hz-126)
  and hazard [HZ-126](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-126-a-safeguarding-concern-not-progressed-or-protective-measures-not-visible-to-the-staff-caring-for-the-person).
