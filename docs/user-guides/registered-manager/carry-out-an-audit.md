# How to carry out an audit or mock inspection

An audit asks a set of scored questions at one location. You answer each one, add an action for
each shortfall and give your own self-rating. A mock inspection works the same way.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager, the owner, the group executive and
the quality and compliance lead, at the locations where they run audits. Care workers, senior
carers and nurses cannot.

**Register row**: QUAL-004.

## Start the audit

1. In the side bar, choose **Quality audits**, then **Start an audit**.
2. Under **Audit details**, choose the **Template**. Each says whether it is an audit or a mock
   inspection.
3. Choose the **Location**.
4. Enter **Date carried out** as day, month and year. It cannot be in the future.
5. Choose **Start audit**. The page says "Audit started. Answer each question and save as you go."

## Answer the questions

1. Under **Answers**, in **Answer each question**, choose **Yes**, **Partly**, **No** or **Not
   applicable** for each question. Each question shows its weight and whether "A no or partly needs
   an action."
2. Choose **Save answers** as you go. The page says "Answers saved."
3. **Score** shows the score so far and how it is worked out. Until every question is answered it
   also says how many are not answered yet.

## Complete the audit

1. When every question is answered and saved, go to **Complete the audit**. Until then it says
   **Not ready to complete**.
2. Under **Actions and self-rating**, for each answer that fell short (**Action for:** the
   question), fill in:
   - **What will be done**;
   - **Owner**: choose from the list;
   - **Due date** (optional). Left empty, your organisation's default is used.
3. Under **Your self-rating**, choose **Inadequate (1)**, **Requires improvement (2)**, **Good
   (3)** or **Outstanding (4)**. This is your own judgement on the CQC rating scale.
4. Choose **Complete audit**. The page says "Audit completed. Its actions are with their owners."

## Important

- **The score never works out the self-rating.** The score is the weighted share of points: yes
  counts in full, partly counts half, no counts nothing, and not applicable is left out. If no
  question can be scored, it says "No score: no question could be scored", never 0. The
  self-rating is always a person's choice, recorded with who gave it.
- A completed audit cannot be changed.
- Each action owner gets a task. Owners must run audits at the location. If you cannot list the
  organisation's members, you can only choose yourself.
- A mock inspection can only start against the framework version in force. An older audit keeps
  the version it was scored against and says "Scored against an earlier framework version".
- Completed audits show under **Completed audits** with their scores, and mock inspections under
  **Mock inspections**. **What fell short, by quality statement** lists the findings.
- To use a completed audit as evidence, choose **Tag as evidence** (see
  [How to tag a record as evidence](tag-a-record-as-evidence.md)).
- The rules: [quality spec section 17, rules 9 to 15](../../specs/quality.md#17-phase-2-domain-rules-slice-8-qual-001-002-004-005-006-008-012).
