# How to withdraw a lost or unused phone

When a phone used for Care mode is lost, stolen, replaced, or belonged to someone who has left,
withdraw it, so it can no longer reach Thapsus Care and removes the care records stored on it.

**Who can do this**: Registered Managers, deputy managers and owners. Others who can see the list
can see the phones but not withdraw them.

**Register rows**: MOB-010.

## Steps

1. On the web, open **Care mode phones** in the menu.
2. Find the phone. Each one shows who used it, when it was last seen, and how many entries were
   waiting to send when it last reported. Choose the phone, for example **Phone ending 4F2A**.
3. Under **Withdraw this phone**, choose why: **Lost or stolen**, **The member of staff has left**,
   **Care mode access withdrawn**, **Replaced by another phone** or **Another reason**.
4. If entries were waiting on the phone, tick the box to confirm you understand what happens to
   them (see below).
5. Choose **Withdraw this phone**. Thapsus Care asks you to sign in again with your passkey or a
   code first; then choose the reason again.
6. The page says **The phone is withdrawn. It is refused the next time it connects.** It then shows
   the phone as **Withdrawn**, with the date and the reason.

## What happens next

- The next time the phone connects, Thapsus Care refuses it and the phone removes the care records
  stored on it.
- Entries on it that were not sent stay on it, locked, until their owner or a manager signs in on
  that phone to send them or delete them with a reason. See
  [How to send or delete another person's unsent entries on a phone](send-or-delete-another-persons-unsent-entries-on-a-phone.md).
- If entries were waiting, the task to deal with them is due now and marked urgent. Ask the member
  of staff what they recorded, so care that never reached Thapsus Care can be recorded again.
- Withdrawing a phone that is already withdrawn changes nothing, but makes that task due again.

## Good to know

- A phone is named by the last four characters of its id, because Thapsus Care does not know the
  phone's make or number. Use who used it and when it was last seen to tell phones apart.
- The number of entries waiting is what the phone last told Thapsus Care. Entries recorded after
  that cannot be known.
- To sign a person out of a phone rather than withdraw the phone, see
  [How to handle a lost or stolen phone](../all-staff/handle-a-lost-or-stolen-phone.md).
