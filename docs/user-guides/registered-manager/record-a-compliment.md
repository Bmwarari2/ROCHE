# How to record a compliment

Keep a record of praise from the people we support, their families, professionals and visitors.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager, the quality and compliance lead
and the owner. Other staff: pass compliments to a manager to record.

**Register row**: SAFE-011.

## Steps

1. Choose **Complaints** in the menu, then **Compliments**.
2. Under **Record a compliment**, choose the **Home** if you work at more than one.
3. Enter **Who it is from** and choose **Who they are**, for example **Family member or friend**.
4. Enter **When it was received**.
5. Under **What they said**, write the compliment.
6. Choose **Record compliment**. The page says "Compliment recorded" and the compliment shows in
   the list below, newest first.

## Good to know

- Compliments never run a clock and need no response in Harbour.
- A compliment cannot be changed or removed on the web once recorded.
- Linking a compliment to the members of staff it praises has no web screen yet.
