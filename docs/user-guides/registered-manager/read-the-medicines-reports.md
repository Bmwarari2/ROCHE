# How to read the medicines reports

The medicines reports count what was recorded at a home over a period: competence overrides and
late records, when-required and psychotropic use, and medication errors by month.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners, for the homes they work at.

**Register rows**: MEDS-012, MEDS-013, MEDS-003.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines reports** in the menu. If Thapsus Care asks, choose the home.
2. Choose a report:
   - **Competence overrides and late records**: for each member of staff, doses **Given under a
     competence override**, **Given without current competence** and **Recorded after it was
     given**.
   - **PRN and psychotropic use**: for each person and medicine, **When-required doses**,
     **Psychotropic doses** and **Psychotropic and when required**.
   - **Medication error trend**: medication errors and near misses by month, in three tables:
     whether it reached the person, what went wrong, and the stage where it went wrong.
3. To change the period, fill in **From** and **To** and choose **Show the report**. The first two
   reports show the last 28 days, and the trend the last six calendar months, until you change it.
   You can bookmark the page with its period.

## Important

- **Every report counts records for a person to look into. Thapsus Care does not judge what a count
  means**, and draws no chart: each count is in its own table cell.
- Look into every dose given without current competence and every override. An override should be
  the exception, never routine (HZ-119).
- Every dose recorded after it was given raises a task to review the record.
- The PRN report shows its note above the table every time. Read it before drawing any conclusion.
- The trend counts only the kinds and stages that happened. The error categories follow the
  National Reporting and Learning System (NRLS), the NHS list of medication error types (HZ-134).
- A member of staff no longer listed is shown as "A member of staff no longer listed".
- A period longer than your organisation allows is refused, in Thapsus Care's words.
- For the medication review tracker, see
  [How to check the medication review tracker](check-the-medication-review-tracker.md).
- Approving a competence override is not on the web yet.
