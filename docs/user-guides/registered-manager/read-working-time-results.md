# How to read working time results

See whether a member of staff's approved hours go over the Working Time Regulations limits, as
warnings for planning the rota, not legal advice.

**Who can do this**: people who approve rotas and timesheets (Registered Manager, deputy manager,
owner).

**Register row**: SCHED-003.

## Steps

1. Go to **Timesheets** in the menu and choose **Working time for** and the person's name under one
   of their entries.
2. Read **Advice, not a decision** at the top.
3. Each check shows the dates it covered and when it was checked. For each finding, read:
   - the rule, for example **Average weekly hours**, **Daily rest** or **Young worker daily hours**;
   - whether it is a **Breach** or **Allowed only with an agreement**;
   - the time against the limit.
4. If there is a breach, talk with the member of staff and check your working time policy before
   you plan their next shifts.

## Important

- These results are warnings for planning, not legal advice. They never stop a shift or a
  timesheet.
- A check runs each time one of the person's timesheet entries is approved, using their approved
  hours, any opt-out on file and your organisation's setting.
- The limits are national figures, kept apart from your organisation's own settings.
- The rota check shows the same kind of warnings for planned shifts before you publish.
