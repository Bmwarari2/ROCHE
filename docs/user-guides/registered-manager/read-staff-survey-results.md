# How to read staff survey results

See what staff said in a closed survey, without anyone being picked out.

**Who can do this**: people who run staff wellbeing (Registered Manager, deputy manager, owner).

**Register row**: SCHED-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Wellbeing** in the menu and choose **Staff surveys**.
2. Choose a survey shown as **Closed**.
3. Under **Results**, read each question:
   - how many people answered it;
   - for a 1 to 5 question, a table of how many people chose each number;
   - for a written question, the answers, in alphabetical order.

## Important

- Results show only once the survey has closed and enough people answered. The smallest number is
  set by Thapsus Care for every organisation; yours may raise it but never lower it.
- A question is shown only when enough people answered it. If any number on a 1 to 5 question was
  chosen by too few people, every count for that question is hidden.
- There is no breakdown, filter or export, so no one can be picked out. A home that wants its own
  results runs its own survey.
- Written answers are shown as staff wrote them. Treat what you read with care.
