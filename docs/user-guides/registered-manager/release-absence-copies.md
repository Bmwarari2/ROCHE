# How to release absence copies from the HR platform

Absences from the HR platform keep people off the rota. When many of them drop out of the HR
platform at once, or after the link is disconnected, Harbour keeps them until a person checks and
releases them, so nobody is rostered while still off.

**Who can do this**: people who manage the link (owner, Registered Manager, HR lead).

**Register rows**: INTG-014, SCHED-002.

## Steps

1. Open **HR platform link** in the menu and go to **Absence copies to release**. A task also
   tells you when absences are held.
2. While the link is connected, choose **Check for absence copies to release**. You confirm it is
   you first. Harbour reads the HR platform's list again and shows, with each person's name, what
   it no longer lists. Once disconnected, the copies still in force are shown.
3. Check each absence in the HR platform: has it ended or been cancelled?
4. Under **Why are you releasing them?**, choose the reason, and tick the box to say you have
   checked them.
5. Choose **Release**. You confirm it is you first.

## Important

- Harbour releases exactly the copies you checked. If the list has changed since, nothing is
  released and you are asked to check again.
- While the link is connected, absences that are already over are released without a person,
  because they cannot change the rota. Once disconnected, they are listed with the rest.
- Every release is recorded with who released what and why.
