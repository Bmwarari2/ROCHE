# How to check the medication review tracker

The medication review tracker lists everyone living at a home with medicines, overdue first, so no
one's medication review is missed.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners, for the homes they work at.

**Register row**: MEDS-013.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines reports** in the menu. If Thapsus Care asks, choose the home.
2. Choose **Medication review tracker**.
3. Read the line at the top, for example "1 review overdue and 2 due soon".
4. For each person, read the status in words and **Next due**, **Last done**, **Booked for** and
   **Due by the interval**.
5. Choose **Medication reviews for** a person to book a review or record one done (see
   [How to book and record a medication review](../nurse/book-and-record-a-medication-review.md)).

## What the words mean

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Review overdue | The next review date has passed. |
| Review due soon | The next review is close. |
| Review booked | A review is booked in time. |
| Review due by the interval, not yet booked | The interval since the last review says one is due. Book it. |
| No review booked or done | Nothing is recorded. Book one. |
| The booked review is later than the review interval allows. | Bring the booking forward, or record why with the clinician. |

## Important

- The interval comes from your organisation's settings. Thapsus Care counts dates and judges
  nothing about the medicines (HZ-134).
- Thapsus Care never suggests a change to a medicine. Changes come from the clinician and go on the
  medicines record.
