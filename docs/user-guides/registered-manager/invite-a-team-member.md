# How to invite a team member

**Who can do this**: the owner, Registered Manager and deputy manager. You must confirm it is you
with your passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: PLAT-004.

## Steps

1. Go to **Team** in the menu.
2. Under the invite card, enter their **Full name** and **Work email address**.
3. Choose their **Role**. Only roles you may give are listed. One role per invitation.
4. Choose **Where the role applies**: one home, or across the organisation.
5. Choose **Send invitation**. Confirm it is you if asked, then send it again.
6. The member's page shows "Invitation sent". The link works for 72 hours.

## Resend or withdraw

1. Open the member from **Team**.
2. Choose **Send a new invitation** (any older link stops working), or **Withdraw invitation**.
