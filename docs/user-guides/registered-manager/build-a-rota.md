# How to build a rota

Start a rota for a home, add its shifts and put people on them, before you check and publish it.

**Who can do this**: people who build rotas (Registered Manager, deputy manager, care coordinator,
owner). Everyone else on the rota can read it.

**Register rows**: SCHED-001, SCHED-002.

## Steps

1. Go to **Rota** in the menu and choose the home. Its rotas are listed under **Rotas**, newest
   first, each with where it stands: Draft, Published or Changed since published.
2. To start a new one, go to **Start a rota**, enter the **First day** and **Last day** and choose
   **Start rota**. A rota covers up to six weeks, and rotas at a home never overlap.
3. Under **Add shifts**, either choose a **Day** and a **Shift** and then **Add shift**, or choose a
   **Pattern** and then **Add pattern** to add its shifts on every day of the rota. If no shift
   template exists yet, [set up shift templates first](set-up-shift-templates-and-patterns.md).
4. Under **Shifts by day**, each shift shows Open or Assigned. For an open shift, choose a member
   of staff from the list and choose **Check they can work it**.
5. If Thapsus Care says they can work the shift, choose **Put on shift**. If it says they cannot
   work it yet, it lists why. Choose someone else, or, if you can, [override the rostering
   rule](override-the-rostering-rule.md).
6. To take someone off, choose **Take off shift** and give a **Reason**; the shift is open again.
   To remove a shift, choose **Cancel shift** and give a **Reason**.
7. When the shifts are filled, choose **Check and publish**. See [how to check and publish a
   rota](check-and-publish-a-rota.md).

## Good to know

- The check each time is the rostering rule: whether the person works for you that day, their DBS,
  right to work, professional registration and the competences the shift needs. Thapsus Care
  refuses someone already on an overlapping shift or away on approved leave or sickness.
- Open shifts give no care hours in the rota check.
- After a rota is published, any change is marked **Changed after the rota was published.** on the
  shift, and the rota says to check it and publish again so staff see the change.
- If staff data from the HR platform may be out of date, the page says so. Check the HR platform
  before you rely on who is available.
- Every shift added, cancelled, assigned or taken off is recorded, with who did it.
- Swapping shifts between staff, offering open shifts to staff and booking agency workers are not
  on the web yet.
