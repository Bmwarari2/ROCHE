# How to override the rostering rule

Put someone on a shift that the rostering rule blocks, when you have checked it is safe and there
is no one else.

**Who can do this**: people who approve rotas (Registered Manager, deputy manager, owner). A care
coordinator can build rotas but cannot override.

**Register row**: SCHED-002.

## Steps

1. On the rota, choose the member of staff for the open shift and choose **Check they can work
   it**. Thapsus Care says they cannot work the shift yet and lists each problem.
2. Read each problem and check it yourself, for example that a lapsed competence has been
   observed again on paper or that a DBS check is in hand with supervision in place.
3. Under **Override the rostering rule**, write **Why it is safe**: what you checked and what is in
   place, in at least 10 characters.
4. Tick the box to say you have checked each problem listed and it is safe.
5. Choose **Override and put on shift**. The shift shows **Assigned with an override**.

## Important

- Some problems can never be overridden: a missing right to work, a member of staff who is not
  active, no DBS or no professional registration. Choose someone else.
- You cannot override for your own shift.
- If the problems change before you send the override, it is refused and you check again.
- Your name, your reason and each problem the override covers are recorded with the shift. The rota
  check shows the override, and publishing asks you to confirm you have read it.
- The rule is checked again when the person clocks in. If something new blocks them by then, the
  clock-in is refused and the person in charge gets an urgent task that the shift may be short.
