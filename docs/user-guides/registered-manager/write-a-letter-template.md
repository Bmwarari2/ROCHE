# How to write and approve a letter template

Letters are made only from a template that someone has approved. The person who wrote or edited a
draft can never approve it.

**Who can do this**: write: the owner, Registered Manager, deputy, administrator and quality lead.
Approve: the owner, Registered Manager, deputy and quality lead.

**Register row**: COMMS-007.

## Write a draft

1. Go to **Letters** in the menu. The page is called **Letter and email templates**.
2. Choose **New template**.
3. Choose **What the template is for** and **How it is sent** (letter or email; this cannot be
   changed later).
4. Enter the **Template name** and the **Text**. Use the merge fields listed under
   **Merge fields you can use**, for example `{{recipient.full_name}}`.
5. Choose **Save draft**.
6. Fix anything under **Problems to fix before it can be approved**, then choose **Save changes**.
7. Ask someone else who approves templates to approve it.

## Approve a draft (a different person)

1. Open the template and read the draft.
2. Choose **Approve this draft**. It becomes the version in use.

## Change a template in use

1. Choose **Start a new draft**. The version in use stays in use until the new draft is approved.
2. To stop using a template, choose **Retire this version** and say why.

## Good to know

- Email templates can be written, but sending email is not available yet. Send letters.
- Duty of candour letters cannot be made from the record on the web yet.
