# How to answer the notification questions on an incident

Harbour checks which statutory notifications an incident needs. When it cannot tell from what was
recorded, it asks questions. Until they are answered, a notification may be missed.

**Who can do this**: people who manage incidents (Registered Manager, deputy, nurse or clinical
lead, owner). The person who raised a fall can answer the two falls questions.

**Register rows**: SAFE-002, SAFE-009.

## Steps

1. Open the incident. The top of the page says how many questions are left, for example
   "2 questions about statutory notifications to answer".
2. Go to **Answer the questions about statutory notifications**.
3. Answer what you can. A question left blank stays open.
4. If you do not know, choose **Not sure yet**. Harbour then drafts the notification as
   provisional, so its clock shows. If in doubt, notify.
5. For a number (days or hours), type a whole number or tick **Not sure yet**, not both.
6. Choose **Save answers**. Harbour checks again which notifications are needed.

## Good to know

- **Harbour supports your judgement. It never replaces it.** The Registered Manager decides what
  the law needs. If something feels notifiable, notify.
- Answers can be asked for again when facts change, for example a worker who is still off work
  after several days (RIDDOR).
- Harbour checks the law in England only for now. Outside England it says "Notifications are not
  checked outside England": check what your nation's regulator needs yourself.
