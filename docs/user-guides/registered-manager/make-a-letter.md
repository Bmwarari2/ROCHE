# How to make a letter from the record

**Who can do this**: the owner, Registered Manager, deputy and administrator. Incident follow-up
letters also need permission to manage incidents.

**Register row**: COMMS-007.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Letters**.
2. Under **Make a letter**, choose the **Template**. Only approved letter templates are listed:
   email templates are not offered until Harbour can send email.
3. For an incident follow-up, choose the **Incident it is about**.
4. Fill in any details the template asks for, such as a fee, in pounds and pence.
5. Choose **Preview the letter**.
6. Under **Check before sending**, check the photo, who it is about, who it is to, every word,
   and the personal details it uses, listed by name (for example "Person's date of birth").
7. Tick **I have checked who this letter is about, who it is to and what it says**.
8. Choose **Make the letter**. It is saved with the person's documents, ready to print.

## Good to know

- Letters are addressed to the person for now. Letters to a contact are not available yet.
- If the template changed after your preview, Harbour shows the new version. Check it and confirm
  again.
