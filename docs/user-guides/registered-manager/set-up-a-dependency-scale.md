# How to set up a dependency scale

Write your organisation's own scale for the care hours people need, so the rota check can compare
each shift period with it.

**Who can do this**: people who manage the dependency scale (Registered Manager, deputy manager,
owner). A second person approves each version. Anyone who can read the rota can see the scales.

**Register rows**: CARE-004, SCHED-001.

## Steps

1. Go to **Dependency scales** in the menu.
2. Under **Make a scale**, enter a **Name**, choose **Where it applies** (a home, or **The whole
   organisation** for homes with none of their own) and choose **Make scale**. It starts as a draft.
3. Open the scale and, in the draft, fill in:
   - **Shift periods**: each period's name and when it starts, in the order they start. Each ends
     when the next one starts;
   - **Categories**: each category's name, description and the care minutes one person in it
     needs in each period;
   - **Skill mix**: the fewest staff with each skill you want on a period, for example Registered
     nurse;
   - **Review every (days)**, or leave it blank for no set review.
4. Choose **Save draft**. Any problems to fix before it can be approved are listed.
5. Ask someone else who manages the scale to open it and choose **Approve draft**, then **Approve
   and use it**.
6. To change a scale later, choose **Start a draft** under **Change the scale**. The version in use
   stays in use until the new draft is approved.

## Important

- Every figure in the scale is yours. Thapsus Care does not score people or say whether staffing is
  safe: it adds up the minutes your scale gives.
- Whoever wrote or changed a draft cannot approve it.
- Once approved, a version is in use at once: required care hours and every rota check use it.
- **Discard draft** keeps the draft as discarded with your reason. **Archive scale** stops it
  applying anywhere, with your reason, and is final: the home shows no scale until another is
  approved.
- Each person's category is recorded on their record. See [how to record a dependency
  category](../senior-carer/record-a-dependency-category.md).
