# How to record an admission and a room

Record the day a person moves in, their placement and their bed, then any move to another room.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers and the owner. Nurses and clinical
leads can also tick the welcome checklist.

**Register row**: PEOP-007.

## Record the admission

1. Open the person's **Admission and room** tab. It says "The decision is to admit and every
   condition is met." If it does not, it says what is still needed: choose **Go to the
   pre-admission record**.
2. Choose the **Service** and the **Kind of placement**, and enter the **First day of the
   placement** (and **Trial ends** for a trial).
3. Choose the **Room and bed**. Only free beds today, in rooms in use, are listed.
4. Tick **Funded by the council** or **Funded by the NHS** where it applies.
5. Enter **When did the person arrive?**.
6. Choose **Record the admission**. The page says "Admission recorded. The room shown at the top
   of the record now comes from the bed recorded here."

## Move to another room

1. Under **Room and bed**, choose **Move to another room**.
2. Choose **Move to**, enter the **Day of the move** (not later than today) and the **Reason for
   the move**, for example "the person asked for a room nearer the garden".
3. Choose **Record the move**. The page says "Move recorded" and the move shows under **Move
   history**.

## Welcome checklist

Under **Welcome checklist**, each item says **Done**, **Not applicable**, **To do** or
**Optional**. Choose the item's "Done:" button, or its "Not applicable:" button and then **Mark
not applicable** with a reason. The page counts how many safety-critical items are still to do.

## Good to know

- If the page says "No bed is free in a room in use today.", ask a manager to check the rooms.
- A move is recorded on the day it happens, never before.
