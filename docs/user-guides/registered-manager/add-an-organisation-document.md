# How to add an organisation document or a new version

Upload a document about the organisation, such as a procedure, for staff to read and sign, and
replace it with a new version when it changes.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers, the owner, administrators or
receptionists, and the quality and compliance lead.

**Register row**: PLAT-009.

## Add a document

1. Go to **Documents** in the menu.
2. Under **Add a document**, enter a **Title**, for example "Medicines policy".
3. Under **File**, choose a PDF of up to 25 MB.
4. Choose **Upload document**. The page says "Document uploaded."

## Upload a new version

1. Open the document from **Organisation documents**.
2. Under **Upload a new version**, choose the **New version** file.
3. Choose **Upload new version**. The page says "New version uploaded. The earlier version is
   kept."

## Good to know

- The current version and its signatures are kept. The new version starts with no signatures, so
  staff sign it again (see
  [How to read and sign an organisation document](../all-staff/read-and-sign-an-organisation-document.md)).
- Each file is checked for viruses before it is kept. Until then it cannot be opened.
- **Versions** lists every version, with the one you are looking at marked **(shown)**.
- Letter and email templates are written and approved with letters: choose **Open letters and
  templates** (see [How to write and approve a letter template](write-a-letter-template.md)).
