# How to reconcile an outage

Check every paper form from an outage has been entered once, at the right time.

**Who can do this**: people who manage outages (Registered Manager, deputy, nurse, senior carer,
owner), but **not** anyone who entered or sent paper records from that outage. Ask another
manager if you did.

**Register row**: OPS-028.

## Steps

1. Go to **Downtime** and open the outage. It shows **Closed: paper records to enter**.
2. Read the counts under **Reconcile the paper records**: how many records were entered, from how
   many forms, and how many have no form reference, a time outside the outage, or were recorded
   in Harbour during the outage.
3. Under **Check these entries**, look in each person's record for every item listed, for example
   "Possibly the same care recorded twice". Tick each one once you have checked it.
4. Under **What was checked**, say how many paper forms there were and that each one is entered.
   Do not write anyone's name.
5. Choose **Confirm reconciled**. The outage is now final.

## Good to know

- Possible duplicates are entries for the same person within 10 minutes of each other.
- After reconciling, paper records can no longer be entered against the outage. A late form is
  kept for the nurse in charge to review.
