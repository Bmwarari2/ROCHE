# How to compose and release an evidence pack

When an inspector or commissioner asks for evidence, put exactly the records they need into a
watermarked pack, have a second person check and release it, and send them the link.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers, the owner and the quality and
compliance lead. The person who releases a pack must not have composed, edited or redacted it.
Releasing needs you to confirm it is you with your passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: QUAL-003.

## Compose the pack

1. Go to **Evidence packs** in the menu and choose **Compose a pack**.
2. Under **About this pack**, enter **Their full name**, **Their organisation** (for example CQC,
   the Care Quality Commission, or the local authority) and **Their work email address**. The
   link opens only for this address.
3. Write **What the pack is for**. It is printed on every page.
4. Enter **Records from** and **Records to**, and tick the **Locations**.
5. Tick each of the **People the pack is about**. Anyone else named in a record is shown by role
   or withheld.
6. Choose **Staff names**: **Staff shown by role only**, unless the inspector needs names, for
   example to check staffing.
7. Under **Which areas of the inspection**, choose **Every area** or **Only the areas I choose**.
8. Choose **Compose the pack**. The page says "Pack composed. Add the records it should hold."

## Add records

1. Under **Add records**, choose a person.
2. Tick the **Records to add**, choose **The areas these records are evidence for**, and choose
   **Add to the pack**.
3. Records outside the pack are left out, with the reason, under the records left out. They are
   never sent.
4. Free text is withheld until someone redacts it. To include it, choose **Redact by hand**, type
   the **Redacted copy** with every name, address, date of birth and long number taken out, and
   choose **Save the redacted copy**. Otherwise leave it withheld. Whoever redacts becomes someone
   who worked on the pack, so another person must release it.

## Release it

1. Ask someone else who can release packs and did not work on this one to open it and read
   **What will be shared** and every record and redaction.
2. They choose **How long the link works**: the shortest time the inspector needs.
3. They tick **I have read every record and redaction in this pack** and choose **Release and make
   the link**.
4. The link is shown once. Choose **Copy the link**, send it to the recipient's email address,
   then choose **I have sent the link**.

## Good to know

- When the recipient opens the link, a one-time code goes to their email separately (see
  [How to open an evidence pack](../inspector/open-an-evidence-pack.md)).
- **Links** shows when each link was issued, last opened, and how many codes did not match. If a
  link is locked, choose **Send a new link**: the current link stops working at once.
- To stop sharing, choose **Withdraw the pack**, give a **Reason** and tick **I understand this
  cannot be undone**. The link stops working and the content is deleted; the record of what was
  shared is kept. A draft can be discarded the same way with **Discard the draft**.
- A restricted record is never shared.
