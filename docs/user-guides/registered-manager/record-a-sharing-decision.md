# How to record a sharing decision

Decide what a family member, a professional or a share link may see of a person's record, on
what basis and until when.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers and the owner. Thapsus Care asks you
to confirm it is you with your passkey or authenticator app before you make a share link.

**Register row**: PEOP-013.

## Before you start

- **Nothing in a person's record is shown to anyone outside the team unless a decision here is in
  force** for that recipient and that section.
- Add family members and representatives on the **Personal details** tab first (see
  [How to change a person's details and contacts](change-a-persons-details-and-contacts.md)). For
  a decision resting on a lack of capacity, record the capacity assessment on the **Consent,
  capacity and DoLS** tab first.

## Record a decision

1. Open the person's record and choose **Sharing**.
2. Under **Record a sharing decision**, choose **Who will see it**: **A family member or
   representative**, **A professional or member of staff**, or **Whoever holds a share link (About
   me only)**. Then choose the person.
3. Under **What they will see**, tick each section, for example **Daily updates** or **Care plan**.
   The care plan is shared whole.
4. Choose **What the decision rests on**:
   - **Consent**: they agree, or an attorney or deputy agrees for them within their authority.
     Say **Who gave consent**.
   - **Legal authority**: the family member holds a power of attorney, deputyship or
     appointeeship that covers these sections.
   - **Best-interests decision**: a recorded best-interests decision, when they lack capacity for
     this.
   - **Direct care**: for professionals caring for them, and clinical sections only.
5. Answer **Their capacity to decide about sharing**, and choose the capacity assessment where
   one is needed.
6. Enter **In force until the end of**: every decision ends.
7. Choose **Record decision**. The page says "Sharing decision recorded".

## Check what someone can see

Under **What one recipient can see now**, choose the **Recipient** and **Check**. Each section
says **Open** or **Closed**, and why.

## Suspend, lift or withdraw

On a decision, choose **Suspend sharing**, **Lift the suspension** or **Withdraw decision**, give
the reason, and confirm.

- Suspending closes the sections at once. What happens while it is suspended stays closed, even
  after the suspension is lifted.
- Withdrawing closes the sections for good, and its share links stop working. This cannot be
  undone: record a new decision to share again.

## Make a share link to About me

1. Record a decision for **Whoever holds a share link (About me only)** first.
2. On the **About me** tab, under **Share links**, choose **Make a share link**.
3. Choose **The sharing decision it rests on** and the day **The link stops working at the end
   of**. It cannot last longer than its decision.
4. Choose **Make link**. Copy the **Link code** now and give it only to the person it is for.
   Thapsus Care shows it this once.
5. To stop it, choose **Revoke** on the link, say **Why are you revoking it?** and choose **Revoke
   link**. It stops working at once.

## Good to know

- The page where the recipient opens About me with the code is not built yet: it comes with the
  family portal.
- A decision closes by itself when the person leaves, an authority it rests on ends, or their
  capacity for the decision changes. It then says **Not in force**.
