# How to handle a complaint

Record a complaint as it arrives, acknowledge it, look into it, send the response and close it.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager, the quality and compliance lead
and the owner, at the homes where they manage complaints. Other staff cannot see complaints: if
someone complains to you, write down what they said and pass it to a manager the same day.

**Register row**: SAFE-011.

## Record the complaint

1. Choose **Complaints** in the menu, then **Record a complaint**.
2. If you work at more than one home, choose the **Home**.
3. Enter the **Name of the person complaining** and choose **Who they are**, for example
   **Family member or friend**.
4. Add **How to contact them** if they gave it. It is optional.
5. Choose **How it arrived**, for example **Phone** or **Letter**.
6. Enter **When it was received**. The acknowledgement and response targets start from this time.
7. Enter **When they first knew of the matter**. The Ombudsman's time limit runs from this date.
   It cannot be after the complaint was received.
8. Under **What the complaint is about**, write what they told you.
9. Choose **Record complaint**. The complaint opens and says "Complaint recorded".

## Take it through to the end

1. Open the complaint from **Complaints**. Each one is titled **Complaint received** and the date
   and time, never by the complainant's name, and shows **Acknowledge by** and **Respond by**
   dates while they are still ahead.
2. Read **Targets and time limits**.
3. Under **Acknowledge the complaint**, choose **Acknowledge** once you have told them you have it.
4. Under **Start investigating**, choose **Start investigating**.
5. Before you respond, upload the letter or email you sent under **Documents**.
6. Under **Send the response**, choose the **Outcome** (**Upheld**, **Partly upheld** or **Not
   upheld**) and write **What was done**.
7. Tick **The response tells them they can go to the Local Government and Social Care
   Ombudsman**. Harbour will not record a response without it.
8. Under **Copy of the response**, choose the document you uploaded. Choose **Record the
   response**.
9. If they appeal, under **Record an appeal**, enter **When the appeal was received** and **Why
   they appealed**, then choose **Record the appeal**. Respond to the appeal the same way, under
   **Respond to the appeal**.
10. When it is finished, under **Close the complaint**, write **Why it is closed** and choose
    **Close the complaint**. If the person withdraws the complaint, close it the same way and say
    so.

## Good to know

- The acknowledgement and response targets are your organisation's, counted in working days. The
  regulation sets none for independent providers.
- Harbour never decides the outcome and never sends anything to the complainant or the Ombudsman.
  You send the response yourself and record it here.
- Closing is final: the complaint cannot be reopened.
- A complaint about how personal data was handled is a data protection complaint (see
  [How to handle a data protection complaint](../data-protection-lead/handle-a-data-protection-complaint.md)).
- Compliments are recorded separately (see [How to record a compliment](record-a-compliment.md)).
- The rules: [safety specification, Phase 2 rule 2](../../specs/safety.md#phase-2-rules-plan-slice-7-safe-006-safe-011-safe-012-safe-014-safe-015-safe-016-meds-012)
  and [web screens](../../specs/safety.md#web-screens-phase-2-slice-62).
