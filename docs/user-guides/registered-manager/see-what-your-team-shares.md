# How to see what your team shares

Read the wellbeing check-ins staff choose to share with you, and the thanks between colleagues at
a home.

**Who can do this**: people who run staff wellbeing (Registered Manager, deputy manager, owner).

**Register row**: SCHED-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Wellbeing** in the menu and choose **What your team shares**.
2. Choose a home.
3. Under **Shared check-ins**, read each one: who, when and how they said they were, with any note.
4. Under **Thanks between colleagues**, read who thanked whom, and when.

## Important

- You see a check-in only while the member shares it. If they stop sharing, it disappears from
  this page.
- A check-in is never a referral, a score or a judgement. If you are worried about someone, talk
  with them.
- Thanks are never part of a performance record.
- Each time you open this page, it is recorded, with who looked.
