# How to pay staff through the HR platform

Choose whether pay goes to payroll through Harbour's payroll export or through your HR platform,
and map the pay codes. When the HR platform pays, Harbour sends hours and other units by pay code,
never money, and the HR platform applies the rates.

**Who can do this**: people who manage the link change the route; pay codes are set by someone who
both manages the link and approves pay.

**Register rows**: INTG-014, SCHED-005, SCHED-008.

## Steps

1. Open **HR platform link** in the menu and go to **Pay**. **Route to payroll now** and any
   **Change waiting** are shown.
2. Under **Pay codes**, enter the HR platform's pay code for each kind of unit (hours, hours at
   night, weekend, bank holiday or overtime, sleep-ins, on-call periods away and miles), then choose
   **Save pay codes**.
3. Under **Change the route to payroll**, choose the new route and **From the first day of the pay
   period**: a pay period later than any pay run and, to pay through the HR platform, the first day
   of one of its pay periods. Choose **Save route change**. You confirm it is you first.

## Important

- Each pay run goes to payroll one way only, fixed when it is drafted, so nobody is paid twice. A
  correction pays the same way as the run it corrects.
- A pay run paid through the HR platform uses one of its pay periods exactly and checks the minimum
  wage against the HR platform's lowest rate in that period. A person with no rate there stops the
  run; it cannot be overridden.
- A run cannot be locked until every kind of unit has a pay code and everyone in it is matched. The
  pay codes in force when it is locked are the ones sent.
- If the link is off when you draft a run for a period set to the HR platform, Harbour asks you to
  wait or confirm the payroll export instead.
- Pay through the HR platform is not available until the HR platform offers it.
