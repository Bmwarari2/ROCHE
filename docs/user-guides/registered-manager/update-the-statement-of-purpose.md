# How to update the statement of purpose

Keep your statement of purpose (what your service does, for whom and where, as the Care Quality
Commission (CQC) holds it) up to date. Every change is a new version, approved by someone who
did not write it, and sent to CQC on time.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers, the owner and the quality and
compliance lead write drafts. Only the Registered Manager and the owner approve a version, and
never one they wrote or edited. They also record that it was sent to CQC, after confirming it is
them with their passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: QUAL-009.

## The first time: enter the statement CQC holds

1. Go to **Statement of purpose** in the menu.
2. Under **The statement CQC already holds**, copy in the version you last sent CQC.
3. Choose **Save the statement**. Once approved it needs no notification, because CQC already holds
   it.

## Change the statement

1. Under **Change the statement**, choose **Start a new draft**. The version in force stays until
   the draft is approved.
2. Under **Edit the draft**, fill in each part: **1. Aims and objectives**, **2. Services and the
   needs they meet**, **3. Names and contact details**, **4. Legal status** and **5. Locations**.
3. Choose **Save the draft**, as often as you like. Thapsus Care lists anything the law asks for
   that is missing, and says **Everything the law asks for is there** when it is complete.
4. Read **What changed**, and **You may also need to apply to CQC** if it shows. Thapsus Care
   prompts; it does not decide. Check with CQC what the change needs.
5. Ask the Registered Manager or the owner, whoever did not write it, to approve it.

## Approve it

1. Open the draft and read it.
2. Under **Approve this version**, choose **Approve this version**. It becomes the statement in
   force, and the period for the details to reach CQC starts.

## Tell CQC

1. Send the details through the CQC provider portal or by email.
2. The card for the version, for example "Version 2: tell CQC", says when it is due. Under
   "Record that version 2 was sent to CQC", choose **When it was sent**: **Just now** or
   **Earlier** with the date and time.
3. Enter the **CQC reference, or how it was sent**, for example the portal reference.
4. Choose **Record it as sent**. Thapsus Care never sends it to CQC itself.

## Good to know

- To throw away a draft, choose **Discard the draft** and give a **Reason**. The version in force
  stays as it is.
- **Every version** lists each version, with when it was approved.
