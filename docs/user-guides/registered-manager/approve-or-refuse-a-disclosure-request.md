# How to approve or refuse a disclosure request

Check a disclosure request someone else has prepared, and approve the release or refuse it.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers and the owner, for people at the
homes where they work, but never on a request they logged themselves. Nurses and clinical leads can
prepare a request but cannot approve it.

**Register row**: SAFE-016.

## Steps

1. Choose **Disclosure requests** in the menu. Open a request marked **Ready for approval**.
2. Read **The request**: who it is about, the **Legal basis**, who asked and **What they need it
   for**.
3. Under **Records**, read the count and open records to check the decisions: what is disclosed,
   what has parts removed and what is withheld, and why.
4. Under **Approve or refuse**, choose **Approve the release** or **Refuse it**. To refuse, write
   the **Reason**.
5. Choose **Save decision**. The page says "Decision saved". The state changes to **Approved** or
   **Refused**.
6. If approved, tell the person who prepared it, so they can release the pack (see
   [How to release and hand over a disclosure pack](../nurse/release-and-hand-over-a-disclosure-pack.md)).

## Good to know

- If you logged the request, the page says "You logged this request, so someone else who holds
  the approval permission must decide it." Ask another approver.
- Refusing is final: nothing is released. Tell the requester yourself.
- Thapsus Care never decides whether a disclosure is lawful. It shows the legal basis recorded when
  the request was logged. If you are unsure, ask the data protection lead before you approve.
- Your decision is recorded, with who did it.
- The rules: [safety specification, disclosure requests](../../specs/safety.md#12-disclosure-requests-slice-41-safe-016).
