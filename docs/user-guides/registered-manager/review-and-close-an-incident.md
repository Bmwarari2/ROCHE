# How to review and close an incident

**Who can do this**: people who manage incidents: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager, the
nurse or clinical lead, and the owner. Senior carers and care workers cannot.

**Register rows**: SAFE-001, SAFE-002.

## Steps

1. Go to **Incidents** and open the incident. Open incidents show **Open**, those being looked at
   show **Under review**.
2. Read **What happened**, **Who was told** and **Statutory notifications**.
3. Under **Review and close**, choose **Start the review**. Others can now see a manager is on it.
4. Answer any questions about statutory notifications (see
   [How to answer the notification questions](answer-notification-questions.md)).
5. Deal with every notification: submit it or record that it is not required (see
   [How to prepare a statutory notification](prepare-a-statutory-notification.md)).
6. If you cannot finish yet, for example while you wait for information, choose
   **Send it back to open** and say what is still needed.
7. When the review is finished, write **Why can it close?**: what was done and what was learned.
8. Choose **Close the incident**. The page says "Incident closed".

## When it will not close

- **You raised it.** The person who raised an incident can never close it. The page says so. Ask
  another manager.
- **Before it can close** lists what is left: a notification still a draft or ready, or questions
  still to answer. Each item links to it.
- **The duty of candour record is still open.** The candour record has no web screen yet. Ask
  the person who manages Harbour for your organisation to complete it through the system, then
  close the incident.

## Good to know

- A closed incident cannot be changed.
- Someone may have changed it since you opened the page. If Harbour says so, reload the page and
  try again if it is still needed.
- Investigations, root cause and lessons learned have no web screen yet (register row SAFE-010).
