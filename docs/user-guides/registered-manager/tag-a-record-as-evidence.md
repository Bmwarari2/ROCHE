# How to tag a record as evidence

Tagging adds a record to your evidence library against one or more quality statements, so you can
show it to an inspector. You can also remove a tag with a reason.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager, the owner and the quality and
compliance lead. Care workers, senior carers, nurses and the group executive cannot.

**Register row**: QUAL-002.

## Steps

1. Open the record you want to tag. Today you can tag from:
   - an incident: open it from **Incidents** and choose **Tag as evidence** at the top;
   - a completed audit: open it from **Quality audits** and choose **Tag as evidence** at the top.
2. The page **Tag as evidence** opens. Under **Tags on this record** you see any tags already on
   it, each marked **Tagged** or **Tag removed**.
3. Under **Add a tag**, in **Choose the quality statements**, tick the statements this record is
   evidence for. They are grouped by key question. Choose up to 20.
4. Choose **Tag as evidence**. The page says "Tagged as evidence."
5. To go back, choose **Back to the record**.

## Remove a tag

1. On **Tag as evidence**, find the tag under **Tags on this record**.
2. Under **Remove the tag**, write **Why remove it**, for example "tagged against the wrong
   statement".
3. Choose **Remove tag**. The page says "Tag removed. It stays in the history."

## Good to know

- Only the record's type and reference are stored, never its content.
- You tag against the CQC framework version in force. If none is in force, the page says "No
  framework version is in force, so records cannot be tagged."
- You can only tag a record you are allowed to see. A restricted incident has no **Tag as
  evidence** button.
- A removed tag is never deleted. It shows when and why it was removed, and stops counting as
  evidence.
- Other record types have no **Tag as evidence** button yet.
- The rule: [quality spec section 17, rule 6](../../specs/quality.md#17-phase-2-domain-rules-slice-8-qual-001-002-004-005-006-008-012).
