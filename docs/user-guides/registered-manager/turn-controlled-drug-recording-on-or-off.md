# How to turn controlled drug recording on or off

One controlled drugs register is the record at a home at a time: the paper register, or the
register in Thapsus Care. This page changes which.

**Who can do this**: the Owner and the Registered Manager of the home. You need to confirm it is
you with your second sign-in step.

**Register rows**: MEDS-005, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines reports** for the home and choose **Controlled drug recording**.
2. Read which register is the record now.

### To turn recording on

Do this only once the phones at the home have the version of the app that records controlled
drugs.

1. With a witness, count every controlled drug against the paper register, and close each page
   with the date, time and both signatures.
2. Tick each statement: the paper register's pages are counted, witnessed and closed now; each
   balance will be entered in Thapsus Care's register, witnessed, before the next dose; the phones
   have the right version.
3. Choose **Turn on controlled drug recording**.
4. Straight away, for each controlled drug, open its register page and record the balance as
   received, with a witness, before anyone gives the next dose.

### To turn recording off

1. Print each controlled drug's register page from Thapsus Care.
2. Restart the paper register with each balance, witnessed.
3. Fill in **Why recording is being turned off** and tick that the paper register is restarted.
4. Choose **Turn off and go back to the paper register**.

## Important

- **Never both at once.** Until recording is on, every controlled drug record at the home stays
  in the paper register: receipts, doses, counts and returns (HZ-115, HZ-008).
- **Phones offline when it changes**: a phone keeps what it last read until it connects again. A
  dose recorded on a phone after recording was turned off is refused when it arrives, and the
  nurse in charge gets an urgent review: enter it in the paper register.
- The change, who made it and why are kept in the audit trail.
- The register printout says it is not the live record and which register is.
