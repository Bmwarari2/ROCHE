# How to record an enquiry and start the pre-admission assessment

Record that someone has asked about living at, or using, your home, then start their
pre-admission assessment, or close the enquiry if they are not being admitted.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, deputy managers and the owner.

**Register row**: PEOP-005.

## Record the enquiry

1. Go to **Residents** in the menu (your home may call it **Clients** or **People we support**),
   choose **Enquiries and pre-admission**, then **Record an enquiry**.
2. **Search first.** If the person has lived here or been assessed before, choose **Search for
   the person**, open their record and record a re-referral there, so they keep one record.
3. Under **The enquiry**, choose the **Home**, and enter the **Given name**, **Family name**,
   **Preferred name (optional)** and **Date of birth**.
4. Enter **When did the enquiry come in?**.
5. Choose **Record the enquiry**. The person's **Pre-admission** tab opens and says "Enquiry
   recorded. Start the pre-admission assessment when you are ready."

## Start the assessment

1. On the **Pre-admission** tab, under **Enquiry**, choose the **Service sought** and the **Kind of
   placement sought**: **Permanent**, **Respite**, **Trial** or **Care package without a bed**.
2. Choose **Start the pre-admission assessment**. The page says "Pre-admission assessment
   started." Nurses and managers then fill in the sections (see
   [How to fill in a pre-admission assessment](../nurse/fill-in-a-pre-admission-assessment.md)).

## Close or reopen an enquiry

- To close it, choose **Close as not admitted**, write **Why the person is not being admitted**
  (at least 3 characters) and choose **Close as not admitted**. The record is kept, but nothing
  more can be added to it.
- If they are referred again later, open the closed record and choose **Record a re-referral**,
  write **Why the person has been referred again** and choose **Record the re-referral**. The
  enquiry is open again on the same record.

## Good to know

- **Enquiries and pre-admission** lists **Enquiries and assessments in progress** and, under
  **Not admitted**, those closed without an admission.
- If the page says "No services are set up at this home yet", ask an administrator to add them.
