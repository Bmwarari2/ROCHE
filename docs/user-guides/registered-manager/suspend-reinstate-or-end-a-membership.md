# How to suspend, reinstate or end a team member's membership

Stop someone using Thapsus Care for your organisation, for a while or for good, and erase their
sign-in once they have left.

**Who can do this**: the owner, the Registered Manager and deputy managers, for the people they
manage. You cannot change your own membership. Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is you with your
passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: PLAT-004.

## Suspend a membership

1. Go to **Team** in the menu and open the member.
2. Under **Membership**, read what their phone last reported, for example care entries not yet
   sent.
3. Choose **Suspend membership**. Thapsus Care asks whether to suspend them and says what happens:
   they can no longer use Thapsus Care for your organisation, Care mode on their phone stops, and
   they are signed out everywhere, unless they also work for another organisation. If they are on
   shift, arrange cover first.
4. Choose the **Reason**, for example **An investigation** or **A long absence**.
5. If care entries are waiting on their phone, tick to confirm you understand they have not been
   sent. They stay on the phone, protected, until a manager hands them over.
6. Choose **Suspend membership**, or **Keep it active**. The page says "Membership suspended".

## Reinstate a membership

1. Open the suspended member and choose **Reinstate membership**.
2. Choose the **Reason**, for example **They were suspended by mistake** or **They are back from an
   absence**.
3. Choose **Reinstate membership**. They can use Thapsus Care again with the roles they had, and
   sign in again if they were signed out.

## End a membership

1. Suspend the membership first. Hand over any care entries still held on a device, or record
   their deletion on the device: the page lists the **Care entries to hand over**.
2. Choose **End membership**, the **Reason** and **How you checked their devices**, for example
   **The device was returned and checked, and anything waiting was handed over**.
3. Choose **End membership**, or **Keep membership**. Their roles end; their name and job title stay
   on the records they made.

## Erase their sign-in

After the membership has ended, choose **Erase sign-in**, read what is deleted, and choose **Erase
sign-in** again, or **Keep the sign-in**. Their email address, password, passkeys, authenticator
and sessions are deleted, and this cannot be undone. Their sign-in is kept if they also work for
another organisation.

## Good to know

- For a member of staff leaving, the HR lead can also end their access at once (see
  [How to end a leaver's access now](../hr-lead/end-a-leavers-access-now.md)).
- Changing a member's role, and acting-up (time-limited extra permissions), have no screen yet.
  Ask whoever runs Thapsus Care for your organisation.
