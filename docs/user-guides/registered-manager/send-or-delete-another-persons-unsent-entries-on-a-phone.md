# How to send or delete another person's unsent entries on a phone

When Thapsus Care signs a colleague out (for example, their access changed) before their entries
reach it, the phone keeps those entries, encrypted (scrambled so only Thapsus Care can read them),
until they sign in again. If they cannot (for example, they have left, or the
phone was withdrawn), a manager can sign in on that phone to send the entries in their name, or
delete them with a reason.

**Who can do this**: Registered Managers, deputy managers, clinical leads or nurses, and owners.
The member of staff who wrote the entries can also delete their own.

**Register rows**: MOB-005, MOB-006, MOB-010.

## Send them in their name

1. Open the Thapsus Care app on the phone. The sign-in screen shows a card, for example
   **Sign in again to send 3 entries**, with whose they are, for example "Entries from Ada Example
   are waiting to be sent.", and when they must be sent by.
2. Choose **Send 3 entries for them**.
3. Under **Sign in to send unsent entries**, enter your own email address and password (and your
   authenticator code if asked), then choose **Sign in and send**.
4. The phone sends the entries in your colleague's name, with you recorded as having sent them. It
   then says, for example, **3 entries were sent to Thapsus Care. Nothing is left on this device.**
5. Your sign-in ends straight after. The phone goes back to the sign-in screen.

## Delete them

Delete them only when the care has been recorded another way, or the person in charge knows and
will make sure it is.

1. On the card, choose **Delete 3 unsent entries**.
2. Under **Why are these entries being deleted?**, choose the reason, for example **The member of
   staff who wrote them has left**.
3. Enter your email address and password and choose **Sign in and delete**.
4. The phone asks **Delete 3 unsent entries?** Choose **Delete for good**, then confirm it is you
   with the phone's face, fingerprint or passcode.
5. Your sign-in ends straight after. The deletion and its reason are recorded in Thapsus Care.

## Important

- Nothing on the card is in Thapsus Care. Until it is sent, the care those entries record is missing
  from the person's record (HZ-006).
- Send rather than delete wherever you can. Deleted entries cannot be recovered (HZ-040).
- Nothing is deleted automatically. When the time allowed has passed, the card says a manager must
  now resolve it, and Thapsus Care gives you a task.
- Deleting another member of staff's entries needs a connection, so Thapsus Care records the
  deletion first. If the phone says **Connect to the internet and try again.**, nothing has been
  deleted.
- If only part was sent, the phone says how many are left, protected. Tell the data protection lead
  or your Registered Manager, and send them again when the phone is online.

## Good to know

- If the card says **Entries from another member of staff are waiting to be sent. This device cannot
  tell whose they are, so only a manager can resolve them.**,
  the entries cannot be sent in anyone's name. A manager can only delete them, with a reason.
- On a phone a manager withdrew, the member of staff who wrote the entries can send them here too.
