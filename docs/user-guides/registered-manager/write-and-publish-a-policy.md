# How to write and publish a policy

Add a policy with its first draft, change it in later drafts, and publish a draft to put it in
force. Publishing asks the roles you choose to read and sign it.

These screens arrive with the next merge; until then they are on the `feat/quality-dashboard-pir`
branch.

**Who can do this**: the Registered Manager, the deputy manager, the owner and the quality and
compliance lead. Where a policy needs a second reviewer, the person who worked on the draft cannot
publish it. Care workers, senior carers and nurses cannot.

**Register row**: QUAL-008.

## Add a new policy

1. In the side bar, choose **Policies**, then **New policy**.
2. Under **Policy and first draft**, fill in:
   - **Title**;
   - **Short name**: lower-case letters, numbers and underscores, starting with a letter, for
     example safeguarding;
   - **Someone other than the author must publish it**: ticked already, and recommended for every
     policy;
   - **What changed**: shown in the change log, for example "First version";
   - **Policy text**, or choose a **Policy document** from your organisation's documents (the
     choice shows only when your organisation has documents);
   - **Who must read and sign it**: tick each role;
   - **Review date** (optional while drafting; needed to publish).
3. Choose **Add policy**. The page says "Policy added as a draft. Ask someone else to publish it if
   it needs a second reviewer."

The first version is always a major change: everyone asked will read and sign it.

## Start a new version

1. Open the policy from the **Policy library**.
2. Under **Draft**, in **Start a new draft**, choose the **Kind of change**:
   - **Minor change: earlier signatures still count**;
   - **Major change: everyone asked signs again**.
3. Write **What changed** and choose **Start draft**. The draft starts from the version in force.
4. Under **Draft version** (the number), change the text, document, roles or review date. Choose
   **Save draft** as often as you need.

## Publish a draft

1. Open the policy. Under **Draft**, check the draft.
2. If it says **Not ready to publish**, add what it names (a role, a review date, or both) and
   choose **Save draft**.
3. If it says **Someone else must publish this draft**, ask another person who manages policies to
   check and publish it.
4. Otherwise, under **Publish version** (the number), tick **I have checked this draft and want to
   put it in force**.
5. Choose **Publish version** (the number). The page says "Version published. It is now in force,
   and the version before it is superseded."

## Important

- Only one version is in force at a time. The **Policy library** shows only the version in force.
- In the **Change log**, a superseded version always names the version in force, for example
  "Superseded: version 3 is in force", so nobody reads it as current. Once the policy is retired it
  says "Superseded. No version is in force now."
- A published version cannot be changed. To change it, start a new draft.
- Only one draft at a time.
- Harbour gives the author (or the publisher) a task when the review date is near or past.
- The rules: [quality spec section 17, rules 25 to 28](../../specs/quality.md#17-phase-2-domain-rules-slice-8-qual-001-002-004-005-006-008-012),
  [section 18, policy library](../../specs/quality.md#18-policy-library-and-read-and-sign-slice-25-qual-008-migration-0087)
  and section 21 of the quality spec on the `feat/quality-dashboard-pir` branch; the hazard
  [HZ-128](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-128-staff-follow-a-superseded-policy-or-procedure).
- Staff sign with [How to read and sign a policy](../all-staff/read-and-sign-a-policy.md).
