# How to sign in

**Who this is for**: everyone who uses Harbour.

**Register row**: PLAT-004.

## With a passkey

1. Go to Harbour. You see **Sign in to Harbour**.
2. Choose **Sign in with a passkey**.
3. Use your fingerprint, face or device PIN.

## With a password and an authenticator app

1. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**.
2. Open your authenticator app and type the 6-digit code under **Enter your 6-digit code**.
3. Choose **Sign in**.

If the code does not match, enter the newest one: codes change every 30 seconds.

## If you work for more than one organisation

Harbour asks you to **Choose an organisation**. Choose the one you are working for now. You can
switch later from **Organisation** in the bar at the top of the page.

## Why Harbour asks you to sign in again

- **Care records need a second step.** If you signed in with a password only, Harbour says "Use
  your second way to sign in". Sign in again with your passkey, or your password and a code.
- **Some actions need you to confirm it is you** (step-up), for example opening a record outside
  your access, printing the downtime pack, or recording a statutory notification. Harbour shows
  **Confirm it is you** and a **Sign in again** button. You must have used your passkey or a code
  in the last 10 minutes. A password alone never counts. Afterwards, do the action again: what
  you typed is not kept.

## Sign out

Choose **Your account** at the top of the page, then **Sign out**. Always sign out of a shared
computer.

## Good to know

- After several attempts, Harbour asks you to wait a minute. It never locks you out.
- There is no "forgot password" link and backup codes cannot be typed in yet. Ask your manager if
  you cannot sign in.
- First time? See [How to accept an invitation](accept-an-invitation.md).
