# How to accept an invitation and set up your sign-in

**Who this is for**: anyone a manager has invited to Harbour.

**Register row**: PLAT-004.

## Steps

1. Open the link in your invitation email. It works for 72 hours unless your home set another
   time.
2. You see **Accept your invitation** and the organisation's name.
3. Enter **Your name**: the name your colleagues know you by.
4. Under **Create a password**, enter at least 12 characters. Three or four random words you will
   remember work well. Common passwords are turned down.
5. Choose **Create sign-in**, then **Sign in**.
6. Set up a passkey or an authenticator app (see
   [How to set up a passkey or an authenticator app](set-up-a-second-sign-in-step.md)). You need one
   before you can open care records.

If you already have a Harbour sign-in, Harbour asks you to **Sign in first**. Sign in, then choose
**Accept invitation**.

## If the link does not work

- **Expired** or **withdrawn**: ask your manager to send a new one.
- **Signed in with a different email address**: sign out, then open the link again.
