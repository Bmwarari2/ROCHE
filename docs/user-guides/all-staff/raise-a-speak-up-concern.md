# How to raise a speak-up concern

Tell the Freedom to Speak Up lead about something that worries you: care, safety, how someone
behaves, bullying, or fraud.

**Who can do this**: every member of staff, including agency and bank workers. Family members
using the family portal cannot. To speak up without signing in, see
[How to speak up anonymously](speak-up-anonymously.md).

**Register row**: SAFE-012.

## Steps

1. Choose **Speak up** in the menu.
2. Under **Raise a concern**, answer **Who may know it was you**:
   - **Named**: the lead and anyone the lead names on the case know it was you.
   - **Confidential**: only the Freedom to Speak Up lead knows it was you.
3. Under **What it is about**, choose the closest answer, for example **Care quality or safety**
   or **Bullying or harassment**. If none fits, choose **Something else**.
4. Under **What happened**, say what you saw or heard, when and where.
5. Choose **Send to the Freedom to Speak Up lead**. The page says "Sent to the Freedom to Speak Up
   lead".
6. Your concern now shows under **Concerns you raised**, with its state (for example
   **Received** or **Being looked into**) and a reference.

## Read replies and write back

1. Go to **Speak up**. Under **Concerns you raised**, find your concern by its reference.
2. Messages show oldest first. When the lead has finished, their answer is under **What happened**.
3. To write back, use **Reply** and choose **Send**. You cannot reply once the case is
   **Closed**.

## Good to know

- Your concern goes to the Freedom to Speak Up lead, not to your manager. No manager sees it unless
  the lead names them on the case.
- If you chose **Confidential**, people the lead names on the case see the concern but not your
  name.
- What you write may still show who you are, for example a shift only you worked.
- If something is putting a person in danger now, also tell the person in charge or follow your
  safeguarding procedure straight away. A speak-up concern is not an emergency route: the lead
  is told in Harbour that it has arrived, without your name, but may not read it straight away.
- You can also speak up outside the organisation, to the National Guardian's Office or a prescribed
  person such as the Care Quality Commission.
- The rules: [safety specification, Phase 2 rule 3](../../specs/safety.md#phase-2-rules-plan-slice-7-safe-006-safe-011-safe-012-safe-014-safe-015-safe-016-meds-012)
  and [ADR-0089](../../decisions/0089-restricted-silos-whistleblowing.md).
