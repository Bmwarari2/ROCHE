# How to see the history of a record

Nothing in a care record is deleted. Every earlier version is kept.

**Who can do this**: anyone who can read the person's record.

**Register row**: PLAT-007.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **History**.
2. Under **Care records with earlier versions**, find the care plan, risk assessment or advance
   care record.
3. Choose **View the changes to** it.
4. Each earlier version is marked **Earlier version: not in use now**, with when it was in place
   and who changed it. Fields marked **Changed in the next version** are the ones that changed.
   On the newest earlier version, **Changed since this version** shows what the latest change
   altered.
5. To see the record in use, choose the **See ... as it is now** link at the top.
6. Choose **Back to the history**.

## Good to know

- **Never act on an earlier version.** Follow the record as it is now.
- Corrections to notes are shown on the **Notes** page, with the original struck through.
- Some fields and values in the history are shown as codes, not plain words, for now.
