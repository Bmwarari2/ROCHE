# How to read your home page

See what is waiting for you now, with each count linked to the list it comes from.

**Who can do this**: every member of staff. Each person sees the tiles their role uses. Family
members see their updates page instead.

**Register row**: INSI-001.

## Steps

1. Choose **Home** in the menu. The page says "Welcome," and your name, and shows the time the
   counts were worked out.
2. **Today** shows the work of the shift: **Alerts**, **Tasks on the round** and **Handover**.
3. **Safety and oversight** shows the rest your role uses, for example **Reviews due**,
   **Incidents open**, **Statutory notifications**, **DoLS expiry reminders** (DoLS is the Deprivation of Liberty
   Safeguards), **Outages** and
   **Break-glass reports**.
4. Each tile says where it stands in a word: **Nothing waiting**, **None open**, **To do**,
   **Overdue**, **Urgent** or **Not known**. It says how many, and how many of those are overdue or
   urgent.
5. Choose the tile's link, for example **View the round** or **View incidents**, to open the list.
6. To read the counts again now, choose **Read the counts again**.

## Good to know

- **The tiles are counts, not the record.** Open the list before you act.
- **Some not loaded** means only part of the count could be read; the tile says "At least" a
  number. **Could not load** means Thapsus Care could not read it: open the list to check.
- If you work at several homes, some tiles say **Choose a location**. Choose one in the location
  switcher.
