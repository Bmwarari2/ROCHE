# How to handle speak-up cases

Acknowledge, look into and close the concerns people raise, and keep in touch with the person who
raised them.

**Who can do this**: the Freedom to Speak Up lead (the built-in role "Freedom to Speak Up lead").
That role carries nothing else, and nobody who manages users or roles can hold it. Members of staff
the lead names on a case can read that case and write on it, but only the lead moves it on.
Managers, the owner and the quality lead cannot see cases unless the lead names them.

**Register row**: SAFE-012.

## Open your cases

1. Choose **Speak up** in the menu, then **Speak-up cases**. If you are named on a case but are
   not the lead, the button says **Cases you are named on**.
2. You need to have signed in with your passkey or authenticator app.
3. Cases show newest first, titled by what they are about, the date and a reference, never a name.
   Each shows its state and whether it is **Named**, **Confidential** or **Anonymous**. Use
   **Older cases** and **Newest cases** to move between pages.
4. Open a case by its title.

## Steps for the lead

1. Read **What was raised**. Under it Harbour says who raised it, or that it was raised
   anonymously or in confidence.
2. Under **Acknowledge**, choose **Acknowledge**.
3. Under **People on the case**, tick who works on it and choose **Save people on the case**. They
   see the case and its messages. They see who raised it only when it is named.
4. Under **Start looking into it**, choose **Start looking into it**.
5. Keep in touch: under **Messages**, use **Write to the person who raised it** and choose
   **Send**.
6. When you have finished, under **Share the outcome**, write **What happened** in words for the
   person who raised it. They see this. Choose **Share the outcome**.
7. Under **Close the case**, write **Why it is closed** and choose **Close the case**.

## Good to know

- Closing is final: the case cannot be reopened, and nobody can reply on it.
- An anonymous concern raised without a reply code says "Nobody can be told about this case: it
  was raised anonymously with no way to reply."
- If the page warns **Possible flood**, many anonymous concerns arrived today and new ones are
  refused above the organisation's daily limit. Check for repeats.
- When a concern arrives, every Freedom to Speak Up lead is told in Harbour: "A speak-up concern
  has arrived", or, for one about care quality or safety, an urgent "A speak-up concern about care
  quality or safety has arrived". The alert names nobody and says nothing else of the concern.
  It is not sent to your phone or email, and nobody else is told, so check your cases often,
  and arrange cover with your organisation when you are away.
- Harbour never tells managers about a case, and never sends it to the regulator or the police.
  If a concern needs a safeguarding referral or an incident record, you decide and make it
  yourself.
- Appointing the lead has no web screen yet. Ask the person who manages Harbour for your
  organisation.
