# How to search for a person

**Who can do this**: most staff. You only find people, colleagues, documents and notes you can
already see.

**Register row**: PLAT-013.

## Steps

1. Go to **Search** in the menu.
2. Type a name, a room, a word from a note, a document title, or a 10-digit NHS number.
3. To narrow it, choose under **Look in**: people, colleagues, documents or notes.
4. Choose **Search**.
5. Before you open a result, check the photo, date of birth and NHS number.

## Same name

If two people have the same name, Harbour says "Same name as another result: check the photo and
date of birth before opening". **Always check before you open or record anything.**

## Good to know

- Each result shows the person's status, for example **Receiving care** or **In hospital**, and
  their communication needs.
- If nothing is found, check the spelling or search by NHS number.
- Someone you cannot find may be outside your access. Managers can search outside it with
  break-glass (see
  [How to open a record with break-glass](../registered-manager/open-a-record-with-break-glass.md)).
- Searching with no signal is not available yet.
