# How to set up a passkey or an authenticator app

You need a second way to prove it is you before you can open care records.

**Who this is for**: everyone, including family members.

**Register row**: PLAT-004.

Go to **Settings** in the menu, or **Your account** then **Sign-in and security**.

## Add a passkey

1. Under **Passkeys**, give it a name if you like, for example "Work laptop".
2. Choose **Add a passkey** and confirm on your device with your fingerprint, face or PIN.
3. Harbour says "Passkey added".

## Set up an authenticator app

1. Under **Authenticator app**, enter **Your password** and choose
   **Set up an authenticator app**.
2. Scan the QR code with the app on your phone. If you cannot scan it, type the key shown instead.
3. Write down the backup codes and keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone.
4. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and choose **Finish setting up**.

## Sign out of other devices

Under **Where you are signed in**, choose **Sign out of this device** beside any device you do not
recognise, or **Sign out of all other devices**.

## Good to know

- If you change phone, set the authenticator app up again on the new one.
- Removing a passkey or turning off the authenticator app is not available on the web yet. Ask
  your manager.
