# How to send someone to hospital

Harbour makes the hospital transfer pack (the "red bag" summary) from the record in one step.

**Who can do this**: senior carers and team leaders, nurses, managers and the owner. Care workers
cannot: tell the person in charge.

**Register row**: PEOP-009. On the web only; the mobile app has no transfer pack.

## Before you send

1. Open the person's record and choose **Hospital**.
2. Read **The pack as it would be made now**. Check any section shown as **Not recorded** and
   anything under "needs checking", for example resuscitation records that disagree.

## Send the person

1. Under **Send to hospital**, enter the **Hospital**.
2. Check **When did they leave?** and add the **Reason for transfer and what has changed**.
3. Check the **Belongings to send with them** (glasses, hearing aids, dentures, walking aids).
4. Choose **Send to hospital and make the pack**. Rounds pause and the bed is kept.
5. Choose **Print the transfer pack (PDF)** and print it.
6. **Check the name, date of birth and NHS number on the pack against the person** before it goes.
7. Choose **Record the hand-over**. If the pack is missing safety-critical information, say why it
   is going as it is.

## When they come back

1. Open **Hospital** and, under **Return from hospital**, enter **When did they come back?**
2. Choose **Record the return**. Rounds restart and the care plan review comes forward.
3. Work through **Checks on return** (discharge letter, medicines, new allergies, resuscitation
   decisions, food and drink levels, skin and body map, belongings). Choose **Mark as done** for
   each.

## Good to know

- Missing information prints as "not recorded", never blank. The pack always goes, even when
  something is missing: phone the hospital with anything important.
- Earlier packs can be printed again from **Earlier transfers**.
