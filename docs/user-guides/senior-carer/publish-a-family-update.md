# How to publish a family update

Write a short everyday update for the person's family, from a recent note, activity or photo.

**Who can do this**: senior carers and team leaders, nurses, managers and the owner.

**Register row**: COMMS-001.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Family updates**.
2. Under **What is the update from?**, choose **A note**, **An activity** or **A photo**, then choose
   which one. Only records from the last two weeks are listed.
3. For a note, write the **Words for the family** in plain words. The note itself is never shared.
   Do not name other residents or give health details.
4. For a photo, add a caption if you like, and tick that nobody else can be recognised in it.
5. Choose **Preview the update**.
6. If Harbour asks for a **Reason it may be published**, say why the family can read this here, for
   example that they have already been told by phone. Then preview again.
7. Read **Check before publishing**: this is what the family will read.
8. Choose **Publish the update**.

## Take an update down

1. Under **Updates published**, choose why it is being taken down.
2. Choose **Take the update down**. The family is told only that an update was taken down.

## Good to know

- **The feed is not for news.** Never use it for falls, hospital visits, illness or deaths. Phone
  the family as your home's policy says.
- Who in the family sees an update depends on the person's sharing decisions. Sharing decisions
  have no screen yet.
- Updates are on hold while the person is in hospital, while the next of kin is still to be told
  about an incident, or while a duty of candour step is open.
