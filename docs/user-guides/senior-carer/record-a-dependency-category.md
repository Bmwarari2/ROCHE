# How to record a dependency category

Record which category of your organisation's dependency scale a person is in, so their care hours
count in the rota check.

**Who can do this**: team leaders or senior carers, nurses, managers and the owner. Anyone who can
read care plans can see a person's category.

**Register row**: CARE-004.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose the **Dependency** tab. **In force now** shows their current
   category, the date it applies from and the scale version it came from.
2. Under **Record a category**, choose the **Category**.
3. Choose the **Kind of record**: **First record**, **Review** or **Correction**.
4. Enter **Applies from**: today or earlier.
5. Under **Why**, say why, for example "Needs two staff for all transfers since the fall on
   02/11/2026".
6. Choose **Decided by** and then **Record category**.

## Important

- The scale is your organisation's own. The category is your judgement; Thapsus Care does not work
  it out or score the person.
- A person with no category is not counted in the staffing figures until one is recorded.
- A review applies from its own date. A correction replaces the record in force from that record's
  date; the earlier record stays under **All records**, shown as **Corrected**.
- Any one-to-one hours are carried into the new record unchanged.
- If no approved scale applies at the person's home, you cannot record a category. Ask your
  Registered Manager to [set up a dependency
  scale](../registered-manager/set-up-a-dependency-scale.md).
