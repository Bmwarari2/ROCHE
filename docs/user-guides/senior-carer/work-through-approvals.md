# How to work through approvals

See, in one list, the records waiting for a decision, such as incidents, duty of candour
records, statutory notifications and complaints, and where each stands against its deadline.

**Who can do this**: staff who review, close or submit records: team leaders or senior carers,
nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager, the owner and the quality and
compliance lead. Each person sees the records for the homes and people they work with, and the
steps their role can take.

**Register row**: PLAT-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Approvals** in the menu.
2. Read the three lists: **Waiting for your approval**, **Raised by you, waiting for someone else**
   and **Other open work in your scope**.
3. Each record says what it is and when it was raised, where it stands, for example **Under
   review**, and its deadline in words: "Overdue since", "Due today by", "Due by" a time,
   "Deadline kept on the record: open it to check" or "No deadline". For a duty of candour record,
   the time for the written notification is your organisation's target, not a legal deadline:
   open the record to see it.
4. For an incident waiting for you, you can close it, or send it back to open, here, in the same
   way as on the incident (see
   [How to review and close an incident](../registered-manager/review-and-close-an-incident.md)).
   The page says "Incident closed:" or "Sent back to open:" and the incident.
5. For anything else, open the record and take the next step on its own page.

## Good to know

- **Whoever raised a record cannot approve it.** A record you raised says "You raised this, so
  someone else must approve it."
- If an incident cannot close yet, open it to see what must be done first.
- If the page says "Not every open record fits on this page.", open the list for each kind of
  record, for example the incidents list, to see the rest.
