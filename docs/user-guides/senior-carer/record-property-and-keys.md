# How to record a person's property and keys

Describe, count and sign for each item a person brings in, record keys and fobs, and record
when an item is returned, disposed of or lost.

**Who can do this**: team leaders or senior carers, nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers,
the Registered Manager and the owner.

**Register row**: PEOP-007.

## Record an item in

1. Open the person's **Property and keys** tab.
2. Under **Record an item in**, write the **Description**: what it is and how to tell it apart,
   for example "a gold ring with a blue stone".
3. Choose the **Category** and enter **How many**. Tick **A valuable** for anything of value that
   is not jewellery, a watch, electronics or documents (those are always valuables).
4. Enter the **Day it came in**.
5. Under **Who signed for it**, choose **The person signed** or **Their representative signed**
   and type the name they typed, or choose **Nobody could sign** and say **Why nobody could
   sign**.
6. For a valuable, or when nobody could sign, a second member of staff witnesses it: type their
   **Colleague's name**, choose **Find colleague**, then **Choose the witness**. They do not sign
   in; the record names you both.
7. Choose **Record the item**. The page says "Item recorded" and it shows under **Held now**.

## Return, dispose of or lose an item

- **Return:** choose **Return:** and the item. Enter the **Day it was returned**, the **Name of
  who received it** and how **They received it as**, then choose **Record the return**. After a
  death, property goes only to the personal representative.
- **Dispose of:** choose **Dispose of:** and the item, enter the day and **Why it was disposed
  of**, name a witness, and choose **Record disposal**.
- **Lost:** record the incident first. Then choose **Record as lost:** and the item, enter the
  day, choose the **Incident recorded for the loss** and choose **Record as lost**.

Once returned, disposed of or lost, an item moves to **Returned, disposed of or lost**. It cannot
be returned afterwards.

## Keys and fobs

1. Under **Issue a key or fob**, describe the **Key or fob**, for example "room 12 door key", the
   **Day it was issued** and who it was **Issued to**.
2. Choose **Issue the key**. The page says "Key issued".
3. When it comes back, or is lost, choose **Returned** or **Reported lost** on the key, enter
   the day and choose **Record the return** or **Record as lost**.

## Good to know

- Cash is not recorded here: it goes in the residents' money ledger.
- Glasses, hearing aids, dentures and walking aids are marked **Goes to hospital with the
  person**, so they are offered to go with them.
