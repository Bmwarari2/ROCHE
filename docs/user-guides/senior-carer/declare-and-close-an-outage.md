# How to declare and close an outage

Declare an outage when Harbour cannot be used, so paper records can be entered against it later.

**Who can do this**: senior carers and team leaders, nurses, managers and the owner.

**Register row**: OPS-028.

## Declare an outage

1. Go to **Downtime** in the menu and choose your home.
2. Under **Declare an outage**, choose **What has stopped working?**, for example "Power cut" or
   "Network down".
3. Under **What happened**, say what has stopped and what staff are doing instead (at least 10
   characters). Do not write anyone's name: care workers can read this.
4. Enter **When did it start?** in UK time on the 24-hour clock.
5. Leave **When did it end?** empty if it is still going on.
6. Choose **Declare outage**. The Registered Manager is told.
7. Get out the downtime pack and the paper forms (see
   [How to print the downtime pack](print-the-downtime-pack.md)).

If you cannot reach Harbour at all, use the paper forms first and declare the outage when you can.
An outage declared more than 2 hours after it started shows **Declared late**.

## Close the outage

1. Open the outage from **Downtime**.
2. Choose **Close the outage**.
3. Enter **When did systems come back?**
4. Choose **Close outage**. Paper records can now be entered (see
   [How to enter paper records after an outage](../care-worker/enter-paper-records-after-an-outage.md)).
   They must be entered within 24 hours.

## Other changes

- **Correct the times** if the start or end was wrong, and say why.
- **Cancel the outage** if it was declared by mistake. This cannot be undone, and is refused once a
  paper record has been entered against it.

When the paper records are in, a manager reconciles the outage
([How to reconcile an outage](../registered-manager/reconcile-an-outage.md)).
