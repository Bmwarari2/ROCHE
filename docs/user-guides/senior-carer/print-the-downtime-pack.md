# How to print the downtime pack

The downtime pack is a paper copy of what staff need to give safe care when Harbour cannot be
reached: a front sheet of warnings and one page per person.

**Who can do this**: senior carers and team leaders, nurses, managers and the owner. Care workers
cannot. You must confirm it is you with your passkey or authenticator app (step-up). A password
alone is not enough.

**Register row**: OPS-028.

## Steps

1. Go to **Downtime** in the menu. Choose your home if Harbour asks.
2. Choose **Open the pack**. If Harbour shows **Confirm it is you**, sign in again with your
   passkey or a code from your authenticator app.
3. Check the chip says **In date**, and read the **Use until** time.
4. Choose **Download the pack to print (PDF)** and print it.
5. Keep the paper copy where your home's downtime policy says. It holds personal and health
   details for everyone at the home.
6. Shred the old pack when you put the new one out.

## What is in the pack

- Everyone receiving care at the home, including people on leave or in hospital, in room order.
- For each person: name, date of birth, NHS number, room, allergies, resuscitation decisions, food
  and drink levels, communication needs, key contacts, care plan summary and risk assessments.
- No photos. Confirm who the person is with a colleague who knows them.
- Missing information is said in words, for example "Allergies not recorded. Do not assume there
  are none."
- The paper MAR and personal evacuation plans are not in the pack. It says where they are kept,
  or to ask the nurse in charge.

## How old it can be

- A new pack is made every night, and again when people move in, move out or change rooms.
- A pack can be used for 36 hours from **Information as at**.
- Paper does not change. Always check the **Use until** line on the paper before you rely on it.
- If it is out of date, check allergies, resuscitation status and diet with the nurse in charge
  before acting.

## Good to know

- Opening and printing each count towards a limit (30 an hour) and each is recorded.
- Harbour does not keep a copy of the printed pack.
