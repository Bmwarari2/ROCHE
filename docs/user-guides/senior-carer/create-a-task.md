# How to create a task

Add a one-off or repeating task for a home or service, for example a fire door check.

**Who can do this**: team leaders or senior carers, nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers,
the Registered Manager and the owner.

**Register row**: PLAT-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Tasks** in the menu.
2. Under **Create a task**, enter **What needs doing**: a short title, for example "Check the fire
   doors". Do not include anyone's name or care details.
3. Enter **When it is due**, in UK time on the 24-hour clock. It must be a time that has not
   passed yet.
4. Choose **Where**: the home or service the task belongs to.
5. Answer **Does it repeat?**: **No, just once**, **Every day at that time** or **Every week on that
   day and time**.
6. Tick **Urgent** if it is, and **Assign it to me** if you will do it yourself.
7. Choose **Create task**. The page says "Task created.", or "Repeating task scheduled." for a
   repeating one.

## Good to know

- To work through tasks, take, unassign or cancel them, see
  [How to work through your tasks](../care-worker/work-through-your-tasks.md).
- Thapsus Care also creates tasks itself, for example after a fall, or when medicines stock needs
  counting. Those come with the record that needs them.
