# How to respond to a lone-working alarm

Check on a lone worker who has asked for help or stopped checking in, and acknowledge the alarm
once you have.

**Who can do this**: people who monitor lone workers (team leaders or senior carers, care
coordinators, managers and the owner), at the homes where they work. You need to have signed in with
your passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: SCHED-012.

## Steps

1. Go to **Lone working** in the menu and choose **Lone workers now**. Open alarms come first. A
   silent alarm, shown as duress, is at the top, then requests for help, then missed check-ins and
   sessions not finished.
2. Read the alarm: the worker's name, **Where**, **Alarm raised**, **Check-in was due**, **Last
   check-in**, and how many times it has been **Sent**.
3. Contact the worker now. If they asked for help and you cannot reach them, call 999. A duress
   alarm means they may be in danger and unable to say so. Do not call them in a way that gives this
   away. Follow your duress procedure. Call 999 if you cannot confirm they are safe.
4. Only once you have checked on them, choose **What did you do?**: **Spoke to them: they are
   safe**, **Help sent to them**, **Called 999** or **Something else**.
5. Write a **Note**: what happened and who you spoke to.
6. Choose **Acknowledge alarm**. If they were not confirmed safe, choose **Report an incident**.

## Important

- An alarm is raised when a check-in is overdue and the grace period ends, or at once when the
  worker asks for help. Step 1 of the on-call route is told at once by email, and later steps,
  including the Registered Manager, follow by their minutes.
- The alarm is sent again to everyone on the route until someone acknowledges it. Reading an email
  does not stop it.
- Thapsus Care tells people; it does not send help. Whoever acknowledges must have acted.
- The worker cannot acknowledge their own alarm, and your own session is not on your board.
- The board checks again every 30 seconds and the tab title shows how many alarms are open. If it
  says **Not updated since**, check your connection: alarms are still sent by email.
- Every acknowledgement is recorded, with who did it, what was done and the note.
- Neither the phone app nor the web page sends where a worker is yet, so the board does not show
  **Last location shared**.
