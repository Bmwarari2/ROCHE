# How to set the staff privacy notice

Choose which document is your staff privacy notice. Staff are then asked to confirm they have read
it, in **My privacy**.

**Who can do this**: the owner.

**Register row**: WORK-011.

## Steps

1. Upload the staff privacy notice in **Documents** as an organisation document.
2. Open **Data protection** in the menu.
3. Under **Staff privacy notice**, choose the document under **Notice in force**. Each version is
   listed by its title and version number.
4. Choose **Save the notice**.

## Good to know

- When you upload a new version, choose the new version here. Staff are then asked to confirm they
  have read it.
- On each staff file, HR and managers see whether the member has acknowledged the notice in force.
