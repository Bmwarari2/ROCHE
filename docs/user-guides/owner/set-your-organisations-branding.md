# How to set your organisation's branding

Choose the name, colour and logo your staff see at the top of every screen.

**Who can do this**: the owner only.

**Register row**: PLAT-002.

## Steps

1. Go to **Branding** in the menu.
2. Under **Name and colour**, enter a **Display name** if you want staff to see a name other
   than your registered name. Leave it blank to use the registered name.
3. Enter an **Accent colour** as a six-digit hex code, for example #0074e8. Leave it blank to use
   the standard colour.
4. Read **How easy it is to read**. Each check says **Passes** or **Fails**, and **Preview** shows
   an **Example button** and a **Selected item** in the **Light theme** and the **Dark theme**.
5. If the colour cannot be used, the page says why and offers the nearest colour that works, on
   a button such as "Use #005bb5 instead". Choose it, or try another colour.
6. Choose **Save name and colour**. The page says "Name and colour saved."

## Add or remove the logo

1. Under **Logo**, choose a **Logo image**: a PNG or JPEG image of 512 KB or less.
2. Choose **Upload logo**. The page says "Logo saved." It shows 24 pixels high beside the product
   name at the top of every screen.
3. To take it off, choose **Remove logo**. Thapsus Care asks **Remove your organisation's
   logo?**: choose **Remove logo** again, or **Keep logo**. You can upload it again at any time.

## Good to know

- Letters and statutory documents always keep your registered name, whatever the display name.
- A colour that is too light or too dark to read on buttons is never saved. The check covers both
  themes, so words on buttons stay readable for everyone.
- Any location or camera details in the logo file are removed when it is uploaded.
