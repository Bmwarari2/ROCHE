# How to work through the getting started checklist

The checklist shows what must be done before your organisation goes live. Each item is worked out
from your records, so it shows as done only when it is.

**Who can do this**: the owner, Registered Manager, deputy and quality lead can read it. Only the
owner can record decisions.

**Register row**: PLAT-018.

## Steps

1. Go to **Getting started** in the menu.
2. Read **Progress**, for example "6 of 14 done".
3. Under **Checklist**, work through items marked **Not done: blocks go-live** first.
4. For a setting, choose **Accept Harbour's setting** once it has been reviewed. For clinical
   settings, first choose who reviewed them (the clinical lead or Clinical Safety Officer).
5. For retention, choose **Keep Harbour's period** or **We do not keep these**.
6. For "people ready", open each person listed and add what is missing, such as a photo or
   allergy status.
7. When it says "Ready to go live: nothing is blocking it", you are ready.

## Practise with sample data

Before you add your first real person you can load a few invented people to practise with.

1. Under **Sample data**, choose **Load sample data**, then confirm.
2. While loaded, every page says "Sample data is in use" and the people show **Sample**. Nobody
   can add a real person.
3. When you are done, choose **Remove sample data** and confirm it is you. This removes everything
   recorded about them and cannot be undone.

## Good to know

- Each setting is named in plain words made from Harbour's own name for it, for example "Care plan
  review interval (months)". The reasons are in English only for now.
