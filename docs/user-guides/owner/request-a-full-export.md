# How to get a full export of your data

A full copy of everything your organisation holds in Harbour, free at any time.

**Who can do this**: the owner only. Every step needs you to have confirmed it is you with your
passkey or authenticator app in the last 10 minutes, including opening the page.

**Register row**: PLAT-015.

## Steps

1. Go to **Data export** in the menu. Confirm it is you if asked.
2. Choose **Ask for an export**. Only one can be prepared at a time.
3. You are told when it is ready. The export shows **Ready to download**.
4. Choose **Download**, read the warning, then **Download now**.
5. Save the file straight away somewhere only authorised people can open.

## Good to know

- **You can download it once.** Harbour then deletes the file. To download again, ask for a new
  export.
- The link expires 72 hours after it is ready, then the file is deleted.
- Only the person who asked can download it.
- It holds the records of everyone the organisation supports and employs.
- To stop one being prepared, choose **Cancel export**. This cannot be undone.
