# How to lock and unlock Thapsus Care on the phone

Thapsus Care locks itself when you stop using it, so nobody else can read the care records on your
phone. You unlock it with your face, fingerprint or passcode.

**Who can do this**: everyone who uses Care mode on the phone: care and support workers, agency or
bank workers, team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and
deputy managers.

**Register rows**: MOB-009, MOB-010.

## Unlock

1. When Thapsus Care is locked it shows "Thapsus Care is locked" and who is signed in.
2. The phone asks for your face, fingerprint or passcode straight away. If you cancelled, choose
   "Unlock" and try again.
3. You go back to the screen you were on.

## Lock it yourself

1. Go to **More** in the tab bar, then choose **Settings**.
2. Under "Security", choose "Lock now".

Lock it whenever you put the phone down, even for a moment.

## Check when it locks

1. Go to **More**, then **Settings**.
2. Under "Security", read the line, for example "Thapsus Care locks after 2 minutes without use."
3. Under "This device is", check the right choice is selected:
   - "My own device": it locks after 2 minutes without use.
   - "Shared with colleagues": choose how soon under "Lock a shared device after". See
     [How to use a shared phone or tablet](use-a-shared-phone-or-tablet.md).

## If it will not unlock

- "Unlock was cancelled. Tap Unlock to try again." Choose "Unlock".
- "Too many attempts. Unlock your device with its passcode first, then try again." Unlock the phone
  itself with its passcode, then choose "Unlock".
- "This device has no screen lock. Set a passcode, PIN or pattern in Settings to use Thapsus Care."
  Set a screen lock in the phone's own settings.
- If the screen says "This device's security settings changed, so the data stored on it was
  reset.", the care records were removed from the phone because its screen lock, face or
  fingerprints changed. They download again when you are online. Anything recorded on the phone
  but not yet sent was lost with them. Tell the nurse or senior in charge what you recorded, so it
  can be recorded again. Choose "Dismiss" once you have read it.

## Important

- The lock screen also has **Sign out**. Do not sign out to get past the lock. Anything you
  recorded but not yet sent would be deleted with the care records (HZ-040). If the app has just
  been opened, the phone cannot even count them, and says **This device may hold entries that have
  not reached the server. They will be deleted.** Unlock instead, or ask the person in charge.
- If the phone's screen lock, face or fingerprints change, the care records and anything not yet
  sent are removed from the phone (HZ-006). See
  [How to keep the phone safe for care records](keep-the-phone-safe-for-care-records.md).
- The care records stay on the phone while it is locked. The phone sends entries only while
  Thapsus Care is open, not when the app is closed. Check **Today** shows **No changes waiting to
  upload** before you rely on it.
- While you are signed in, the app's preview in the phone's app switcher is hidden, and screenshots
  of care records are blocked. See [How to keep the phone safe for care records](keep-the-phone-safe-for-care-records.md).
