# How to work through your tasks

See what is due, start it, and mark it as done or say why it was not done.

**Who can do this**: every member of staff, for their own tasks. Team leaders or senior carers,
nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the owner can also cancel
tasks, take them and unassign them.

**Register row**: PLAT-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Tasks** in the menu. **My tasks** shows the tasks for you; **Team tasks** shows your
   team's.
2. Under **Show tasks**, choose **Open** or **Finished**, and **Yesterday to tomorrow** or
   **7 days either side**. **Open from earlier** lists open tasks that were due before then.
3. Each task says where it stands in a word: **Upcoming**, **Due**, **Started**, **Overdue**,
   **Overdue: escalated**, **Done**, **Not done** or **Cancelled**. **Urgent** and **Review** are
   marked in words too. It also shows **About**, **Where**, **Due** and **Assigned to**, in UK
   time.
4. Choose **Start** when you begin. The page says "Task started."
5. When it is finished, choose **Mark as done**. The page says "Task marked as done."
6. If it could not be done, choose **Record as not done**, say **Why was it not done?** in a few
   words, and choose **Record as not done**. Choose **Keep task** to go back.

## Tasks that need a record

Some tasks say "This task needs a record of what was done. Record it on the person's record and
the task is completed with it." They have no **Mark as done**: record what you did on the
person's record, for example a chart entry, and the task finishes itself.

## Reviews

Thapsus Care raises a **Review** task when an entry needs checking, for example one recorded
late, or one sent from a phone after someone else had changed the record. The task lists
**Entries to review**, or the rounds missed. Choose **Show reviews only** to see just these.

## For team leaders, senior carers, nurses and managers

- **Take this task** assigns it to you. **Unassign task** takes it off whoever has it.
- **Cancel task** needs a reason: **Why is it being cancelled?**. The page says "Task cancelled."
- To add a task, see [How to create a task](../senior-carer/create-a-task.md).

## Good to know

- Do not write anyone's name or care details in a reason.
- If more than 1,000 tasks fall in the period, the page says so. Choose **Yesterday to tomorrow**,
  or **My tasks**, so fewer tasks fall in the period.
- **Open from earlier** shows the most recent open tasks first and may leave older ones out. If it
  does, the page says how many, for example "1 older open task is not listed. Ask Thapsus Care
  support if you need to see it."
