# How to download a training course to the phone

Download an e-learning course while you have a connection, so you can take it later without one.

**Who can do this**: staff who take training in Thapsus Care: care and support workers, team leaders
or senior carers, nurses and clinical leads, Registered Managers, deputy managers, owners,
activities, catering, housekeeping and maintenance staff, and agency or bank workers.

**Register rows**: WORK-006, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Connect to the internet, ideally Wi-Fi.
2. Go to **Settings** at the bottom of the screen. Under **Training**, choose
   **Open training courses**.
3. Find the course. **Not on this phone** means it is not downloaded yet.
4. Choose **Download**. The screen shows "Downloading: 3 of 40 files", for example.
5. When it is done, the course says **On this phone** and its size, for example "Size: 12 MB".
6. To take it, see [How to take a training course offline on the phone](take-a-training-course-offline-on-the-phone.md).

## To remove a course

1. Choose **Remove from this phone** on the course.
2. Thapsus Care asks **Remove this course from the phone?** and says **Work waiting to send is kept
   and still sent. You can download the course again later.**
3. Choose **Remove from this phone** again, or **Keep course**.

## Good to know

- A course is kept only when every file has arrived. If the download stops, the screen says
  **The course did not download, so nothing was kept. Try again when the connection is better.**
- The phone needs room for the course with some to spare. If there is not enough, it says how much
  the course needs and how much is free. Free some space and try again.
- With no connection, the list says **There is no connection, so only the courses on this phone are
  shown.**
- Downloaded courses are removed from the phone when you sign out or the phone is wiped. Download
  them again when you next sign in.
