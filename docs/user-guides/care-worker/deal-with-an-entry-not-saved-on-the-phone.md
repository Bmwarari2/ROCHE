# How to deal with an entry that was not saved on the phone

Sometimes Thapsus Care does not accept something the phone sends: a note, a chart entry, a body map
mark, a task you marked or a handover acknowledgement. The phone keeps it under **Not saved: needs
attention**, with the reason, until someone deals with it. It is never dropped on its own.

**Who can do this**: everyone who uses Care mode on the phone. Tell the person in charge before you
remove anything.

**Register rows**: MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Find **Not saved: needs attention**. It shows on **Today**, at the top of the round, and on the
   person's record. The banner at the top also says, for example, "1 entry was not saved and needs
   attention."
2. Read each item:
   - what it is and who it is for, for example "Chart entry for Ada Example"
   - **Reason:** the reason Thapsus Care gave
   - **What was recorded:** the details you entered.
3. Tell the person in charge what it says. If it shows **Thapsus Care has a copy for the
   nurse in charge to review.**, they can see it on the web too.
4. If the care still needs recording, record it again, on the phone or on the web, or ask the
   person in charge to.
5. When it has been recorded again, or the person in charge knows about it, choose **Remove from
   this device**.
6. Under **Why are you removing this entry?**, choose a reason, for example **Recorded again
   elsewhere, for example on paper** or **The nurse in charge knows and will make sure the care is
   recorded**. If you choose **Other**, say why under **The reason in your own words**.
7. Choose **Remove from this device**. The phone asks **Remove this entry?** Choose **Remove from
   this device** again.
8. Confirm it is you with your face, fingerprint or passcode. The phone asks **Confirm it is you to
   delete unsent entries**.

## When a task or the handover changed first

The phone does not have a separate conflict review. If a colleague changed a task or the handover
before your change reached Thapsus Care, your change is not recorded and shows here with the
reason, for example:

- "This task was already marked done before your change reached Thapsus Care, so your change was
  not recorded. Check the round, and tell the person in charge of this shift if the record is
  wrong."
- **This task changed after you marked it. Check it and mark it again.**
- **The handover changed before your acknowledgement reached Thapsus Care. Open it again, read what
  was added, then acknowledge it.**

Do what it says, then remove the item as above.

## Important

- An entry under **Not saved: needs attention** is not in Thapsus Care, unless the item says
  Thapsus Care has a copy. Until someone records it again, the care is not on the person's record
  (HZ-006).
- Remove it only once it has been recorded again or the person in charge knows. Once removed it
  cannot be got back. The removal and its reason are recorded.
- Never sign out, delete the app, reset the phone to factory settings, or change its passcode, face
  or fingerprint while entries are waiting to upload or need attention: they would be lost. If the
  phone switches off, nothing is lost, but nothing is sent until it is back on (HZ-006, HZ-040).

## Good to know

- If the phone says **Thapsus Care could not keep a copy: only this device has this entry. Tell the
  nurse in charge.**, the phone holds the only copy. Tell them straight away.
- If the list says **Entries the server refused could not be counted and need attention. Thapsus
  Care tries again each time it unlocks.**, lock and unlock the app. If it stays, tell the nurse in
  charge.
- Clock events and training work that were not accepted show in the same list. See
  [How to clock in and out on the phone](clock-in-and-out-on-the-phone.md).
