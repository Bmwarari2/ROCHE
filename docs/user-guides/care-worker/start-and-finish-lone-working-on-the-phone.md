# How to start and finish lone working on the phone

When you work alone, for example on a home visit or a night shift, start a lone-working session.
You then check in on a schedule. If you miss a check-in, the people on call are alerted.

**Who can do this**: anyone who may work alone: care and support workers, team leaders or senior
carers, nurses and clinical leads, Registered Managers, deputy managers, owners, activities,
catering, housekeeping and maintenance staff, and agency or bank workers. Your organisation must
have switched lone working on.

**Register rows**: SCHED-012, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Go to **Shifts** at the bottom of the screen and choose **Open lone working**.
2. Check **Where:** shows the right location: the location of your shift, or your only work
   location.
3. Under **Kind of work**, choose **Home visit**, **Night shift** or **Other work**.
4. Under **How long**, choose **2 hours**, **4 hours** or **8 hours**.
5. Under **Check in every**, choose **30 minutes** or **60 minutes**. Your choice says "chosen".
6. Choose **Start lone working**. The screen shows when the next check-in is due, for example
   "Next check-in due at 10:30", and "Session until 14:00".
7. Check in each time one is due. See
   [How to check in while lone working on the phone](check-in-while-lone-working-on-the-phone.md).
8. When you are no longer alone, choose **Finish lone working**. Thapsus Care asks
   **Finish lone working?** and says **Nobody will expect your check-ins after this.** Choose
   **Finish lone working** again, or **Keep lone working** to stay on.

## Important

- **A session started offline does not protect you yet.** Until it reaches Thapsus Care, the screen
  says **This session has not reached Thapsus Care yet, so nobody is told if you miss a check-in
  until it does.** Connect as soon as you can. **Ask for help** still raises the alarm once the
  phone connects. If you are in danger, call 999.
- **If the start was refused**, the screen says lone working did not start, and the reason is under
  **Not saved: needs attention**. Nobody is told if you miss a check-in, but **Ask for help** still
  raises the alarm. If the reason is a length or check-in time outside your organisation's limits,
  start again with other choices. Otherwise ask the person in charge.
- If the screen says **This phone has not reached Thapsus Care since it opened, so it cannot tell
  whether you are in a session.**, you can still start. Thapsus Care checks it when the phone
  connects.
- If it says **This phone cannot tell where you are working, so it cannot record this. Ask the
  person in charge of your shift.**, nothing was started. This happens when there is no shift now
  and you work at more than one location.
- If it says **Lone working is not switched on for your organisation.**, you cannot start a
  session. Agree another way to keep in touch with the person in charge.
- At the end of the session time, finish or check in to stay on. If you do neither, the people on
  call are alerted.
- Only one session at a time. Thapsus Care keeps no address or place name, only your work location.
  The phone never sends where you are, so the people on call see no location for you.
