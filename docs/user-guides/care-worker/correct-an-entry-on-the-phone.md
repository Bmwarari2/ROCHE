# How to correct an entry on the phone

Put right a note, chart entry or body map mark that was recorded wrongly. Entries cannot be changed
or deleted: your correction is added, and the original stays in the record, marked as corrected.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers and agency or bank workers (for the people assigned
to them), team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy
managers.

**Register rows**: MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Today** or **People** and choose the person.
2. Scroll to **Recent entries** and find the entry.
3. Choose **Correct this entry** under it.
4. Under **Correcting an earlier entry**, check the entry you are correcting.
5. Under **Why are you correcting it?**, say why, for example "Wrong amount recorded".
6. Put the entry right. A note starts with the words of the original, so change only what was wrong.
   A chart entry or body map mark is filled in again in full.
7. Under **When did this happen?**, the time of the original is chosen for you, for example "Same
   time as the original entry, 08/11/2026 at 14:30". Change it only if the time was wrong.
8. Choose **Save**. The correction shows "Corrects an earlier entry. Reason: Wrong amount recorded"
   and **Waiting to upload** until it reaches Thapsus Care.

## Important

- The correction is on this phone only until it is sent. Until then, colleagues still see the
  original as it was (HZ-006).
- Correct an entry only for the person it is about. If you recorded something for the wrong person,
  tell the person in charge.
- The phone holds only the last few days of entries. To correct an older entry, use Thapsus Care on
  the web.

## Good to know

- Who corrected it, when and why stay in the record's history. See
  [How to see the history of a record](../all-staff/view-a-records-history.md).
- **Correct this entry** shows only where your role lets you record for the person.
