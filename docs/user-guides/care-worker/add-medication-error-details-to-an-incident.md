# How to add medication error details to an incident

A medication error or near miss is first recorded as a medication incident. Its medication error
details say what went wrong with the medicine, what was done, and what is still to do.

**Who can do this**: the person who raised the incident, the member who added the details, and
managers of incidents (nurses and managers). The incident must be a medication incident, still
open, and one you can see in full.

**Register row**: MEDS-012.

## Steps

1. Record the incident first, with the type **Medication incident** (see
   [How to record an incident](record-an-incident.md)).
2. Open the incident and choose **Medication error details**.
3. If several people we support are on the incident, choose **Record the error for** the person
   it was about.
4. Under **Record the details**, fill in what you know:
   - **What went wrong?**: choose all that apply, for example **Dose omitted**.
   - **At which stage did it go wrong?**, **Which medicine?** and **Which dose record?**: choose
     from the lists, or leave **Not known**.
   - **Did it reach the person?**, **Was a controlled drug involved?** and **Could it be
     deliberate or repeated?**: answer **Yes**, **No** or **Not sure**.
   - **What was done straight away**.
   - Under **Clinical advice**: **Who was told**, **When they were told** and **Their advice**.
   - **Root cause**, once it is known. Leave it empty until then.
   - Under **Controlled drug report**: **Date reported** and **Reference given**, when a
     controlled drug was or may have been involved.
5. Choose **Record the details**. To change them later, choose **Save the changes**.
6. Read **What is still to do**.

## What is still to do

Thapsus Care lists what follows from the details, in this order, and sends none of it. Each one
goes from the list once what answers it is recorded.

- **Get clinical advice**: when it reached or may have reached the person, or a dose was omitted.
  Tell a clinician and record who, when and their advice. If the person is unwell, call 999.
- **Report to the Controlled Drugs Accountable Officer**: when a controlled drug was or may have
  been involved, until the date and reference are recorded.
- **Consider safeguarding**: for example when it could be deliberate or repeated, or a dose was
  omitted. It goes once the incident's own safeguarding question is answered. A person decides;
  nothing is referred automatically.
- **How CQC is decided**: information only. A medication error is notifiable only through what it
  caused or shows, which the incident's own questions decide.

## Important

- **An omitted dose always reaches the person, by its absence.** Answer **Yes** or **Not sure**,
  never **No** (HZ-003).
- An answer left empty is sent as not answered, never as "no".
- Severity, harm and notifications stay on the incident's own questions.
- If the medicine has a controlled drug schedule, Thapsus Care answers **Was a controlled drug
  involved?** with yes, and says why.
- A closed incident shows its details but they cannot be changed. If you cannot see everyone on
  the incident, the details are not shown to you (HZ-134).
- The error categories follow the NRLS. The manager sees trends in **Medicines reports**.
