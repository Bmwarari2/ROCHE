# How to answer a staff survey

Have your say in an anonymous staff survey.

**Who can do this**: any member of staff, except whoever wrote the survey and the managers who run
surveys at that home.

**Register row**: SCHED-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Wellbeing** in the menu. Under **Surveys for you**, choose the survey. Each shows the day
   to answer by.
2. Read **Your answers are not linked to you**: what is recorded and what is not.
3. Answer the questions. For a 1 to 5 question, 1 is the lowest and 5 the highest. A written answer
   can be up to 2,000 characters.
4. Choose **Send my answers**. The survey then shows **Answered**.

## Good to know

- Every question is optional, but answer at least one.
- Thapsus Care records that you took part, so you are not asked again, and your answers with the
  week the survey closed. It never records your name with your answers or when you answered.
- Results are shown only after the survey closes and only when enough people answered.
- Your written answers are shown to managers as you wrote them. Leave out names and details that
  would show who you are, if you want to stay anonymous.
- You can answer each survey once.
