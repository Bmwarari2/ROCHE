# How to record a missing person

Record that a person we support is missing, or has left without agreement, so the police steps and
the search log start straight away.

**Who can do this**: anyone who records incidents (care workers, agency and bank workers, senior
carers, nurses, managers and the owner). The police steps in Harbour are for nurses and managers.

**Register rows**: SAFE-015, SAFE-001.

## First

- **Tell the person in charge at once and start searching, as your missing person procedure
  says.** If the person is at immediate risk, call the police now. Harbour never calls the police.

## Steps

1. From the person's record, choose **Record an incident** (see
   [How to record an incident](record-an-incident.md)).
2. Under **What kind of incident was it?**, choose **Missing person**, or **Left without
   agreement** if they chose to leave.
3. Under **When did it happen?**, choose **Just now**, or **Earlier** and enter when they were
   noticed missing.
4. Under **What happened**, write when and where they were last seen, what they were wearing, and
   who is searching where.
5. Answer **Were the emergency services called?**. If you do not know, ask the person in charge.
6. Choose **Record the incident**.

## What happens next

- Harbour drafts **Missing person: Herbert Protocol profile to the police** on the incident. Its
  time is Harbour's target, not a legal deadline: it is done as soon as possible all the same.
- It sets urgent tasks for nurses and managers, **Give the police the Herbert Protocol profile**
  and **Keep the missing person search log**, and a task **Check welfare on return** for when the
  person comes back.

## Good to know

- The Herbert Protocol profile (photograph, description, places they may go, routines), the
  missing person timeline (last seen, search, sightings, police reference, found, welfare check,
  debrief) and the reminder to call the police have **no web screen yet**. Keep the search log as
  your procedure says, and give the police the profile your home keeps.
- If the person is under a Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards authorisation or the Mental Health
  Act, a manager may need to notify the Care Quality Commission. Harbour never notifies anyone
  itself.
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-127](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-127-a-missing-persons-escalation-delayed-or-the-herbert-protocol-profile-out-of-date).
