# How to record a missing person

Record that a person we support is missing, or has left without agreement, so the police call
and the search log start straight away.

**Who can do this**: anyone who records incidents: care and support workers, agency or bank
workers, team leaders or senior carers, care coordinators, nurses and clinical leads, deputy
managers, the Registered Manager and the owner, for people they can see in Thapsus Care. Staff in
activities, catering, housekeeping and maintenance, and administrators or receptionists, can record
the incident but cannot open the missing person episode: tell the person in charge.

**Register rows**: SAFE-015, SAFE-001.

## First

- **Tell the person in charge at once and start searching, as your missing person procedure
  says.** If the person is at immediate risk, call the police now. Thapsus Care never calls the police.

## Steps

1. From the person's record, choose **Record an incident** (see
   [How to record an incident](record-an-incident.md)).
2. Under **What kind of incident was it?**, choose **Missing person**. Choose it also when they
   left without agreement and nobody knows where they are: only a **Missing person** incident can
   open the missing person episode and the police call target at present.
3. Under **When did it happen?**, choose **Just now**, or **Earlier** and enter when they were
   noticed missing.
4. Under **What happened**, write when and where they were last seen, what they were wearing, and
   who is searching where.
5. Answer **Were the emergency services called?**. If you do not know, ask the person in charge.
6. Choose **Record the incident**.

## What happens next

- Open the missing person episode straight away from the **Missing person** or **Left without
  agreement** incident: choose **Record the missing person episode for** the person affected. The episode shows the latest time to call the police and
  keeps the search log (see
  [How to keep a missing person's timeline](keep-a-missing-person-timeline.md)). If you cannot
  see the button, tell the person in charge.
- Thapsus Care drafts **Missing person: Herbert Protocol profile to the police** on the incident.
  Its time is Thapsus Care's target, not a legal deadline: do it as soon as possible all the
  same.
- It sets urgent tasks for nurses, deputy managers and the Registered Manager: "Give the police
  the Herbert Protocol profile" and "Keep the missing person search log". It also sets "Check
  welfare on return" for when the person comes back.

## Good to know

- If you are worried, call the police straight away. The time on the episode is your
  organisation's target, never a reason to wait.
- The person's Herbert Protocol profile (what they look like, places they may go, routines) is on
  their record, under **Herbert Protocol**. Anyone who can read incidents can read it.
- If the person is under a Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) authorisation or the Mental
  Health Act, or the police are involved, a notification to the Care Quality Commission is
  needed: the Registered Manager or the owner submits it. Thapsus Care never notifies anyone
  itself.
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-127](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-127-a-missing-persons-escalation-delayed-or-the-herbert-protocol-profile-out-of-date).
