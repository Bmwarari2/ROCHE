# How to keep a wellbeing check-in

Note how you are feeling, for yourself, and choose whether to share it with your manager.

**Who can do this**: any member of staff.

**Register row**: SCHED-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Wellbeing** in the menu and find **Your check-ins**.
2. Choose **Where do you work?**.
3. Under **How are you?**, choose the word closest to how you feel, from **Struggling** to **Very
   good**.
4. Add a **Note (optional)** if you want to remember something, up to 1,000 characters.
5. Tick **Share this check-in with my manager** only if you want them to see it.
6. Choose **Save check-in**. It shows under **Earlier check-ins** as **Private** or **Shared with
   your manager**.
7. To change that later, choose **Share with my manager** or **Stop sharing** under the check-in.

## Good to know

- A check-in is yours. It stays private unless you share it, and you can stop sharing at any time.
- Your manager sees a shared check-in only while it is shared. It is never a referral or a
  judgement.
- Your organisation's own support details, set by your organisation, show with your check-ins.
