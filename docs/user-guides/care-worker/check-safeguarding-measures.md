# How to check a person's safeguarding measures

See what you must do to keep a person safe while a safeguarding concern about them is open.

**Who can do this**: anyone who reads care plans: care workers, agency and bank workers, senior
carers, nurses, managers and the owner.

**Register row**: SAFE-006.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Safeguarding measures**.
2. Read **Measures in place**. Each measure shows when it was last updated, newest first.
3. Do what the measures say, every time you care for the person.

## What the page can say

- **No protective measures are in place.** There is no open concern with measures for this
  person.
- **"1 open concern has no measures recorded yet. Ask the safeguarding lead what to do."** A
  concern is open but nobody has written what staff must do. Ask the safeguarding lead before you
  give care.

## Good to know

- You see only the measures, never the concern itself, who raised it or why. The concern is with
  the safeguarding lead.
- The measures are on the web only. The mobile app does not show them yet.
- If a measure is unclear or cannot be followed, tell the person in charge straight away.
- The rules: [safety specification, safeguarding log](../../specs/safety.md#safeguarding-log-plan-slice-13-safe-006-hz-126)
  and hazard [HZ-126](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-126-a-safeguarding-concern-not-progressed-or-protective-measures-not-visible-to-the-staff-caring-for-the-person).
