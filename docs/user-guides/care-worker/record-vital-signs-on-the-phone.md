# How to record vital signs and read the NEWS2 result on the phone

Record the person's vital signs at their side. The phone works out the NEWS2 score from the
published charts as you type, and Thapsus Care checks it again when the entry is sent.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers and agency or bank workers (for the people assigned
to them), team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy
managers.

**Register rows**: CARE-007, MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Today** or **People** and choose the person. Check their photo and allergies at the top.
2. Under **Record care**, choose **Record a chart**, then **Vital signs**.
3. Enter each reading:
   - **Respiration rate, breaths per minute**
   - **SpO2, %**. The line under it says which SpO2 scale is used, for example **Scored on SpO2
     scale 1**.
   - **Air or oxygen**: choose **Air** or **Oxygen**
   - **Systolic blood pressure, mmHg**
   - **Pulse, beats per minute**
   - **Consciousness**: **Alert**, **New confusion**, **Responds to voice**, **Responds to pain** or
     **Unresponsive**
   - **Temperature, °C**.
4. Read the result under the readings, for example "NEWS2 score 5", with the clinical risk, the
   response and how often to monitor. It shows how each reading was scored and the published bands.
5. Under **When did this happen?**, choose **Now**, or **Earlier** with the day and the time.
6. Choose **Save**. The entry shows **Waiting to upload** on the person's record until it reaches
   Thapsus Care.

## Important

- **Follow your organisation's escalation policy for this score.** Thapsus Care shows the score as
  the published NEWS2 charts give it. It does not diagnose or tell you what treatment to give.
- If a reading needs a response, tell the nurse or senior in charge straight away. Do not wait for
  the phone to send the entry: until it connects, nobody else can see it (HZ-006).
- If the phone says "NEWS2 is not totalled here: this person's SpO2 scale is not on this device.",
  the score is worked out when the entry is sent. Follow your escalation policy for the readings you
  took.
- **Enter every reading to see the NEWS2 score.** means a reading is missing.
- Never switch off or reset the phone, or sign out, while entries are waiting to upload (HZ-006,
  HZ-040).

## Good to know

- To read the published NEWS2 charts, choose **Open the RCP NEWS2 page**.
- On the person's record, the entry shows its NEWS2 score, for example "NEWS2 score 5".
