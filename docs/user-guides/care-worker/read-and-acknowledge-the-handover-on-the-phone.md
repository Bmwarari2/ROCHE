# How to read and acknowledge the shift handover on the phone

The **Handover** tab shows the handover from the shift that has just ended, for each home whose
handover your phone receives. Read it before you start, then record that you have read it. The
phone sends your acknowledgement when it connects.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers, agency or bank workers, team leaders or senior
carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy managers. You see only items about
the people you may see. If you do not see **Handover**, your role does not take part in it.

**Register rows**: CARE-013, COMMS-006, MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Handover** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Read **The handover on this phone** first. It says which handovers the phone holds and when it
   last synced.
3. Under your home's name, check the state of the handover:
   - **Final**: the outgoing staff can no longer add to it.
   - **Not final**: the outgoing staff can still add to it. Check it again before you rely on it.
   - **This shift, not final**: the handover for the shift running now, still being added to.
   - **Not compiled yet**: the phone does not have the handover for the shift that has just ended.
     Ask the person in charge before you rely on the phone.
   - **Cannot tell**: the phone does not know your home's shift pattern. Check with the person in
     charge, or on the web.
4. Read every item. Each says who it is about, their communication needs, what happened (for
   example "Fall, written in a care note" or "Overdue: Reposition") and when. Items marked
   **Added late** were added after the handover was compiled.
5. When you have read them all, choose **I have read this handover**.
6. The phone says **Your acknowledgement is saved on this phone and waiting to upload.** Once it is
   sent it says **You have acknowledged this handover.**
7. If items are added after you acknowledged, they show **New since you acknowledged**. Read them
   and choose **I have read the new items**.

## Important

- **The handover on this phone** is not always the whole handover. Items added since the last
  sync, and items about people you do not support, are not shown (HZ-018, HZ-028). If the phone
  says **This handover holds no items you can see on this phone.**, it does not mean there is
  nothing to hand over. Ask the person in charge.
- Your acknowledgement is on this phone only until it is sent. Until then, Thapsus Care shows that
  you have not read the handover.
- If the person in charge tells you something that is not on the phone, follow what they say and
  tell them the phone did not show it.

## Good to know

- To add something to the next handover from the phone, write a note and choose **Include in
  handover** under **Also mark this note for handover**. See
  [How to write a daily note on the phone](write-a-daily-note-on-the-phone.md).
- On a tablet turned sideways, the homes are listed on the left and the chosen home's handover
  opens on the right.
- The web version: [How to read and acknowledge the shift handover](read-and-acknowledge-the-handover.md).
