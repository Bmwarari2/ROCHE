# How to sign as a controlled drugs witness in the browser

Every movement on the controlled drugs register needs a second person who watched it. On the web,
the witness signs first, signed in as themselves, for what they saw. The person they watched then
chooses that signature when they record it.

**Who can do this**: care workers, senior carers, nurses and managers, with current medicines
competence. Agency or bank workers cannot witness controlled drugs.

**Register row**: MEDS-005.

> **If the page says "Controlled drug recording is not turned on for this home"**, the paper
> controlled drugs register is the record at your home. The page still shows what Thapsus Care
> holds, which may be incomplete. It does not show the forms in these steps, and Thapsus Care will
> not accept a controlled drug entry for this home. Record every receipt, dose given, transfer,
> disposal and stock count in the paper register, with a witness, as your home's policy says. One
> register is the record, so its balance is never split between paper and Thapsus Care (HZ-115;
> decision H-1517).

## Steps

1. Sign in as yourself. Never sign while someone else is signed in, and never let anyone else use
   Thapsus Care while you are signed in.
2. Open the person's record and choose **Controlled drugs**.
3. Under **Register pages**, choose the drug.
4. Watch the movement happen: the drug received, returned to the pharmacy, disposed of or
   destroyed, the correction, or the stock count.
5. Under **Sign as witness**:
   - **What you saw**: for example **Received** or **A stock count**.
   - **How much, in** the page's unit: the quantity you saw. For a correction of the quantity,
     the change, for example -2. For a correction of a detail, 0.
   - **Who you watched record it**: choose the person. Only they can use your signature.
6. Choose **Sign as witness**. If Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is you, use your passkey or a
   code from your authenticator app, then come back to the page and try again.
7. The page says **Signed as witness. The person you watched can use it until** a time. Tell them
   it is ready.

## Important

- **Controlled drugs need two different people.** You cannot sign for yourself, and you are never
  offered as the recorder. Thapsus Care refuses the same person twice (HZ-116).
- **Sign only for what you saw with your own eyes.** The signature names the movement, the
  quantity and the person. Thapsus Care refuses the entry if what is recorded does not match it
  (HZ-116).
- The signature lasts a short time and can be used once. If it runs out, sign again.
- If the page says "Nobody else here can record controlled drugs, so there is no one to sign for.",
  ask the nurse or senior in charge (whoever your home gives medicines record management).
- Giving a controlled drug to the person cannot be recorded on the web. Until controlled drug
  recording is turned on for your home, the paper controlled drugs register is the record, with a
  witness, alongside the paper MAR (medicines administration record). It is turned on for a home
  only once both the web and the phone record controlled drugs there (HZ-115; decision H-1517).
  There is no setting for it on screen: ask your Registered Manager.
- The register is never changed. A mistake is put right with a correction, which you witness the
  same way (HZ-115).
