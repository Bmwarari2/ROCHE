# How to record a chart entry (fluids, food, repositioning, bowel, weight)

**Who can do this**: care workers (for the people assigned to them), senior carers, nurses and
managers.

**Register row**: CARE-010.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Charts**.
2. Under **Chart to record**, choose the chart, for example **Fluid intake** or **Food**.
3. Fill in the entry:
   - **Fluid intake**: the **Amount (ml)**, and the **Thickness given (IDDSI drink level)** if one
     is prescribed. Tick that they refused the drink if they did.
   - **Food**: the **Meal**, **How much was eaten?** and the **Texture given (IDDSI food level)**.
   - **Repositioning**: the **Position now**, and whether the skin was checked.
   - **Bowel**: the **Bristol stool chart** type, or **No bowel movement**. Record "no movement"
     too: three days without one raises a prompt.
   - **Weight**: the **Weight (kg)** and how they were weighed.
4. Leave **When did it happen?** on **Now**, or choose **Earlier**.
5. Choose **Save entry**. Harbour says "Chart entry saved".

## Food and drink levels

- The form shows the prescribed level. Give that level.
- If you choose a different level, Harbour asks for the **Reason for giving a different level**.
  If it is less safe than prescribed, the nurse in charge is alerted.

## Fluid balance

- **Fluid balance** shows today's intake against the target, from 00:00 to 24:00 UK time.
- Drinks offered and refused are not counted in the intake.
- If it says some entries may be duplicates, check them and tell the nurse in charge.

## Good to know

- Chart entries cannot be corrected on the web. Tell the nurse in charge if one is wrong.
- For vital signs and NEWS2, see [How to record vital signs](record-vital-signs.md).
