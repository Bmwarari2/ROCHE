# How to take a training course offline on the phone

Take a course you have downloaded, with or without a connection. Your progress is saved on the
phone and sent to Thapsus Care when it connects.

**Who can do this**: staff who take training in Thapsus Care (see
[How to download a training course to the phone](download-a-training-course-to-the-phone.md)).

**Register rows**: WORK-006, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Choose **Training** at the bottom of the screen, beside **Shifts**. You can also go to
   **Settings** and, under **Training**, choose **Open training courses**.
2. Find the course that says **On this phone** and choose **Open**.
3. The course opens with its name at the top and the line **Your progress is saved on this phone
   and sent to Thapsus Care when there is a connection.**
4. Work through the course. You can leave and come back: it opens where you left off, if the
   course supports it.
5. When you finish, the course says **Finished on this phone, waiting to send**.
6. Connect to the internet. Once your work has been sent and nothing is waiting, the course says
   **Finished**.

## Important

- **Your training counts only once Thapsus Care has it.** Until the phone connects, the course is
  finished on the phone only and is not on your training record. Connect as soon as you can.
- The training list says how much is waiting, for example "2 pieces of work are waiting to send to
  Thapsus Care". Courses with work waiting are at the top of the list.
- A pass or completion adds the course to your training record. A fail does not. A finished course
  cannot be opened again on the phone. To take it again, use **E-learning** on the web: see [How to
  take an e-learning course](take-an-e-learning-course.md).
- If Thapsus Care does not accept your work, for example because the course was withdrawn or
  changed after you downloaded it, the reason shows under **Not saved: needs attention**. Tell your
  training lead.
- If the screen says **The course tried to open something outside it. Only the course itself opens
  here.**, the phone stopped a link the course tried to open. Carry on with the course.
- If it says **This course is not on the phone. Download it again from the training list.**, choose
  **Close the course** and download it again.
- Signing out does not lose finished work that is waiting: it is kept on the phone and sent when you
  sign in again on this phone.
