# How to clock in and out on the web

Record when you start and finish a shift and take a break, and check your timesheet.

**Who can do this**: anyone who clocks their own time: care and support workers, team leaders or
senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers, deputy managers, owners, activities,
catering, housekeeping and maintenance staff, and agency or bank workers.

**Register row**: SCHED-004.

## Steps

1. Go to **My time** in the menu. Under **My shifts**, your shifts from yesterday to tomorrow are
   listed.
2. At the start of a shift, choose **Clock in** on that shift.
3. When you take a break, choose **Start a break**, and **End the break** when you come back.
4. At the end of the shift, choose **Clock out**.
5. Under **My timesheet**, check the entry for the shift: the times, how long you worked and where
   it stands, for example **Waiting for approval** or **Approved**.

## Good to know

- Clocking in checks again that you can work the shift, for example that your training sign-off is
  current. If you cannot, clocking in is refused and the person in charge is told the shift may be
  short. Speak to them.
- If the times have a problem, for example a break started and never ended, it is said in words
  under the entry. Tell your manager so they can correct it.
- A manager approves your entries; you cannot approve your own. Only approved time is paid.
- A corrected entry is shown with **Not counted: a later entry replaced this one.** and the reason.
- Your timesheet shows the last 14 days.
