# How to check in while working alone on the web

Start a lone-working session before you work alone, check in at the times you choose, and ask for
help if you need it.

**Who can do this**: any member of staff who works alone, including care and support workers, team
leaders or senior carers, nurses, agency or bank workers, and housekeeping, catering, activities
and maintenance staff.

**Register row**: SCHED-012. A silent alarm can be raised only from the phone app, not on the web.

## Steps

1. Go to **Lone working** in the menu. Under **Your session**, if it says **Lone working is not
   switched on yet**, your organisation has not named who is told. Ask your Registered Manager.
   Until then, if you are in danger, call 999.
2. Choose **Where are you working?** and **What kind of work?**: **Home visit**, **Night shift** or
   **Other work alone**.
3. Under **How long will you work alone?**, enter the **Hours** and **Minutes** from now.
4. Enter **Check in every (minutes)**. Thapsus Care tells you if this is outside your
   organisation's shortest or longest gap.
5. Choose **Start session**. The page shows when your next check-in is due.
6. Each time, choose **Check in: I am safe** by the time shown.
7. If you need help now, choose **Ask for help now**. It is sent at once, with no question first.
8. When you stop working alone, choose **Finish session**, then **Finish session** again to confirm.

## Important

- If you miss a check-in, you are reminded first. When the grace period ends, an alarm goes to the
  on-call person by email, then to the Registered Manager, and keeps being sent until someone
  acknowledges it. Checking in or finishing ends the alarm.
- **Ask for help now** tells the on-call person straight away. It does not mean help is on its way.
  If you are in danger, call 999.
- If the page says **Your request for help may not have been sent.**, call 999 if you are in danger,
  and press the button again.
- After you finish, no one is told if you stop checking in.
- The page checks again every 60 seconds while it is open. If it says **Not updated since**, check
  your connection, then check in again.
- Your check-ins are kept, with the time you made each one.
