# How to witness a controlled drug on the phone

> Arrives with the phone release from `feat/mobile-meds-cd-offline` (slices 69 and 70); not on main yet.

The witness watches a controlled drug given and signs for it with their own PIN on the giver's
phone.

**Who can do this**: care workers, senior carers, nurses and managers who may witness controlled
drugs, with current medicines competence and a medicines PIN on that phone. You cannot witness a
dose you are giving.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-005.

## Steps

1. Make sure you have a medicines PIN on the giver's phone. If not, set one while there is a
   connection: see [How to set a medicines PIN on the phone](set-a-medicines-pin-on-the-phone.md).
2. Stand with the giver. Check the person, the medicine, the pack and the amount with them.
3. On the giver's phone, under **Controlled drug: two signatures**, check that "Witness:" shows your
   name.
4. Watch the dose given. Then type your PIN in **Witness's PIN** and choose **Witness signs
   now**.
5. The phone says "Witnessed by" with your name, then "Now record it with your PIN." The giver then signs with their
   own PIN.

## Important

- **The witness must be a different person from the giver.** The phone and Thapsus Care both
  refuse a dose where the giver and the witness are the same person (HZ-116).
- **Sign only for what you saw.** Your signature fixes the amount and the time. If the amount
  changes after you sign, the phone asks you to sign again.
- If your PIN does not match, try again. After several wrong PINs the phone makes you wait.
- Thapsus Care checks your signature, your competence and that you could witness for this person
  when the record is sent. Until then the dose is only on the phone (HZ-115, HZ-119).
