# How to witness a controlled drug on the phone

The witness watches a controlled drug given and signs for it with their own medicines PIN, on the
phone of the person giving it.

**Who can do this**: care or support workers, team leaders or senior carers, nurses, deputy
managers, Registered Managers and owners, with current medicines competence and a medicines PIN on
the giver's phone. A care or support worker can witness only for the people assigned to them.
Agency or bank workers cannot witness controlled drugs. You cannot witness a dose you are giving.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-005.

> **Where your home still uses the paper controlled drugs register**, controlled drugs are not
> recorded on the phone: the phone says so at the top of the dose. Witness in the paper register as
> usual (H-1517).

## When a witness is needed

- **At a care home** (residential, nursing, respite or children's home), every controlled drug
  given needs a witness. The phone says **A second member of staff must witness this dose. This
  home does not allow a controlled drug to be recorded without a witness.**
- **Where the home runs only home care, supported living, live-in care, complex care or day
  services**, a lone worker may record a dose with no witness and say why. Thapsus Care flags it for
  a manager to review. Where a home runs both kinds of service, a witness is always needed.
- **When the phone does not know the home's services yet**, a witness is needed. The phone says
  **This phone does not know this home's witness rules yet, so a second member of staff must
  witness.**

## Steps

1. Make sure you have a medicines PIN on the giver's phone. If not, set one while there is a
   connection: see [How to set a medicines PIN on the phone](set-a-medicines-pin-on-the-phone.md).
2. Stand with the giver. Check the person, the medicine, the pack and the **Amount given** with
   them.
3. On the giver's phone, under **Controlled drug: two signatures**, the giver chooses you:
   **Witness:** and your name.
4. Watch the dose given. Then type your PIN in **Witness's PIN** and choose **Witness signs now**.
5. The phone says **Witnessed by** your name, then **Now record it with your PIN.** The giver
   then signs with their own PIN and records the dose straight away.

## Important

- **The witness must be a different person from the giver.** The phone never offers the giver as
  the witness, and Thapsus Care refuses a dose where they are the same person (HZ-116).
- **Sign only for what you saw.** Your signature fixes the amount and the time. If the amount
  changes after you sign, the phone says **The amount changed after the witness signed. The witness
  signs again for what is given.**, and you sign again (HZ-116).
- If your PIN does not match, the phone says **The witness's PIN did not match. Try again.** After
  6 wrong PINs in a row it makes you wait a little before you try again.
- **Offline, the dose is only on the giver's phone** until it is sent. When it arrives, Thapsus
  Care checks your signature, that you could witness controlled drugs for this person and that your
  medicines competence was current, both when you signed and when it arrives. If any check fails,
  the dose is not taken: it shows under **Not saved: needs attention** on the giver's phone, and the
  nurse in charge gets an urgent review (HZ-115, HZ-116, HZ-119).
- Every witnessed dose is recorded, with who did it, on the person's controlled drugs register page.
