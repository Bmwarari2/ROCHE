# How to check in while lone working on the phone

Check in to tell Thapsus Care you are safe. If a check-in is missed, the people on call are alerted
until someone acknowledges the alarm.

**Who can do this**: anyone in a lone-working session (see
[How to start and finish lone working on the phone](start-and-finish-lone-working-on-the-phone.md)).

**Register rows**: SCHED-012, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Go to **Shifts** and choose **Open lone working**.
2. Read the line at the top: "Next check-in due at 10:30", or "Check-in overdue since 10:30. Check
   in now." with a warning icon. When a check-in becomes overdue, the phone also says it aloud if
   you use a screen reader.
3. Choose **Check in**. The phone says, for example, "Checked in at 10:02." The next check-in time
   moves on.
4. Your check-ins show under **This session**, for example "Checked in at 10:02". One not sent yet
   says **Waiting to send**.

## What happens if you miss a check-in

1. As soon as the due time passes, the lone-working screen shows the check-in as overdue.
2. After a short grace period set by your organisation, an alarm is raised. Your organisation's on-call person or role is
   told first, then the Registered Manager. The alarm is sent again until someone acknowledges it.
3. At launch they are told by email and in Thapsus Care on the web. There are no text messages yet,
   and staff phones do not get notifications yet. Someone has to read the email or the screen.
4. A check-in made after the alarm ends it, unless you are overdue again by then. Everyone alerted
   is told.

## Important

- **Offline**: a check-in is saved on the phone with the phone's time and sent when it connects.
  Until then Thapsus Care does not have it. A line says, for example, "1 lone-working action is
  waiting to send."
- **No signal looks the same as being hurt.** Thapsus Care cannot tell them apart, so if your
  check-ins do not arrive by the end of the grace period, the alarm is raised. When they do arrive,
  the people alerted are told you were safe at the time you checked in, not now. If you know you
  will have no signal, tell the person in charge before you go.
- The phone does not remind you of a due check-in while it has no connection. Keep an eye on the
  time yourself.
- If the phone has restarted with no connection, it shows what it last knew, with "Thapsus Care
  last answered at 10:02.", for example.
- If you are in danger, use **Ask for help**, and call 999. See
  [How to ask for help while lone working on the phone](ask-for-help-while-lone-working-on-the-phone.md).
