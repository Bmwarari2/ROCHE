# How to record an activity or a visit

**Who can do this**: care workers (for the people assigned to them), senior carers, nurses and
managers.

**Register row**: CARE-014.

## Record an activity

1. Open the person's record and choose **Activities and visits**.
2. Under **Record an activity**, enter the **Activity**, for example "Music session".
3. Choose group or one to one, and whether they took part.
4. If they took part, say how they engaged.
5. Choose **Save activity**.

## Record a visit

1. Under **Record a visit**, choose the **Type of visit**.
2. Enter the **Visitor's name** and their relationship to the person.
3. Enter when it started and, if you know, when it ended.
4. Answer **Were any restrictions applied?**. If yes, you must say why. The law (Regulation 9A)
   needs the reason every time.
5. Choose **Save visit**.

## Good to know

- Activities and visits cannot be corrected on the web yet.
- A recorded activity can be shared with the family as an update (see
  [How to publish a family update](../senior-carer/publish-a-family-update.md)).
