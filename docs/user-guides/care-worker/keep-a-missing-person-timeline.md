# How to keep a missing person's timeline

Open the missing person episode, see when the police must be called, and record the search until
the person is found.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers, agency or bank workers, team leaders or senior
carers, care coordinators, nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and
the owner, for people they can see in Thapsus Care. The quality and compliance lead can read the
timeline but not add to it. Staff in activities, catering, housekeeping and maintenance, and
administrators or receptionists, can record the incident but cannot open these pages: tell the
person in charge.

**Register row**: SAFE-015.

## First

- **Tell the person in charge at once and start searching, as your missing person procedure
  says.** If the person is at immediate risk, call the police now. Thapsus Care never calls the
  police.

## Open the episode

1. Record the missing person incident first (see
   [How to record a missing person](record-a-missing-person.md)).
2. On the incident, choose **Record the missing person episode**. The button carries the person's
   name.
3. On **Open a missing person episode**, check the name at the top is the right person.
4. Enter **When they were noticed missing**. The police call target starts from this time.
5. Enter **When they were last seen**. Leave it empty if nobody knows.
6. Answer **Does the person's risk assessment say call the police at once?** with **Yes, call the
   police now** or **No**. Check the risk assessment if you are not sure.
7. Choose **Open the episode**. The episode opens, titled with the person's name and "is missing".

Episodes that are open are also listed under **Missing persons** in the menu, still missing first.

## Call the police

The notice at the top of the episode says what to do about the police, in words:

- **Call the police now**: the risk assessment says call at once. Call now.
- "Call the police now if you are worried. You must call by" a time: the latest time to call. It
  is never a reason to wait.
- "The police call is overdue": the time has passed. Call the police now.

When you have called them:

1. Under **Add to the timeline**, choose **What happened**: **Police called**.
2. Enter **When it happened**.
3. Enter the police **Reference**. Thapsus Care will not record the call without it.
4. Choose **Add to the timeline**. The notice changes to "The police have been called" with the
   reference.

## Record the search

1. Under **Add to the timeline**, choose **What happened**, for example **Search started**,
   **Area searched**, **Sighting** or **Note**.
2. Enter **When it happened**.
3. For **Area searched**, write **Where**. For a **Sighting**, write **Where** and, in the
   **Note**, who saw them.
4. Choose **Add to the timeline**. The page says "Added to the timeline" and the entry shows under
   **Timeline and search log**, oldest first.

## When the person is found

1. Choose **What happened**: **Found**, enter **When it happened**, and say in the **Note** where
   and how they were found.
2. Choose **Add to the timeline**. The episode now says **Found**.
3. Tell the person in charge. A nurse or manager then does the welfare check and the debrief and
   closes the episode (see
   [How to close a missing person episode](../nurse/close-a-missing-person-episode.md)).

## Important

- The police call time is your organisation's target, not a legal deadline. Call sooner if you are
  worried.
- The page updates itself every minute and turns overdue at the target time, even if nobody
  refreshes it.
- Opening the episode gives the Registered Manager an urgent task to make sure the police are
  called by the target. If the risk assessment says call at once, every nurse, clinical lead, team
  leader and senior carer at the home is also told straight away in Thapsus Care.
- Record **Police called** as soon as you have called them, before you record **Found**. Once
  they are found you cannot add it. If the police were called but it was not recorded before
  **Found**, write a **Note** with the time of the call and the police reference, then tell the
  Registered Manager straight away. They must answer the incident's police question so that the
  Care Quality Commission notification is drafted. If nobody called the police, write a **Note**
  saying why, and tell the Registered Manager.
- Each of **Last seen**, **Noticed missing**, **Search started**, **Police called** and **Found**
  goes on the timeline once. After that, add a **Note**. Nothing on the timeline can be changed or
  removed.
- Under **Notifications**, the episode links to what the incident needs in the notification
  centre, for example the Herbert Protocol profile to the police. When the police were involved,
  or the person is under a Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) authorisation or the Mental
  Health Act, the page says "A notification to the Care Quality Commission is needed". The
  Registered Manager or the owner checks it, decides and submits it. Thapsus Care never sends
  anything itself.
- Under **Herbert Protocol profile**, the page says whether the person's profile is up to date.
  Choose **Open the Herbert Protocol profile** to read what the police need: what they look like,
  places they may go, and more.
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-127](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-127-a-missing-persons-escalation-delayed-or-the-herbert-protocol-profile-out-of-date).
