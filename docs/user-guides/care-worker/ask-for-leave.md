# How to ask for leave

Ask your manager for annual leave or another kind of leave, and see whether it is approved.

**Who can do this**: any member of staff who clocks in for shifts, including care workers, senior
carers and nurses.

**Register row**: SCHED-010.

## Steps

1. Go to **Absences** in the menu.
2. Under **Ask for leave**, choose the **Kind of leave**: **Annual leave**, **Compassionate leave**,
   **Carer's leave** or **Parental leave**.
3. Enter the **First day** and **Last day** as day, month and year.
4. Choose **Ask for leave**. Your request shows **Waiting for a decision**.
5. Come back later to see whether it is **Approved** or **Declined**, with any reason your manager
   gave.

## Good to know

- Someone else decides your request: you cannot approve your own.
- Carer's leave and parental leave cannot be declined, only postponed as the law allows.
- To report sickness, tell your manager in the way your organisation asks. Sickness is not
  recorded here yet.
- To cancel a request, talk with your manager. Cancelling is not on the web yet.
