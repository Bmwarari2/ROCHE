# How to sign in on the phone

Sign in to the Thapsus Care app once, choose Care mode and unlock it. After the first sync, the
people assigned to you are on the phone, even with no connection.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers, agency or bank workers, team leaders or senior
carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy managers.

**Register rows**: MOB-001, MOB-002, MOB-009.

## Before you start

- The phone needs a screen lock (passcode, PIN or pattern) and Face ID or a fingerprint set up.
- You need an internet connection to sign in and for the first sync.
- The phone signs in with your email address and password, then the code from your authenticator
  app if your account uses one. It does not use passkeys yet. To set up an authenticator app, see
  [How to set up a passkey or an authenticator app](../all-staff/set-up-a-second-sign-in-step.md).

## Steps

1. Open the Thapsus Care app. It shows "Sign in to Thapsus Care".
2. Enter your "Email address" and "Password" from your care provider, then choose "Sign in".
3. If the phone asks for a code, open your authenticator app, enter the 6-digit code under
   "Authenticator code" and choose "Continue". To go back, choose "Start again".
4. The first time, the phone asks you to "Choose how you will use Thapsus Care". Choose "Care".
   (Manage and Family say they are coming in a later release.)
5. When the phone asks, confirm with your face, fingerprint or passcode. This opens the encrypted
   care records on the phone (scrambled so only Thapsus Care can read them).
6. **Today** opens. Under **Sync**, wait until it shows the time of the last successful sync, for
   example "Last successful sync 08/11/2026 at 07:02". Your people then show below it.

## If it does not work

- "We could not sign you in with those details. Check your email address and password, then try
  again." Check both and try again. Ask your manager if you have forgotten your password.
- "That code did not work. Codes change every 30 seconds, so enter the latest one from your
  authenticator app." Enter the newest code.
- **Care mode on the phone is switched off for your organisation until a clinical safety case covers
  the app. Ask your manager.** Use Thapsus Care on the web until your organisation switches it on.
- **Care mode needs a device with Face ID or a fingerprint set up (strong biometrics), so the records
  on it are locked to you. Set one up in Settings, or use a device that has one.** Set one up in the
  phone's own settings, then open the app again.
- "Set a screen lock (passcode, PIN or pattern) on this device to use Thapsus Care. Care records are
  only stored on locked devices." Set a screen lock in the phone's own settings.
- If the sign-in screen shows a card starting **Sign in again to send**, entries from an earlier
  sign-in are still on the phone. See
  [How to send unsent entries after signing in again on the phone](send-unsent-entries-after-signing-in-again-on-the-phone.md).

## Important

- Before you give any care, check the banner at the top and the **Sync** card on **Today**. Until the
  first sync, the phone holds nobody's record. If it says **Not synced yet. Connect to the internet
  to download your people.**, connect and wait (HZ-002).
- The care records on the phone are encrypted, and only you can open them, with your face,
  fingerprint or passcode. See [How to lock and unlock Thapsus Care on the phone](lock-and-unlock-the-phone.md).
- Never use Thapsus Care on a phone that has been rooted or jailbroken. See
  [How to keep the phone safe for care records](keep-the-phone-safe-for-care-records.md).
- Agency or bank workers: your access ends a set time after your shift. Connect before then, so
  everything you recorded is sent.

## Good to know

- To change mode later, go to **More**, then **Settings**, and choose "Change mode". Only Care mode works today.
- Sign in on the web: see [How to sign in](../all-staff/sign-in.md).
