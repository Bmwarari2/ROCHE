# How to clock in and out on the phone

Clock in at the start of your shift, record your breaks and clock out at the end. It works with no
connection: the phone keeps what you did and sends it when it connects.

**Who can do this**: anyone who clocks their own time: care and support workers, team leaders or
senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers, deputy managers, owners, activities,
catering, housekeeping and maintenance staff, and agency or bank workers.

**Register rows**: SCHED-004, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Go to **More** in the tab bar, then choose **Shifts**.
2. Check **Not saved: needs attention** first, if it shows (see "If it was not accepted" below).
3. Under **Clock in and out**, check the line under your state:
   - **For:** and the shift name: the shift under way, or the next one starting within an hour.
   - **Not linked to a shift**: there is no shift now or within the next hour. If you work at only
     one location, the clock-in is still saved for that location, and Thapsus Care flags it for your
     manager to check.
4. Choose **Clock in**. The phone says **Saved on this phone. It is sent to Thapsus Care when there
   is a connection.** The state changes to "Clocked in at 07:02", for example.
5. For a break, choose **Start a break**. It shows "On a break since 10:15". Choose
   **End the break** when you are back.
6. At the end of your shift, choose **Clock out**. It shows **Not clocked in**.

## Important

- **Offline**: the phone saves each clock event with the phone's time and sends it when it
  connects. Until then it is not recorded in Thapsus Care, and a line says, for example, "1 clock
  event is waiting to send. Thapsus Care checks it against your organisation's rules when it
  arrives." Connect as soon as you can, and do not clock in twice.
- **Two times are kept**: the phone's time, which counts as the time you worked, and the time
  Thapsus Care received it. If they are far apart, the event is flagged for your manager to check.
  Do not change the phone's clock.
- **The phone does not decide** whether you may clock in. Thapsus Care checks your organisation's
  rules when the event arrives. If a clock-in is refused, the person in charge is told the shift
  may be short.
- **What is not tracked**: the phone does not take your location, scan a tag or check that you are
  at work when you clock in. It sends only which work location and shift the event is for. Nothing
  is tracked between clock events.
- If the phone says **This phone cannot tell where you are working, so it cannot record this. Ask
  the person in charge of your shift.**, nothing was recorded. This happens when there is no shift
  now and you work at more than one location. Tell the person in charge your times.
- If it says **Not saved on this phone. Try again.**, nothing was saved. Try again.
- **Agency or bank workers**: your access ends a set time after your shift. Connect before then
  so your clock events are sent. Events sent too long after the shift are refused.

## If it was not accepted

- A clock event Thapsus Care did not accept shows under **Not saved: needs attention**, with
  **Reason:** in Thapsus Care's own words. It stays on the phone until you remove it.
- Tell the person in charge, so your time can be put right. Then choose **Remove from this
  device**, say why under **Why are you removing this entry?** and confirm.
- Clock events cannot be changed once sent. Your manager corrects your timesheet on the web.
