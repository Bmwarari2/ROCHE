# How to print the MAR chart

A printed MAR (medicines administration record) chart is a copy of one month of the person's
medicines record, for example for a hospital visit or to keep with the downtime pack. It is a
snapshot, not the live record. The live record is the eMAR, the electronic MAR in Thapsus Care.

**Who can do this**: anyone who can open the person's **Medicines** tab: staff who give medicines,
nurses, managers, and roles that read care plans.

**Register row**: MEDS-014.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Medicines**.
2. Under **Print the MAR chart**, choose the **Month**. You can choose one of the last 12 months.
3. Choose **Download the MAR chart (PDF)**. The PDF downloads to your device.
4. If the page says "The MAR chart could not be made just now. Try again in a moment.", try again
   in a moment.

## What the PDF shows

- On every page: the person's name, date of birth and NHS number, the month, "Page n of m", and
  "Printed from the eMAR at … This is not the live record: check the eMAR before giving or
  holding any medicine."
- **Allergies** first, most severe first, then communication needs.
- A key to the codes: G given, R refused, N not given, W withheld, U not available, S taken by
  the person themselves, F given by family.
- Each medicine with its directions, prescriber, start and end, and every dose by day with the
  time, amount and the giver's initials, then the when-required doses.
- **Staff initials on this chart**: who each set of initials is.

## Important

- **A printed MAR is not the live record.** Doses recorded, changed or stopped after printing are
  not on it. Check the eMAR before giving or holding any medicine (HZ-122).
- **"Allergy status not recorded. Find out before giving any medicine."** means nobody has recorded
  whether the person has allergies. It never means none (HZ-007).
- "no outcome recorded" means the dose's time passed and nothing was recorded on the eMAR when the
  chart was printed. "not yet due" means its time had not come.
- A corrected dose shows the correction only. The struck-through original is in the eMAR history.
- The downloaded file stays on your device. Keep, share and destroy it as your home's policy says:
  it holds personal data.
- Every print is recorded with who printed which month.
