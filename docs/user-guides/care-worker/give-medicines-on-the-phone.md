# How to give medicines on the phone

The medicines round on the phone lists the doses due, time by time, and records each one, with or
without a connection.

**Who can do this**: care or support workers, team leaders or senior carers, nurses, deputy
managers, Registered Managers and owners, and agency or bank workers. A care worker or agency
worker sees only the people assigned to them. The **Medicines** tab shows only for staff who give
medicines.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002, MEDS-003.

## Steps

1. Check the sync banner at the top of the screen before you rely on the round. If it says
   "Offline", "Sync failed" or "Data may be out of date. Check important details before you act.",
   the round may not show a dose another phone or the web has recorded, or a medicine stopped since
   the last sync. Connect to the internet if you can, and check anything important with the nurse
   in charge.
2. Go to the **Medicines** tab. The round shows the doses from the start of this hour to six hours
   ahead, under each date and time, for example "07/11/2026 at 08:00". Earlier times come first,
   so a late dose from earlier, including yesterday, is at the top.
3. Check each dose's status, shown as a word: "Not due yet", "Due now", "Late: no outcome
   recorded" or "Outcome recorded". A dose marked **Time-critical** must be given on time.
4. Choose a dose to open the person's medicines. Check the top: their photo, name, date of birth
   and allergies.
5. Under **Doses due** or **When required**, choose the medicine. Each one shows the person's
   allergies beside it.
   - A when-required medicine shows when it was last given, for example **Last given**
     07/11/2026 06:10, how much has been given against the most allowed, for example
     2 of 4 **given in the last 24 hours**, and **Not before** a time when it is too soon.
6. Read the allergy line beside the medicine and compare it with the medicine yourself.
7. Under **What happened**, choose **Given**, **Refused**, **Not given**, **Not available** or
   **Withheld**.
   - For anything but **Given**, choose the **Reason**. For **Other** or **Withheld**, also fill
     in **What happened, in words**.
8. For **Given**, do the checks under **Before you give it**:
   - Look at the photo, name and date of birth, then tick **I have checked their photo, name and
     date of birth**.
   - Choose **Scan the pack** and scan the barcode (see
     [How to scan a medicine pack on the phone](scan-a-medicine-pack-on-the-phone.md)).
   - Check **Amount given**. It shows the prescribed amount when the prescription gives one amount.
     Change it only to what was actually given.
9. Give the medicine, then choose **Record dose**. The phone says **Recorded on this phone. It
   will be sent when there is a connection.**
10. To leave without saving, choose **Stop without recording**. Nothing you chose is saved.

If something stops the record, the phone says what, in words, above **Record dose**, for example
**Too soon after the last dose. Do not give it now: the nurse in charge decides.** Nothing is saved
until it is put right.

## Important

- **Thapsus Care never decides for you.** It shows the medicine and the person's allergies side by
  side; you compare them and you decide. It never chooses a dose, says whether a medicine is safe
  or suggests what to do (HZ-007).
- **Not recorded is not none.** **Allergies not recorded. Check with the nurse in charge before
  giving any medicine.** and **Allergies are recorded but are not on this phone yet. Check with the
  nurse in charge before giving any medicine.** both mean check before giving anything (HZ-007).
- **A dose given offline is not on Thapsus Care yet.** The phone keeps it at once and sends it at
  the next connection. Until then it is only on this phone: the office, the web and other phones do
  not see it, and the person's list shows **Recorded on this phone, not sent yet**. Do not switch
  off, reset or hand in the phone with records waiting (HZ-004, HZ-006).
- **If Thapsus Care does not take a dose** when it is sent, it leaves the round and shows under
  **Not saved: needs attention** at the top of the screen. Thapsus Care keeps a copy and sends the
  nurse in charge an urgent review. Tell the nurse in charge straight away as well: the dose may
  already have been given (HZ-006).
- **When-required medicines**: the phone will not record a dose before the **Not before**
  time, and says **Too soon after the last dose. Do not give it now: the nurse in charge decides.**
  When the most allowed in 24 hours has been given, it will not record another and says **This
  medicine may not be given from this entry now. Check the MAR with the nurse in charge.** When the
  last dose may be older than the records the phone holds, it says **The last dose may be older
  than the records on this phone. Check it with the nurse in charge before giving.** and will not
  record it. Ask the nurse in charge. Only the nurse in charge decides on a dose inside the minimum
  interval, on the web, with a reason; nobody can give more than the most allowed in 24 hours. The
  phone does not compare the **Amount given** with what is left of the most allowed in 24 hours:
  check it against the 24-hour line yourself. Thapsus Care refuses an amount over it when the dose
  is sent, which is after it has been given (HZ-004).
- **The phone counts only what it holds.** **Last given** and the 24-hour total come from the
  records on this phone. A dose given on another phone or on the web since the last sync is not
  counted until it arrives, so check with your colleagues before a when-required dose (HZ-028).
- **When the round will not open**, the phone says why instead of showing doses: it has not
  finished syncing at least once, it has not read your organisation's dose windows, or its safety
  checks could not run. Connect to the internet and wait; if it stays, tell your manager (HZ-004).
- **A rooted or jailbroken phone cannot record medicines.** The phone says **This device may be
  rooted or jailbroken, so it is not safe for care records. Medicines features stay switched off.
  Tell your manager.** Use another phone.
- If the round says the phone could not read some medicines, they are not on the round on this
  phone. Record them in Thapsus Care on the web and tell the nurse in charge (HZ-003).
- A time-critical dose sends a reminder when its window opens: **A time-critical medicine is due
  now. Open the medicines round.** It names no one and no medicine. The round, not the reminder,
  says what is due (HZ-003).
- Thapsus Care checks every dose again when it arrives, including your medicines competence. Each
  dose is recorded, with who did it (HZ-119).
- **Controlled drugs** need two signatures and are given by staff who may record controlled drugs:
  see [How to give a controlled drug on the phone](../nurse/give-a-controlled-drug-on-the-phone.md).
  Anyone else sees **You cannot give or record controlled drugs.** at the top of the dose.
- Not on the phone yet: wristband scans (the photo check only for now), doses taken by the person
  themselves or given by family, a witness for medicines that are not controlled drugs, and giving
  a dose early with a reason. Use the web for these.
