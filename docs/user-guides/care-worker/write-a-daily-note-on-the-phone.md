# How to write a daily note on the phone

Write what happened for the person, in plain words. The phone keeps the note and sends it when it
connects, so you can write it at the person's side with no connection.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers and agency or bank workers (for the people assigned
to them), team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy
managers.

**Register rows**: CARE-009, MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Today** or **People** and choose the person. Check their photo and allergies at the top.
2. Under **Record care**, choose **Write a note**.
3. Under **What is the note about?**, choose one, for example **Eating and drinking**, **Mood and
   wellbeing** or **Night**.
4. Under **Note**, write what happened, in plain words.
5. If the handover needs to know, under **Also mark this note for handover** choose **A fall**,
   **A refusal of care** or **Include in handover**. Choose it again to take it off.
6. Under **When did this happen?**, choose **Now**, or **Earlier**. For **Earlier**, choose the day
   under **Which day?** and enter the time under **Time, 24-hour clock**, for example 14:30. The
   phone shows when it will be recorded, for example "This will be recorded as 08/11/2026 at
   14:30."
7. Choose **Save**. You go back to the person's record.
8. Under **Recent entries**, the note shows **Waiting to upload** until it reaches Thapsus Care,
   then **Saved**.

## Important

- **Waiting to upload** means the note is saved on this phone but is not yet in Thapsus Care.
  Colleagues on other phones or the web cannot see it until the phone connects and sends it. If
  something is urgent, tell the person in charge as well (HZ-006).
- Never switch off or reset the phone, delete the app or sign out while notes are waiting to
  upload. They would be lost (HZ-006, HZ-040).
- If the note is not accepted, it shows under **Not saved: needs attention** with the reason. See
  [How to deal with an entry that was not saved on the phone](deal-with-an-entry-not-saved-on-the-phone.md).
- If you see **The entry could not be saved on this device. Try again.**, nothing was saved. Choose
  **Save** again.
- Notes cannot be changed once written. To put one right, see
  [How to correct an entry on the phone](correct-an-entry-on-the-phone.md).

## Good to know

- A note written later than the time it describes is marked **Late entry**. If the phone's clock was
  wrong, it is marked **Device clock was wrong**. Do not change the phone's clock.
- If you see **You can read this record but cannot record in it. If something needs recording, tell
  the person in charge of this shift or your manager.**, your role does not let you record for this
  person.
- A note does not complete a round task. On the round, choose **Done** or **Not done**. See
  [How to do a round on the phone](do-a-round-on-the-phone.md).
