# How to check the person's details before you give care

The top of every page of a person's record shows what you must check before you give care or record
anything.

**Who this is for**: everyone who gives care.

**Register rows**: PEOP-001, PEOP-002, CARE-015.

## Steps

1. Go to **Residents** in the menu (your home may call it **Clients** or **People we support**) and
   choose the person.
2. Check the **photo**, **name**, **date of birth** and **NHS number** match the person in front of
   you. If there is no photo, ask a colleague who knows them.
3. Read **Allergies**.
4. Read **Resuscitation**.
5. Read **Food texture (IDDSI)** and **Drink thickness (IDDSI)**.
6. Read **Communication needs**, for example "Needs information in large print", and use them.
7. Check the **Checked at** time. Choose **Check again** if the page has been open a while.

## What the words mean

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| No known allergies | Someone has recorded that they have no allergies. |
| 2 allergies | Read the list under **Allergies**. |
| Allergies not recorded | Nobody has recorded it. **Do not assume there are none.** Ask the nurse in charge. |
| Attempt CPR / Do not attempt CPR | The decision in force, and who made it. |
| CPR decision not recorded | No ReSPECT plan or DNACPR decision is in Harbour. Check the paper record and tell the nurse in charge. |
| CPR records disagree | Check the signed documents and tell the nurse in charge **now**. |
| Review overdue | The decision still stands until it is reviewed. |
| No level prescribed | No special food or drink level is needed. |
| Not recorded (food or drink) | Check with the nurse in charge before giving any food or drink. |
| Communication needs: Not recorded | Nobody has recorded them yet. It does not mean there are none. |
| None identified | Someone has checked and recorded there are none. |

## Good to know

- If Harbour says "This record could not be read safely", use the paper record and tell the person
  in charge.
- If you see "You can read this record but cannot record in it", tell the person in charge of the
  shift what needs recording.
- Adding people, and changing their details, allergies or photo, is not on the web yet.
