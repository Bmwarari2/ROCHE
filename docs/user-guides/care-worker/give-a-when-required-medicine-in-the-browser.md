# How to give a when-required (PRN) medicine in the browser

When-required medicines are given only when the person needs them. The person's round on the web
shows when each was last given and when the next may be given. The eMAR is the electronic MAR
(medicines administration record) in Thapsus Care.

**Who can do this**: care workers, senior carers, nurses and managers who give medicines, and
agency or bank workers. A care worker or agency worker sees only the people assigned to them.

**Register rows**: MEDS-003, MEDS-002.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines** and then **Open the medicines round**. Or go to
   **Medicines round** in the menu and choose **Open Ada's round** (with the person's name).
2. Under **When-required medicines**, find the medicine. Read:
   - **Last given**: when the last dose was recorded, or **Not given on the eMAR yet**.
   - **In the last 24 hours**: the amount given against the most allowed, for example "2 given of
     8 at most, in tablet".
   - **Next may be given**: **Now, if needed**, **Not before** a time, or **Not now: the most
     allowed in 24 hours has been given**.
3. Decide with the person whether they need it, as the prescription and your home's PRN protocol
   say.
4. Choose **Record a dose of** the medicine, for example **Record a dose of Examplomol 500 mg
   tablets**.
5. On **Record a dose**, check the person, their allergies and **For:** (what the medicine is for).
6. Under **What happened**, choose the outcome. For a dose taken, check **Amount given**.
7. Fill in **Why it is given before the minimum interval (if it is)** only if it applies, and
   speak to the nurse or senior in charge (whoever your home gives medicines record management) first. If a reason is needed, Thapsus Care asks for it.
8. Choose **Record the dose**.

## Important

- **Thapsus Care never decides whether a dose is needed or safe, and never decides an interaction
  or allergy match.** It shows the prescription's figures and the doses recorded. The nurse in
  charge decides when it is too soon (HZ-117).
- **"Not given on the eMAR yet" does not mean never given.** A dose given on paper, or on a phone
  that has not sent its records, may not be shown. "On a device that has not synced yet" beside
  the last dose means it came from a device whose records may not all have arrived. Ask the team
  before giving another dose (HZ-004).
- The 24-hour total is an amount in the medicine's unit, never a count of doses.
- **A dose that would go over the most allowed in 24 hours is refused.** There is no override:
  tell the nurse or senior in charge, who contacts the prescriber. Only a dose that has already happened
  (taken by the person themselves or given by family) is recorded over the maximum, and it is
  escalated to the nurse or senior in charge as an urgent task (HZ-117).
- Check the allergies above the form against the medicine yourself every time (HZ-007).
- The PRN protocol's text and the check on how the dose worked are not on this screen yet. Read
  the person's PRN protocol in their care plan before you give it, and afterwards record how it
  worked yourself, as your home's protocol says, because Thapsus Care does not ask you yet
  (HZ-117, H-1519).
