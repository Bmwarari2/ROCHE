# How to set a medicines PIN on the phone

A medicines PIN lets you sign for a controlled drug on one phone without a connection, as the
person giving it or as the witness.

**Who can do this**: staff who may give or witness controlled drugs: care or support workers (as
witnesses), team leaders or senior carers, nurses, deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners.
Each person sets their own PIN. Agency or bank workers cannot set one: they cannot give or witness
controlled drugs.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-005.

> **Until your home's electronic controlled drugs register is turned on**, keep every controlled
> drug record for your home in the paper controlled drugs register, with a witness, and the paper
> MAR. The phone does not show whether it is turned on: ask your manager (H-1517).

## Steps

Do this while the phone has a connection, before the round.

1. Go to the **Medicines** tab and choose **Medicines PINs on this phone**.
2. Check **People with a PIN on this phone**.
3. Hand the phone to the person setting the PIN. They type, themselves:
   - **Their email address** and **Their password**;
   - **New medicines PIN**: at least 6 digits, not all the same and not a run such as 123456;
   - **The PIN again**.
4. They choose **Set PIN on this phone**.
5. When asked, they enter **The 6-digit code from their authenticator app** and choose **Set PIN
   on this phone** again. This is how they confirm it is them.
6. The phone says **PIN set for** their name **on this phone.** Their sign-in ends at once; the
   phone stays signed in as you.

## Important

- **Each person types their own details and PIN.** Never type someone else's password, code or
  PIN, and never share yours. A PIN belongs to one person on one phone (HZ-116).
- Setting a PIN needs a connection. Without one the phone says **No connection. Setting a PIN
  needs one: try again when the phone is online.**
- If the phone says **Thapsus Care did not accept the PIN. Check the person can sign controlled
  drugs here, then try again.**, the person may not give or witness controlled drugs, or this phone
  has been withdrawn. Ask your manager.
- This screen asks for a code from an authenticator app. Set one up first if you have not: see
  [How to set up a passkey or an authenticator app](../all-staff/set-up-a-second-sign-in-step.md).
- Neither PIN is kept when a dose is signed. After 6 wrong PINs in a row the phone makes that
  person wait before they try again, a little longer each time; it never locks them out.
- A new PIN replaces the old one on that phone. Setting a PIN is recorded, with who did it.
