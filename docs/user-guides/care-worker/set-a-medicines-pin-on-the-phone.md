# How to set a medicines PIN on the phone

> Arrives with the phone release from `feat/mobile-meds-cd-offline` (slices 69 and 70); not on main yet.

A medicines PIN lets you sign a controlled drug on this phone without a connection, as the person
giving it or as the witness.

**Who can do this**: staff who give or witness controlled drugs: care workers (as witnesses),
senior carers, nurses and managers. Each person sets their own PIN.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-005.

## Steps

Do this while the phone has a connection, before the round.

1. Go to the **Medicines** tab and choose **Medicines PINs on this phone**.
2. Check **People with a PIN on this phone**.
3. Hand the phone to the person setting the PIN. They type, themselves:
   - **Their email address** and **Their password**;
   - **New medicines PIN**: at least 6 digits, not all the same and not a run such as 123456;
   - **The PIN again**.
4. Choose **Set PIN on this phone**.
5. When asked, they enter **The 6-digit code from their authenticator app** and choose **Set PIN
   on this phone** again.
6. The phone says "PIN set for … on this phone." Their sign-in ends at once; the phone stays
   signed in as you.

## Important

- **Each person types their own details and PIN.** Never type someone else's password, code or
  PIN, and never share yours. A PIN belongs to one person on one phone (HZ-116).
- Setting a PIN needs a connection. If there is none, the phone says so: try again when it is
  online.
- If Thapsus Care does not take the PIN, check the person can sign controlled drugs at this home.
- Neither PIN is kept when you sign a dose. After several wrong PINs the phone makes you wait a
  little before you try again; it never locks you out.
- A new PIN replaces the old one on that phone.
