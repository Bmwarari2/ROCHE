# How to see your shifts on the phone

The Thapsus Care phone app shows your own published shifts for the coming week, even with no
connection.

**Who can do this**: anyone who clocks their own time or works alone: care workers, team leaders,
nurses and clinical leads, Registered Managers, deputies, owners, activities, catering,
housekeeping and maintenance staff, and agency workers. If you do not see **Shifts**, your role
does not include it: ask your manager.

**Register rows**: SCHED-002, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. Open the Thapsus Care app and go to **Shifts** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Check **Not saved: needs attention** first, if it shows. It lists anything Thapsus Care did not
   accept, such as a refused clock-in, with the reason.
3. Scroll to **My shifts**. Each shift shows:
   - its name, or **Shift** if it has none
   - the date and times in UK time, for example "14/11/2026, 07:00 to 15:00"
   - the kind: **Work**, **Sleep-in**, **On call at the workplace**, **On call away from the
     workplace** or **Training**
   - **Under way** while the shift is on.
4. If a shift says **Changed after it was published**, check the new date and times. Ask the
   person in charge if you are not sure.

## Good to know

- Only your own shifts show, never a colleague's. Shifts show once they are published and the phone
  has connected. They run from the shift under way, or the next to start, up to about a week ahead.
  Shifts that have ended leave the list.
- With no connection, the list is what the phone last received. A change made since then shows
  only after the phone connects again.
- If the list says **No shifts for the next week on this phone. Your shifts show here once they are
  published and the phone has connected.**, connect to the internet. If it still says so, ask the
  person in charge: your shifts may not be published yet.
- You cannot ask to swap a shift on the phone yet. Speak to the person in charge.
- To clock in, see [How to clock in and out on the phone](clock-in-and-out-on-the-phone.md).
