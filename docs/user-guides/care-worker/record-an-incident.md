# How to record an incident

Record an accident, near miss or anything else that went wrong, as soon as you can.

**Who can do this**: anyone who works with people we support (care workers, senior carers, nurses,
managers, agency workers) and most other staff. You need to have signed in with your passkey or
authenticator app.

**Register row**: SAFE-001. On the web only; recording with no signal is a mobile app feature that
is still being built.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Record an incident**. If the incident is not about one
   person (for example, a fire door left open), go to **Incidents** in the menu and choose
   **Record an incident** there.
2. If you work at more than one home, answer **Where did it happen?**. From a person's record,
   Harbour uses their home.
3. Choose an answer for **What kind of incident was it?**, for example "Fall" or "Near miss".
4. Under **When did it happen?** choose **Just now**, or **Earlier** and enter the date and time.
   Choose earlier if you are writing it up later. Every legal clock for this incident starts at this
   time, so get it right.
5. Under **What happened**, write what you saw or were told. Write facts, not opinions.
6. Add **What was done straight away** if anything was.
7. For a fall, answer the two questions under **After a fall** (see below).
8. From a person's record, answer **Did the person die?** and describe any **Injuries**.
9. Answer **Were the emergency services called?** and **Was the GP contacted?**. If you do not
   know, ask the person in charge before you answer. Harbour never assumes "no".
10. If you can, choose **How serious was it?** and **Physical harm**. "Not judged yet" is fine: a
    manager reviews it.
11. Choose **Record the incident**. The incident page opens and says "Incident recorded".

## After a fall

Harbour asks two questions. They decide the checks after the fall, so answer them now.

- Did the person hit their head, or can a head injury not be ruled out (for example, nobody saw
  the fall)?
- Is the person taking anticoagulant or antiplatelet medicine (except aspirin on its own)? Check
  the medicines record.

If you do not know, choose **Not sure yet**. Harbour treats "Not sure yet" as "yes": it sets
tasks for urgent medical advice and timed observations. **Get medical advice as your falls
procedure says straight away.** The incident page lists these under **Checks after the fall**, with
urgent medical advice first and marked **Urgent**.

## Good to know

- You cannot delete an incident or change what you wrote on the web. Tell the person in charge if
  something needs correcting.
- You can never close an incident you raised. Another manager must close it.
- Harbour works out which notifications the law needs (for example to the Care Quality Commission)
  as soon as you save. A manager deals with them. You do not need to do anything else.
- The web form does not have a body map. Record marks on the person's **Body map** page as well
  (see [How to mark the body map](mark-the-body-map.md)). Witnesses, staff and visitors cannot be
  added on the web yet. Put them in **What happened**.
- If the page says "You can read this record but cannot record an incident about this person",
  tell the person in charge of the shift what happened. They will record it.
- If Harbour cannot reach the server, your answers stay on the screen. Try again when you are back
  online. A retry never records the incident twice.
