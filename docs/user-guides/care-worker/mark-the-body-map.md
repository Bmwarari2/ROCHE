# How to mark the body map

Record bruises, pressure ulcers, skin tears and other marks on the person's skin.

**Who can do this**: care workers (for the people assigned to them), senior carers, nurses and
managers.

**Register rows**: CARE-012, SAFE-009.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Body map**.
2. Under **View**, choose **Front** or **Back**.
3. Tap the place on the drawing, or choose it under **Body area, as marked on the drawing**. On the
   front view the person faces you: **their right is on your left**.
4. Under **Confirm the side in words**, choose **Left**, **Right** or **Midline**. It must agree
   with the drawing, or Harbour will not save it.
5. Choose the **Type of mark**, for example "Bruise" or "Pressure ulcer".
6. For a pressure ulcer, choose the **Pressure ulcer category** and answer whether it is caused by
   a medical device.
7. For a bruise, answer whether the cause is known.
8. Add the size and a description if you can.
9. Set the time and choose **Save mark**.

## Important

- A pressure ulcer of category 2 or worse, or a bruise whose cause is not known, sets a task for a
  manager to report an incident and work through the safeguarding decision guide. **Tell the nurse
  in charge straight away as well.** When it does, the message after saving says so.
- The category, the medical device question and the bruise question must be answered before the
  mark is saved.

## Good to know

- Current marks are listed under the drawing, newest first.
- Correcting a mark, marking it healed and adding a photo are not on the web yet.
- Marks on the body map are not linked to an incident on the web. Describe injuries in the incident
  too.
