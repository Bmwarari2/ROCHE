# How to record vital signs and read the NEWS2 result

**Who can do this**: care workers (for the people assigned to them), senior carers, nurses and
managers.

**Register row**: CARE-007.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Charts**.
2. Under **Chart to record**, choose **Vital signs**.
3. Enter the **Respiration rate**, **SpO2**, **Systolic blood pressure**, **Pulse** and
   **Temperature**.
4. Choose **Air** or **Oxygen**, and the level of **Consciousness (ACVPU)**.
5. Choose **Save entry**. The **NEWS2 result** appears.

## Read the result

- The score, the clinical risk (for example "Medium") and the **Response**, in the Royal College
  of Physicians' words.
- **How the result was worked out**: the points for each reading.
- The line under it says who was told:
  - "The nurse in charge was told."
  - If it says **no one could be told**, or "Check that the nurse in charge knows", **tell the nurse
    in charge or a manager now**.
- **Your organisation's escalation policy**: follow it.

## Important

- **The score supports your judgement. It never replaces it.** If you are worried about the
  person, tell the nurse in charge whatever the score says.
- Harbour calculates NEWS2 exactly as published (Royal College of Physicians, 2017) and never
  changes a score.
- A reading copied from a paper form is from the time written on it. Check it was escalated on
  paper, and check the person now.
- If the result "cannot be shown", tell the nurse in charge.
