# How to check consent, capacity and DoLS

Before you act on a decision such as bed rails or photographs, check that consent, or a
best-interests decision, is in place.

**Who can do this**: anyone who can read the care plan, including care workers for the people
assigned to them.

**Register rows**: CARE-021, CARE-022. This page is for reading only. Consent, capacity
assessments, best-interests decisions and DoLS requests are recorded through the system by a
nurse or manager; the web forms are not built yet.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Consent, capacity and DoLS**.
2. Under **Consent by decision**, find the decision, for example "Bed rails".
3. Read its status and the reason under it.

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Consent in place | You can act on this decision. |
| Refused | The person said no. Do not act on it. Tell the nurse in charge. |
| No consent recorded | Nobody has asked yet. Do not act on it until it is recorded. |
| No valid consent | For example, it has expired, or the person lacks capacity for this decision and there is no best-interests decision. Tell the nurse in charge. |
| Capacity review overdue | The capacity assessment needs reviewing. |

## The Mental Capacity Act in plain words

- Everyone is assumed to have capacity for each decision unless an assessment of **that decision**
  shows otherwise.
- A person who has capacity decides for themselves, even if you think it is unwise.
- When a person lacks capacity for a decision, it is made in their best interests, or by a
  representative with the authority to make it (for example a registered health and welfare
  attorney).

## DoLS

The **DoLS register** shows each application or authorisation and its state, such as
**Awaiting the outcome**, **In force**, **Ending soon** or **Expired**.

- "Ending soon" starts 28 days before the end. The Registered Manager is reminded.
- If you see **Review after the legal change: not started**, tell the Registered Manager: the
  authorisation needs reviewing after the June 2026 Supreme Court judgment.
- If you see "CQC notification needed but not drafted", tell the Registered Manager.
