# How to mark the body map on the phone

Record a bruise, pressure ulcer, skin tear or other mark on the person's skin, at their side.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers and agency or bank workers (for the people assigned
to them), team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy
managers.

**Register rows**: CARE-012, SAFE-009, MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Today** or **People** and choose the person. Check their photo and allergies at the top.
2. Under **Record care**, choose **Add a body map mark**.
3. Under **Which view?**, choose **Front of the body** or **Back of the body**.
4. Under **Tap where the mark is on the drawing**, tap the place on the drawing, or choose a side
   with the buttons under it. On **Front of the body** the drawing faces you, so the person's left
   is on your right. On **Back of the body** the drawing shows their back, so the person's left is
   on your left. The phone says where you marked, for example "Marked on the drawing: the person's
   left".
5. Under **Body area**, choose the area, for example "Forearm".
6. Under **Which side is the mark on? Choose it in words too.**, choose **The person's left**,
   **The person's right** or **The midline**.
7. Under **Type of mark**, choose the type, for example **Bruise**, **Pressure ulcer** or
   **Skin tear**.
   - For a **Pressure ulcer**, choose the **Pressure ulcer category** and answer **Is it caused by a
     medical device?**
   - For a **Bruise**, answer **Is the cause of the bruise known?**
8. Add a description (optional), for example its size and colour.
9. Under **When did this happen?**, choose **Now**, or **Earlier** with the day and the time.
10. Choose **Save**. The mark shows **Waiting to upload** on the person's record until it reaches
    Thapsus Care.

## Important

- The side on the drawing and the side in words must agree. If they do not, the phone says, for
  example, "The drawing shows the person's left but the person's right was chosen. Check the side
  and try again." Look at the person again before you change either (HZ-019).
- For a pressure ulcer of category 2 or worse (including unstageable and deep tissue injury), or a
  bruise whose cause is not known, the phone says **When this mark uploads, the manager is asked
  to consider an incident report and the safeguarding decision guide.** Tell the person in charge
  now as well: the manager is not told until the phone sends the mark (HZ-006).
- Consider an incident for any injury, including a skin tear, cut or burn, under your
  organisation's policy, even when the phone does not prompt.
- There is no incident form on the phone yet. Record an incident on the web: see
  [How to record an incident](record-an-incident.md).
- Never sign out, delete the app, reset the phone to factory settings, or change its passcode, face
  or fingerprint while entries are waiting to upload or need attention: they would be lost. If the
  phone switches off, nothing is lost, but nothing is sent until it is back on (HZ-006, HZ-040).

## Good to know
