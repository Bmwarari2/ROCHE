# How to scan a medicine pack on the phone

Scanning the pack checks that it is the medicine on the MAR (medicines administration record) and
has not expired, before you give it.

**Who can do this**: anyone who gives medicines on the phone: care or support workers, team
leaders or senior carers, nurses, deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners, and agency or
bank workers.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002.

## Steps

1. While recording a dose as **Given**, under **Before you give it**, choose **Scan the pack**.
2. If the phone asks, choose **Allow the camera**. If you do not allow it, choose below why the
   pack cannot be scanned instead.
3. Point the camera at the pack's barcode. Square (DataMatrix) and striped barcodes both work.
4. Read the result:

| You see | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Pack checked: it is this medicine.** | Carry on. |
| **Stop: this pack is a different medicine. Do not give it.** | Do not give it. Find the right pack, or tell the nurse in charge. |
| **Stop: this pack expired on** a date | Do not give it. Find another pack, or tell the nurse in charge. |
| **This barcode is not in the medicines data on this phone.** | Check the pack against the medicine by hand, then choose why it could not be matched. |
| **That is not a medicine pack barcode Thapsus Care can read.** | Choose **Scan another pack**, or choose why it cannot be scanned. |

5. If the pack cannot be scanned and matched, choose why under **If the pack cannot be scanned
   and matched, say why:**: **The pack has no barcode**, **The barcode will not scan**, **The pack
   is not in the medicines data**, **The person's own supply** or **Other**. For **Other**, fill in
   **Why the pack could not be scanned**.
6. To stop scanning, choose **Cancel**.
7. Before you give it, read the allergy line above the form and compare it with the medicine
   yourself.

## Important

- **A different medicine or an expired pack cannot be overridden.** No reason clears it: the phone
  hides the reasons and will not record the dose until you scan a pack that matches (HZ-113).
- **A match checks the pack, not the person.** It says the pack is the medicine on the MAR. It does
  not check allergies or whether the medicine is right for the person: the phone shows the
  allergies beside the medicine, and you decide (HZ-007).
- **When a scan fails**, check the pack by hand against the MAR: name, strength, form and expiry.
  If you are not sure, do not give it; ask the nurse in charge.
- The phone matches packs against the medicines data it last downloaded. The line under the scan
  button says when that was (**Medicines data for pack scans on this phone from** a date and time)
  and when a newer download last failed. If it says **Medicines data for pack scans has not reached
  this phone yet, so no pack will match. Connect to the internet.**, connect (HZ-113).
- Thapsus Care checks a scanned pack again against the medicines data when the dose is sent. If
  the pack does not match there, the dose is not taken and shows under **Not saved: needs
  attention** (HZ-113, HZ-006).
- The camera only reads the barcode. The phone keeps the barcode's number, batch and expiry with
  the dose, not a picture.
