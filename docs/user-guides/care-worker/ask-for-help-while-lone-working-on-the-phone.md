# How to ask for help while lone working on the phone

**Ask for help** raises an urgent alarm with the people on call. A silent alarm is there for when
someone is with you and you cannot ask openly.

**Who can do this**: anyone who may work alone (see
[How to start and finish lone working on the phone](start-and-finish-lone-working-on-the-phone.md)). You do
not need to be in a session to ask for help.

**Register rows**: SCHED-012, MOB-007.

## Steps

1. If you are in danger, call 999 first.
2. Go to **Shifts** and choose **Open lone working**.
3. Choose **Ask for help**. It is set apart from the other buttons.
4. Read the line under it. It says only what is true:
   - **Your request for help is being sent. If you are in danger, call 999.**
   - **Your request for help has not reached anyone yet: this phone has no connection. If you are
     in danger, call 999.** Move to where there is a signal, or call.
   - **Thapsus Care has your request for help. If you are in danger, call 999.**
   - **Your request for help did not reach anyone. If you are in danger, call 999, or call your
     on-call person now.**
5. Choosing **Ask for help** again sends the alarm again.

## The silent alarm

- Your manager will show you how to raise a silent alarm when you start lone working. It raises an
  urgent alarm with the people on call without anyone near you knowing.
- A silent alarm ends only when someone on call has checked on you and acknowledged it.
- Ask your manager how your organisation responds to a silent alarm.

## Important

- **Thapsus Care never says help is on its way.** The alarm goes to the on-call person or role your
  organisation has named, then the Registered Manager, by email and in Thapsus Care on the web. It is
  sent again until someone acknowledges it. There are no text messages yet, and staff phones do not
  get notifications yet, so it can take time for someone to see it.
- **Offline**: a request for help or a silent alarm waits on the phone until it connects. It has
  not reached anyone until then.
- You cannot ask for help from the lock screen yet: open the app.
- In an emergency, call 999. Then call your on-call person if you can.
