# How to record a medicines fridge or room temperature

Medicines must be stored within their temperature range. Each reading is recorded against the
fridge or the medicines room or cupboard it was taken in.

**Who can do this**: staff who give medicines (care workers, senior carers, nurses and managers,
and agency or bank workers) and staff who manage medicines stock.

**Register row**: MEDS-008.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines stock** in the menu. If Thapsus Care asks, choose your home.
2. Under **Storage and temperatures**, choose the fridge or room, for example "Treatment room
   fridge (fridge)".
3. Under **Record a reading**, enter **Temperature now, in degrees Celsius**, for example 5.4.
4. If the thermometer keeps them, enter **Lowest since the last reading, in degrees Celsius** and
   **Highest since the last reading, in degrees Celsius**.
5. Choose **Record reading**.
6. Read the result: **In range** or **Out of range**, the range that applied, and what happens
   next.

## Important

- **If a reading is out of range, Thapsus Care raises an urgent task for the nurse in charge**,
  due at once. It says "Medicines storage out of range. Check the storage, and ask the pharmacist
  whether the medicines in it can still be used." If nobody completes it, it escalates. A reading
  with a task says **A task was raised for this reading** (HZ-132).
- **Thapsus Care never says whether the medicines can still be used.** Ask the pharmacist.
- Enter the temperature exactly as the thermometer shows it, with up to 1 decimal place. A
  reading at the edge of the range is never rounded into it (HZ-132).
- The range comes from your organisation's settings. Thapsus Care, not you, decides whether a
  reading is in range.
- Readings are never changed. Record a new one if you need to.
- The page lists the latest 100 readings, newest first.
