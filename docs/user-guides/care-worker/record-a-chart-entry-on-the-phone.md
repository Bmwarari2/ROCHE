# How to record a chart entry on the phone (drinks, fluid output, food, repositioning, bowel, weight)

Record a chart entry at the person's side. The phone keeps it and sends it when it connects.

**Who can do this**: care and support workers and agency or bank workers (for the people assigned
to them), team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy
managers.

**Register rows**: CARE-010, MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Today** or **People** and choose the person. Check their photo, allergies and food and
   drink levels at the top.
2. Under **Record care**, choose **Record a chart**.
3. Under **Choose a chart**, choose **Drink**, **Fluid output**, **Food**, **Repositioning**,
   **Bowel movement** or **Weight**. (For **Vital signs**, see
   [How to record vital signs on the phone](record-vital-signs-on-the-phone.md).)
4. Fill in the entry:
   - **Drink**: **Amount in millilitres**, what the drink was (optional), the **IDDSI drink level
     given**, and **Drink offered but refused** if they refused it.
   - **Fluid output**: **Amount in millilitres** and the **Type of output**.
   - **Food**: the **Meal**, **How much was eaten?**, the **IDDSI food level given**, what was
     offered (optional), and **Food offered but refused** if they refused it.
   - **Repositioning**: the **Position**, **Was the skin checked?**, and **Repositioning offered but
     declined** if they declined.
   - **Bowel movement**: the **Bristol stool type**, or **No bowel movement**, and the amount
     (optional).
   - **Weight**: **Weight in kilograms** and **How was the person weighed?**
5. Under **When did this happen?**, choose **Now**, or **Earlier** with the day and the time.
6. Choose **Save**. You go back to the person's record, where the entry shows **Waiting to upload**
   until it reaches Thapsus Care.

## Food and drink levels (IDDSI)

- The person's prescribed level is marked "(prescribed)" in the list, and the line under the
  question says, for example, "Prescribed: Level 6 Soft and bite-sized (blue)".
- If you choose a different level, the phone asks for the **Reason for giving a different level**.
  If the level may not be safe, it says so, and the nurse in charge or a manager is told when the
  entry is sent. Tell them now if you can.
- If the phone says **Food and drink levels are not on this device. Have you checked this level with
  the nurse in charge?**, check with the nurse in charge before you give it, then answer.

## Important

- **Waiting to upload** means the entry is on this phone only. It is not in Thapsus Care, and
  colleagues cannot see it, until the phone connects and sends it (HZ-006).
- Never switch off or reset the phone, delete the app or sign out while entries are waiting to
  upload (HZ-006, HZ-040).
- If an entry is not accepted, it shows under **Not saved: needs attention**. See
  [How to deal with an entry that was not saved on the phone](deal-with-an-entry-not-saved-on-the-phone.md).
- **Some details need checking. Look for the messages below each question.** means nothing was
  saved yet. Answer the questions marked, then choose **Save** again.

## Good to know

- Under **Earlier entries on this chart**, the phone shows the last few entries it holds. It holds
  only the last few days, so this is not the whole chart (HZ-028).
- To put an entry right, see [How to correct an entry on the phone](correct-an-entry-on-the-phone.md).
- The web version: [How to record a chart entry](record-a-chart-entry.md).
