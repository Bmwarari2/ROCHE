# How to read the sync banner on the phone

The banner at the top of every screen says whether the phone is connected, when it last received
the care record, and whether everything you recorded has reached Thapsus Care. **Today** spells it
out in full.

Syncing is when the phone sends what you recorded to Thapsus Care and gets the latest records from
it. It happens on its own whenever Thapsus Care is open and the phone has a connection.

**Who can do this**: everyone who uses Care mode on the phone.

**Register rows**: MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Look at the banner at the top of the screen. Its first word says where the phone is:
   - "Up to date": connected, and everything has been sent. Check the line under it as well.
   - "Syncing": sending and receiving now.
   - "Offline": no connection. You can keep working. What you record is kept on the phone.
   - "Sync failed": the phone could not reach Thapsus Care. Choose "Retry".
   - "Not all saved": connected, but something you recorded was not accepted.
   - "This phone cannot sync": your access covers more homes and people than a phone can hold. Use
     the web, or ask your manager to reduce what you can see.
2. Under it, read when it last synced, for example "Last synced 5 minutes ago.", and how many
   changes are waiting, for example "2 changes waiting to upload."
3. For the exact time, go to **Today** and read the **Sync** card, for example "Last successful
   sync 08/11/2026 at 07:02", and **No changes waiting to upload** or how many are waiting.

## What "not saved: needs attention" means

- "1 entry was not saved and needs attention." on the banner, and **Not saved: needs attention** on
  **Today**, the round, the handover and the person's record, mean Thapsus Care did not accept
  something you recorded. It is only on this phone. See
  [How to deal with an entry that was not saved on the phone](deal-with-an-entry-not-saved-on-the-phone.md).
- The banner never says "Up to date" while an entry needs attention.

## Important

- "Changes waiting to upload" are saved on this phone but are not yet in Thapsus Care. Nobody else
  can see them. Connect as soon as you can, and tell the person in charge about anything urgent
  (HZ-006).
- Never sign out, delete the app, reset the phone to factory settings, or change its passcode, face
  or fingerprint while entries are waiting to upload or need attention: they would be lost. If the
  phone switches off, nothing is lost, but nothing is sent until it is back on (HZ-006, HZ-040).
  Changing a face or fingerprint, or the passcode, makes the phone remove everything on it,
  including unsent entries.
- "Data may be out of date. Check important details before you act." means the phone has not
  synced for about 15 minutes. What you see may have changed since. Check important details, such
  as allergies, food and drink levels and the handover, with the person in charge before you act
  (HZ-002).
- "No data has been downloaded yet. Connect to the internet to sync." means the phone holds no care
  records yet.
- If the banner says "This phone cannot sync", it also says: before giving medicines, check the
  paper MAR or ask the nurse in charge (HZ-138).

## Good to know

- If records look wrong or incomplete, and support asks you to, go to **Settings** and choose
  "Download all data again". You need a connection. The phone refuses while changes are waiting to
  upload, for example "1 change has not uploaded yet. Connect to the internet and wait for it to
  upload, then try again."
- Entries the phone keeps: **Today** says how many days, for example "Entries from the last 7 days
  are kept on this device."
