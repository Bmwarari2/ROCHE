# How to thank a colleague

Send a short note of thanks to someone you work with.

**Who can do this**: any member of staff.

**Register row**: SCHED-011.

## Steps

1. Go to **Wellbeing** in the menu and find **Thank a colleague**.
2. Choose **Where do you both work?**, then choose the **Colleague**.
3. Write **Your note**, up to 500 characters.
4. Choose **Send thanks**. It shows under **Thanks you sent**.

## Good to know

- Your colleague sees your note, and so do the managers where they work.
- Thanks are never part of a performance record.
- You can read the thanks others send you under **Thanks you received**.
