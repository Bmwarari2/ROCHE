# How to check your shifts

See the shifts you are on in published rotas for the next six weeks.

**Who can do this**: any member of staff who clocks in for shifts, including care and support
workers, team leaders or senior carers, nurses, and agency and bank workers.

**Register row**: SCHED-002.

## Steps

1. Go to **My shifts** in the menu.
2. Under **Shifts**, read each shift: the date, its name, the start and end times, the home and the
   kind of shift, for example work or sleep-in.
3. If a shift says **Changed after the rota was published.**, check the new time or day carefully.

## Good to know

- Only published rotas show here. Shifts in a draft rota are not yours yet.
- Times are UK time on the 24-hour clock. A shift that ends at or before its start time ends the
  next day.
- To swap a shift, talk with whoever builds your rota. Swaps are not on the web yet.
