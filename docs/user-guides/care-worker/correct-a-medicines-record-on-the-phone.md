# How to correct a medicines record on the phone

> Arrives with the phone release from `feat/mobile-meds-cd-offline` (slices 69 and 70); not on main yet.

A correction is a new record that names the one that was wrong. The original stays, crossed
through, with your correction beside it.

**Who can do this**: the person who made the record. Nurses and managers can correct anyone's.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002.

## Steps

1. Go to the **Medicines** tab and open the person.
2. Under **Recorded in the last day**, find the record. Choose one that shows **Correct**.
3. Check what it was "Recorded as", then choose what really happened under **What really happened**. Add
   the **Reason**, **What happened, in words** or **Amount given** if asked.
4. Fill in **What needs correcting, and why**, in at least 20 characters.
5. Choose **Record the correction**. The phone says "Correction recorded on this phone. It will
   be sent when there is a connection."
6. To leave without saving, choose **Stop without recording**.

## Important

- **A correction never changes or deletes the record.** It is a new entry naming the old one, and
  both stay in the history (HZ-004).
- **A record that has not been sent yet cannot be corrected.** The phone says "This record has
  not reached Thapsus Care yet. Correct it once it has synced."
- **A controlled drug's record is corrected on the web, with a witness**, not on the phone.
- A correction made offline waits on the phone like any other record, and Thapsus Care checks it
  again when it arrives. If it is not taken, it shows under **Not saved: needs attention**: tell
  the nurse in charge (HZ-006).
- Only records from the last day are listed. For an older record, use the web.
