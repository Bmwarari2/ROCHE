# How to correct a medicines record on the phone

A correction is a new record that names the one that was wrong. The original stays, crossed
through, with your correction beside it.

**Who can do this**: you can correct only the records you made yourself. Nurses, deputy managers,
Registered Managers and owners can correct anyone's. This applies to care or support workers, team
leaders or senior carers and agency or bank workers alike.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002.

## Steps

1. Go to the **Medicines** tab and open the person.
2. Under **Recorded in the last day**, find the record. Choose one that ends with **Correct**.
3. Check what it was (**Recorded as:** and what it said), then choose what really happened under
   **What really happened**. Add the **Reason**, **What happened, in words** or **Amount given**
   if asked.
4. Fill in **What needs correcting, and why**, in at least 20 characters.
5. Choose **Record the correction**. The phone says **Correction recorded on this phone. It will
   be sent when there is a connection.**
6. To leave without saving, choose **Stop without recording**.

## Important

- **A correction never changes or deletes the record.** It is a new entry naming the old one, and
  both stay in the history. The correction is recorded, with who did it (HZ-004).
- **Correct only your own records** unless you are a nurse or manager. The list shows everyone's
  records for the person, and the phone does not stop you choosing one you did not make. Thapsus
  Care refuses it when it is sent, and it shows under **Not saved: needs attention**. Tell the
  person who made the record, or the nurse in charge, instead.
- **A record that has not been sent yet cannot be corrected.** It shows **This record has not
  reached Thapsus Care yet. Correct it once it has synced.**
- **A controlled drug's record is not corrected on the phone.** It shows **A controlled drug's
  record is corrected in Thapsus Care on the web, with a witness.** The web does not have a screen
  for correcting a dose record yet: tell the nurse in charge.
- A correction made offline waits on the phone like any other record, and Thapsus Care checks it
  again when it arrives. Until then it is not on Thapsus Care. If it is not taken, it shows under
  **Not saved: needs attention**: tell the nurse in charge (HZ-006).
- Only records from the last 24 hours are listed. Older records cannot be corrected on the phone,
  and not yet on the web: tell the nurse in charge.
