# How to check the person before you give care on the phone

The top of every screen about a person shows what you must check before you give care or record
anything: who they are, their allergies, their resuscitation decision, their food and drink levels
and how they communicate.

**Who can do this**: everyone who gives care: care and support workers, agency or bank workers,
team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered Managers and deputy managers.

**Register rows**: PEOP-001, PEOP-002, CARE-015, MOB-002, MOB-006.

## Steps

1. Go to **Today** or **People** and choose the person.
2. At the top of their record, check:
   - the photo matches the person in front of you
   - the name they like to be called, their full name, their date of birth ("Born 02/04/1938", for
     example), their NHS number and their room
   - their allergies, for example "Allergies (2)", "No known allergies" or "Allergies not recorded"
   - their resuscitation decision, for example **Resuscitation: do not attempt CPR**, with the form
     and the date it was completed
   - their food and drink levels, for example "Food: Level 6 Soft and bite-sized (blue)" or
     **Food and drink levels are not on this device. Check with the nurse in charge.**
   - their communication needs, for example "Large print".
3. Scroll down to **Allergies** for each allergy, with **Reaction:** and **Severity:** where they are
   recorded.
4. To read how to support the person, choose **Care plan**. To read their risks and controls,
   choose **Risk assessments**. Both are read only on the phone.
5. When you open a form (a note, a chart or a body map mark), a short bar with the person's photo,
   name and allergies stays at the top while you scroll. Check it again before you choose
   **Save**.

## "Not recorded" is not "none"

- "Allergies not recorded" means nobody has recorded whether the person has allergies. It does
  **not** mean they have none. Check before you give food or medicines.
- "Allergies (details not on device)" means allergies are recorded but have not reached the phone.
  Check the full record on the web or with the person in charge.
- **Resuscitation: not recorded** means no ReSPECT or DNACPR decision is on the phone. Check the
  paper record and tell the nurse or senior in charge.
- **Resuscitation: records disagree** means the records on the phone do not match. Check the
  documents and tell the nurse or senior in charge.
- **Food and drink levels are not on this device. Check with the nurse in charge.** Do not guess a
  texture.

## Important

- Check the photo and the allergies every time, before every entry and every piece of care (HZ-001,
  HZ-007).
- The details are what the phone held at the last sync. If the banner says the data may be out of
  date, check with the person in charge before you act (HZ-002).
- If the care plan says **There is no published care plan for this person on this device. Ask the
  nurse in charge.**, ask before you give care.
- If a review shows as overdue, for example **Review overdue since 01/11/2026**, tell the nurse in
  charge. Follow the plan as it is until it is changed.

## Good to know

- Changes to the care plan and risk assessments are made in Thapsus Care on the web.
