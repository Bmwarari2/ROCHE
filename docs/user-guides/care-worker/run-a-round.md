# How to do a round

The round lists the care tasks due on this shift, room by room.

**Who can do this**: care workers, senior carers, nurses and managers. A care worker sees only the
people assigned to them. Some roles can see the round but not act on it.

**Register row**: CARE-013. On the web only for now: the round is not in the mobile app yet.

## Steps

1. Go to **Round** in the menu. Choose your home if Harbour asks.
2. Check the line at the top, for example "2 overdue, 3 due now, 5 still to come".
3. Go to the person. To see only their tasks, choose **Show only Ada's tasks**. Choose
   **Show everyone** to go back.
4. Check the person's header: photo, name, room, allergies, resuscitation decision, food and
   drink levels and communication needs.
5. For each task:
   - Choose **Start** if it will take a while. It then shows **Started**.
   - Choose **Done** when it is done.
   - If the task shows **Record it**, it needs a record, for example a chart entry. Choose it,
     save the entry, and the task is done.
   - If it was not done, choose **Not done**, say why (for example "Asleep, will try again at
     03:00") and choose **Mark not done**.
6. Done tasks leave the round.

## What the words mean

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Still to come | Not due yet. |
| Due now | Do it now. |
| Overdue | The time has passed. Do it, or mark it not done with a reason. |
| Overdue: escalated | The person in charge has been told. |
| Catching up a missed round | A task from an earlier round that was not done. |
| From an earlier plan: check the current plan | The care plan has changed. Check it before you act. |
| Not a round: see the medicines record | Medicines are not done on the round. |

## "Not recorded" is not "none"

- **Allergies not recorded** means nobody has recorded whether the person has allergies. It does
  **not** mean they have none. Check with the nurse in charge.
- **CPR decision not recorded** means no ReSPECT plan or DNACPR decision is in Harbour. Check the
  paper record and tell the nurse in charge.
- **Not recorded: check with the nurse in charge before giving any food** (or drink) means no food
  or drink level is recorded. Do not guess.
- **No level prescribed** is different: someone has recorded that no special level is needed.

## Good to know

- The page updates itself when a task falls due, and at least every 5 minutes. It waits while you
  are typing a reason.
- If the list says it has more tasks than it can show, do not treat it as the whole round. Ask
  the person in charge.
- Tasks still overdue or not done at the end of the shift go into the handover.
- Check the person's photo and date of birth before every entry.
