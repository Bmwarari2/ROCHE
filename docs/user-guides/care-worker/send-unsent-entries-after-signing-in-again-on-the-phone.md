# How to send unsent entries after signing in again on the phone

If your session ends before everything you recorded has reached Thapsus Care, the phone removes the
care record but keeps those entries, encrypted, for you. Sign in again on the same phone to send
them.

**Who can do this**: the member of staff who wrote the entries. A manager can send or delete them
for you: see [How to send or delete another person's unsent entries on a phone](../registered-manager/send-or-delete-another-persons-unsent-entries-on-a-phone.md).

**Register rows**: MOB-001, MOB-006, MOB-010.

## Steps

1. Open the Thapsus Care app on the phone you used. The sign-in screen shows a card, for example
   **Sign in again to send 3 entries**, with whose entries they are and when they must be sent by.
2. Check the card names you, for example "Entries from Ada Example are waiting to be sent." If it
   names someone else, do not sign in here: tell them, or the person in charge.
3. Sign in as usual. See [How to sign in on the phone](sign-in-on-the-phone.md).
4. The phone puts the entries back to be sent and sends them when it connects. Check **Today**: the
   **Sync** card shows them waiting to upload, then **No changes waiting to upload**.

## Important

- Nothing on the card has reached Thapsus Care. Until you sign in and the phone sends them, the care
  they record is not on anyone's record. Tell the nurse in charge if you cannot sign in soon
  (HZ-006).
- Do not delete them to get past this screen. Choose **Delete 3 unsent entries** only when the care
  has been recorded another way, or the nurse in charge knows. Deleted entries cannot be recovered
  (HZ-040).
- Nothing is deleted automatically. If they are not sent in time, the card says a manager must
  resolve them, and the phone shows a notification.
- If the card says the phone's date and time are earlier than when the entries were held, put the
  phone's date and time right in its own settings.

## If something goes wrong

- After you sign in, a line on **Today** may say entries are "still held on this device and not yet
  back in the upload queue". Lock and unlock Thapsus Care. If it stays, tell the nurse in charge.
- **A manager withdrew this device from Thapsus Care, so the care records on it have been removed.**
  You can still send the waiting entries: on the card choose **Send 3 entries for them**, enter
  your email address and password, and choose **Sign in and send**. Then use another device.
- **Care mode on the phone was switched off for your organisation, so these entries could not be
  sent.** They stay on the phone, encrypted, until Care mode is switched back on or a manager deals
  with them. Ask your manager.
