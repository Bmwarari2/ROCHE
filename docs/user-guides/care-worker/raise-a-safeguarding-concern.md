# How to raise a safeguarding concern

Tell the safeguarding lead that you think a person we support is being abused or neglected, or is
at risk of it.

**Who can do this**: anyone who records incidents (care workers, agency and bank workers, senior
carers, nurses, managers, the owner and most other staff), about a person they can see in
Harbour. You need to have signed in with your passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: SAFE-006.

## Before you start

- **If the person is in danger now, keep them safe and get help first.** Call the emergency
  services if needed, and tell the person in charge of the shift.
- The page is **Raise a safeguarding concern**. Managers reach it from **Safeguarding** in the
  menu. Care workers have no menu link to it yet: ask the person in charge for the link (the
  address ends `/safeguarding/new`). If you cannot open it, tell the person in charge what you saw.
  They will raise it.

## Steps

1. On **Raise a safeguarding concern**, choose **Who it is about**. You only see the people in
   your scope.
2. Under **What kind of abuse or neglect**, tick every kind that fits, for example **Physical**,
   **Financial or material** or **Neglect or acts of omission**. Tick at least one.
3. Under **What happened**, write in your own words what you saw or were told, when and where.
   Write facts, not opinions. This is kept as you write it.
4. If you know what staff must do now to keep the person safe, write it under **Protective
   measures**, for example "Two staff for all personal care". Carers see this on the person's
   record, never the concern.
5. Choose **Raise concern**. The page says "Concern raised".

## Good to know

- The referral clock starts when you raise the concern. A manager decides whether to refer it to
  the local authority. Harbour never refers it for them.
- Harbour does not tell the safeguarding lead straight away when you raise a concern. **Tell the
  person in charge, or the safeguarding lead, as well**, as your safeguarding procedure says.
- You cannot change what you wrote after you raise it. If something needs adding or correcting,
  tell the safeguarding lead.
- After you raise it you cannot open the concern from the menu. Only managers of the safeguarding
  log can. You see the protective measures on the person's record (see
  [How to check a person's safeguarding measures](check-safeguarding-measures.md)).
- You can never decide alone that a concern you raised is not referred, or close it, even if you
  are a manager.
- If it happened as an incident, also record it (see [How to record an incident](record-an-incident.md)).
- The rules: [safety specification, safeguarding log](../../specs/safety.md#safeguarding-log-plan-slice-13-safe-006-hz-126)
  and hazard [HZ-126](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-126-a-safeguarding-concern-not-progressed-or-protective-measures-not-visible-to-the-staff-caring-for-the-person).
