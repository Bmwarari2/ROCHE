# How to give a homely remedy

A homely remedy is a medicine bought over the counter, such as one for a headache, that the home
keeps for anyone. It is given only within a protocol the GP or pharmacist agreed for your home, by
the staff the protocol names.

**Who can do this**: staff who give medicines (care workers, senior carers, nurses and managers,
and agency or bank workers) and whom the protocol names. Thapsus Care checks the protocol names you.

**Register row**: MEDS-007.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines** and then **Homely remedies**.
2. Under **Give a homely remedy**, check the person's name, date of birth, NHS number and every
   allergy.
3. Read **Current medicines**: the medicines on the person's MAR (medicines administration record)
   now.
4. Under **Protocol**, choose the remedy. Only protocols in use at this home are listed. Read the
   protocol's figures shown beside it: one dose, the shortest gap, the most in 24 hours and the
   days before the GP is asked.
5. Check **Amount**. The protocol's dose is filled in for you.
6. Choose **Check against the protocol**. Thapsus Care says either "The check found this dose is
   within the protocol now." or that it is outside the protocol, with each reason.
7. If it is outside the protocol, do not give it. Follow your home's homely remedies policy and
   tell the nurse or senior in charge (whoever your home gives medicines record management).
8. If it is within the protocol:
   - Under **Why it is given**, write the symptom in your own words.
   - Compare the remedy with the current medicines, then tick **I have compared this remedy with
     the person's current medicines**.
   - Compare the person's allergies with the remedy, then tick **I have compared the person's
     allergies with this remedy**.
9. Give the remedy, then choose **Record the dose**. The page says **Homely remedy recorded**.

## If you recorded it by mistake

1. Under **Doses given**, find the dose and choose **Mark the dose of** the remedy **as entered in
   error**.
2. Fill in **Why it was entered in error** and choose **Mark as entered in error**.

The dose stays on the record, struck through, with **Entered in error** and your reason. It no
longer counts towards the protocol's limits. You can do this for a dose you gave; a nurse or
manager can do it for anyone's.

## Important

- **Thapsus Care never decides a dose, an interaction or an allergy match.** It checks only for
  the same product on the MAR, not the same ingredient in another medicine. You compare the
  allergies and ingredients, and ask the pharmacist if you are not sure (HZ-007, HZ-120).
- If the protocol has no NHS medicines code (dm+d), Thapsus Care says it cannot check it against
  the MAR at all. Compare it with the current medicines yourself.
- If the current medicines cannot be read, the page says to check the MAR before giving a homely
  remedy.
- If you change the protocol or the amount after the check, check again. **Record the dose** shows
  only after a check that found the dose within the protocol.
- If the home has no protocol in use, a homely remedy cannot be given. Ask a nurse or manager.
- A dose is recorded as given now, by you. Recording a dose given earlier is not on the web.
