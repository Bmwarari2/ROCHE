# How to use a shared phone or tablet

Some homes keep a phone or tablet on a unit that several staff use. Thapsus Care locks a shared
device sooner, and only one person is signed in at a time.

**Who can do this**: everyone who uses Care mode on a shared device: care and support workers,
agency or bank workers, team leaders or senior carers, clinical leads or nurses, Registered
Managers and deputy managers.

**Register rows**: MOB-005, MOB-009.

## Set the device as shared

1. Sign in as yourself. See [How to sign in on the phone](sign-in-on-the-phone.md).
2. Go to **Settings** at the bottom of the screen.
3. Under "Security", under "This device is", choose "Shared with colleagues".
4. Under "Lock a shared device after", choose "30 seconds", "1 minute", "1 minute 30 seconds" or
   "2 minutes", as your home has agreed. The line above then says, for example, "Thapsus Care locks
   after 30 seconds without use."

## When you finish with the device

1. Check the banner at the top of the screen and the **Sync** card on **Today**. Wait until it says
   **No changes waiting to upload**. If it cannot send (for example, there is no connection), keep
   the device signed in and tell the person in charge.
2. Go to **Settings** and choose **Sign out**. See [How to sign out of the phone](sign-out-of-the-phone.md).
3. The next person signs in as themselves.

## Important

- Never record care while someone else is signed in. Everything you save is recorded as theirs.
  Check the name on the lock screen ("Signed in as ...") and on **Today**.
- Signing out removes the care records from the device. Anything not yet sent is deleted with
  them, so wait for **No changes waiting to upload** first (HZ-006, HZ-040).
- The device still unlocks with its own face, fingerprint or passcode, not with a personal PIN.
  Anyone who can unlock the device can open the signed-in person's session, so choose "Lock now"
  under "Security" whenever you put it down.

## Not on the phone yet

The kiosk features in the design are not built: there is no personal PIN with your photo, no fast
switching between staff, no automatic sign-out at the end of your shift and no set-up by a manager.
Each person signs in in full and signs out at the end. The setting is kept on the device, and anyone
signed in can change it.
