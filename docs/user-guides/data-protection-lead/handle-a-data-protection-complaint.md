# How to handle a data protection complaint

A data protection complaint is someone unhappy with how your organisation uses personal data.
Harbour keeps the acknowledgement clock and reminds you when the next update is due.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality lead, or anyone whose role covers data protection
for the whole organisation.

**Register row**: PLAT-022.

## Record it

1. Go to **Data rights**, then **Complaints**.
2. Under **Record a complaint**, enter the **Day received**, **How it was made**, **Who
   complained** and their name, how to contact them if you have it, **What it is about** and a
   **Summary**.
3. Choose **Record complaint**.

## Follow the clocks

Open complaints come first. Each shows, in words with an icon, when to acknowledge it (for example
"Acknowledge by 14/11/2026", or "Acknowledgement overdue since ...") and when the next update to
the complainant is due. The days come from your organisation's settings and the bank holidays.

## Work through it

1. Open the complaint.
2. Under **Acknowledgement**, enter the day you acknowledged it, then **Record acknowledgement**.
3. Each time you tell the complainant how it is going, record it under **Update to the
   complainant**.
4. Under **Outcome**, write the outcome, confirm you told them they can complain to the ICO, tick
   the box to confirm closing, then **Close complaint**. A closed complaint cannot change.

## Good to know

- Linking a complaint to a person you support or to a breach is not on this screen yet.
