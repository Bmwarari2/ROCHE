# How to handle a data protection complaint

A data protection complaint is someone unhappy with how your organisation uses personal data.
Harbour keeps the acknowledgement clock and reminds you when the next update is due.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or anyone whose role covers
data protection for the whole organisation. A Registered Manager or deputy whose role covers one
home does not see these complaints. Other complaints about care go in **Complaints**, not here.

**Register row**: PLAT-022.

## Steps

### Record it

1. Go to **Data rights**, then **Complaints**.
2. Under **Record a complaint**, enter the **Day received** and choose **How it was made**, **Who
   complained** and **What it is about**.
3. Enter the **Complainant's name** and, if you have them, **How to contact them (optional)**.
4. Write a **Summary** of what the complainant says.
5. Choose **Record complaint**. It says "Complaint recorded."

### Follow the clocks

Open complaints are listed under **Complaints recorded**. Each shows, in words with an icon, when
to acknowledge it, for example "Acknowledge by 14/11/2026" or "Acknowledgement overdue since
14/11/2026", and when the next update is due, for example "Next update due by 14/01/2027". Your
organisation's own target for acknowledging it is shown alongside.

### Work through it

1. Open the complaint.
2. Under **Acknowledgement**, enter **When was it acknowledged?**, then choose **Record
   acknowledgement**.
3. Each time you tell the complainant how it is going, under **Update to the complainant** enter
   **When did you tell them how it is going?**, then choose **Record update**.
4. Under **Outcome**, enter the **Day closed** and write the **Outcome**, as you told the
   complainant.
5. Tick "The outcome told the complainant they can complain to the ICO". The outcome must always
   tell them this.
6. Tick "Close this complaint. It cannot be changed afterwards.", then choose **Close complaint**.

## Good to know

- **Harbour does not contact the complainant.** You write to them; Harbour records the dates.
- The days come from your organisation's settings and the bank holidays. Complaints received
  before 19/06/2026 have no statutory acknowledgement clock.
- Dates cannot be in the future, or before the complaint was received.
- A closed complaint cannot change.
- Linking a complaint to a person you support, or to a breach, is not on this screen yet.
- Your procedure is in [data protection complaints](../../compliance/data-protection-complaints.md).
  The rules are in the
  [platform specification](../../specs/platform.md#breach-register-and-data-protection-complaints-slice-20-plat-022)
  (rule 25).
