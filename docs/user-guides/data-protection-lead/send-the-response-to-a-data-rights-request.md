# How to send the response to a data rights request

Once every record is decided, Harbour builds the response from your decisions. You check it,
download it, send it to the requester yourself, and record that you have.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or anyone whose role covers
data protection for the whole organisation. Checking and recording the response need you to confirm
it is you with your passkey or authenticator app (step-up).

**Register rows**: PLAT-022, WORK-011 (requests from members of staff).

## Steps

1. Open the request. Under **4. Send the response**, choose **Check the response and send it**.
   If it says what to do first, such as "Confirm who is asking first." or "Review every record
   first", do that.
2. Read **Records to send**. Parts taken out read [redacted]; records held back are left out and
   counted. If the page lists **Not searched: check these yourself**, make sure your response says
   what was not searched.
3. Choose **Download the response to send**. Send the file to the requester, in the format their
   communication needs ask for.
4. Under **Record the response**, choose the **Outcome**: **Sent in full**, **Sent in part**,
   **Refused** or **Withdrawn by the requester**. For sent in part or refused, give the **Reason**,
   as you told the requester.
5. Enter the **Day you responded**.
6. Tick "I have sent the response. The request will be closed and cannot be changed afterwards."
7. Choose **Record the response and close the request**.

The request now shows **Response**, with the outcome, when it was sent and its fingerprint. Choose
**Download exactly what was sent** to get the same file again.

## Good to know

- **Harbour does not send the response.** It prepares the file; you send it and record that you
  did.
- The response can be checked only once every record has a decision and you have confirmed who is
  asking. If a record changed after you reviewed it, it goes back to review first.
- The fingerprint (SHA-256) proves later that the file is exactly what was sent. Harbour keeps a
  copy of it.
- A response recorded after the due date stays marked **Responded late**. It is never rewritten as
  on time.
- Once closed, a request cannot be changed.
- The rules are in the
  [platform specification](../../specs/platform.md#phase-2-the-dsar-workbench-plat-022-work-011)
  (rule 5). Your procedure is the
  [subject access request procedure](../../compliance/dsar-procedure.md).
