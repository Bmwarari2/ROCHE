# How to place or release a legal hold

Stop records being destroyed while a complaint, claim, inquest, safeguarding enquiry or
investigation needs them, and release the hold when it is over.

**Who can do this**: only the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or someone whose
organisation-wide role has been given retention management. Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is
you with your passkey or authenticator app each time.

**Register row**: PLAT-012.

## Place a hold

1. Go to **Records retention** in the menu.
2. Under **Place a legal hold**, answer **What should the hold cover?**:
   - **Every record about a person**: choose the **Person**. People who have left or died are
     listed too.
   - **One record**: choose the **Record type** and enter the **Record reference**, as shown on
     the record's page.
3. Under **Why is the hold needed?**, write at least 10 characters. Do not include anyone's care
   details.
4. Choose **Place legal hold**. If Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is you, do so, then come
   back to the page and try again. The page says "Legal hold placed."
5. The hold is listed under **Legal holds in force**, marked **Hold in force**, with when it was
   placed and the reason.

## Release a hold

1. Under **Legal holds in force**, find the hold and choose **Release hold**.
2. Thapsus Care asks **Release this legal hold?** and says that records it covered that are past
   their retention period can then be destroyed.
3. Under **Why can the hold be released?**, write at least 10 characters.
4. Choose **Release hold**, or **Keep the hold**. The page says "Legal hold released." The hold
   moves to **Released legal holds**, with why it was released.

## Good to know

- A hold stops destruction whatever the record's retention period, until it is released.
- For now Thapsus Care destroys only documents, so a hold on one record is a hold on one
  document.
- Placing and releasing holds is recorded, with who did it.
