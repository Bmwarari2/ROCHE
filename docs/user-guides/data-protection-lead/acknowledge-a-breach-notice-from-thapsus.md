# How to acknowledge a breach notice from Thapsus

Thapsus runs Harbour for you as your processor. If Thapsus finds a breach that affects your data,
it sends you a notice. Harbour opens a breach in your register from it, so you can decide what to
do.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or anyone whose role covers
data protection for the whole organisation. The named data protection lead and the owners are told
when a notice arrives.

**Register row**: PLAT-022.

## Steps

1. You get an urgent notification, "Thapsus has told you about a personal data breach", in Harbour
   and by email. Its link opens **Data protection**, not the register, so go to **Data rights**,
   then **Breaches**. A breach opened from a notice says "Opened from a breach notice from Thapsus".
2. Open it. Under **Notices from Thapsus**, read **What happened**, **Kinds of data**, **How
   many**, **Likely consequences**, **What Thapsus has done**, **What your organisation needs to
   do** and the **Thapsus contact**.
3. Choose **Acknowledge notice**. The notice then shows when it was acknowledged.
4. Work through the breach as for any other: see
   [How to record and handle a data breach](record-a-data-breach.md).

## Good to know

- **The ICO clock starts from when your organisation became aware**, which may be when the notice
  arrived. Harbour starts it from the notice.
- A follow-up notice about the same breach appears under the first one. Acknowledge each one.
- Acknowledging a notice does not report anything to the ICO. Whether to report is your decision.
- Correcting when your organisation became aware is not on this screen yet. Ask Harbour support.
- Your procedure is the [breach response procedure](../../compliance/breach-response.md). The
  rules are in the
  [platform specification](../../specs/platform.md#breach-register-and-data-protection-complaints-slice-20-plat-022)
  (rule 24).
