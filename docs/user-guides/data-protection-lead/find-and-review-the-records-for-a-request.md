# How to find and review the records for a data rights request

Harbour finds every record that names the person, then you decide, record by record, what is sent.
Harbour marks other people's names for you, but it never takes anything out by itself.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or anyone whose role covers
data protection for the whole organisation. Opening a record needs you to confirm it is you with
your passkey or authenticator app (step-up).

**Register rows**: PLAT-022, WORK-011 (requests from members of staff).

## Steps

### Find the records

1. Open the request from **Data rights**, **Requests**.
2. Under **2. Find the records**, choose **Find the records**. Harbour searches every part of the
   record that names this person, and says "The records have been collected."
3. If you see **Not searched: check these yourself**, read it first. Harbour could not search those
   parts of the record for this person, and says why for each one. Check each one by hand before you
   respond, and say in your response if anything was left out.
4. If new records may have been written since, choose **Find new records**. Collecting again adds
   only records written since.

### Review each record

1. Under **3. Review each record**, the table lists each record with its **Part of the record**,
   **Record** and **Decision**. The counts say how many are still to review.
2. Choose **Review** beside a record. Confirm it is you if asked.
3. Read **What the record says**. Under **Other people named**, Harbour lists the names of other
   people your organisation records, and marks each one in the record with the words "Name of
   another person". Decide whether each can be sent (for example, because they wrote the record as
   staff) or must be taken out. If Harbour found none, still read the record for anyone else it
   mentions.
4. Under **Your decision**, choose one:
   - **Send it as it is**;
   - **Send it with parts taken out**: tick each of the **Parts to take out** (each reads
     [redacted] in what is sent) and give the **Reason**;
   - **Hold it back**: give the **Reason**, such as the exemption or that it is about someone else.
5. Choose **Save the decision**, then **Back to the request** for the next record.

When every record is decided, [send the response](send-the-response-to-a-data-rights-request.md).

## Good to know

- **Redaction is your decision.** Harbour highlights names but never takes anything out, and never
  decides for you whether a record is sent.
- If someone changes a record after you opened it, Harbour refuses your decision and asks you to
  open it again and review what it says now. A record that changes after you decided goes back to
  **To review**, so what is sent is always what you reviewed.
- Every record you open is in the audit trail, against the person it is about.
- A record that is no longer held cannot be decided here yet, and the response cannot be sent
  until it is. Tell Harbour support.
- The rules are in the
  [platform specification](../../specs/platform.md#phase-2-the-dsar-workbench-plat-022-work-011)
  (rules 3 and 4) and [ADR-0090](../../decisions/0090-dsar-workbench-and-disclosure-collector.md).
  Your procedure is the [subject access request procedure](../../compliance/dsar-procedure.md).
