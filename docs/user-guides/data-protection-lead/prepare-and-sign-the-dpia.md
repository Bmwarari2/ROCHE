# How to prepare and sign the DPIA

The data protection impact assessment (DPIA) records the risks of using Harbour for care records
and how they are reduced. Someone prepares it, and the named data protection lead signs it.
Whoever prepared a version cannot sign it.

**Who can do this**: prepare: the owner, Registered Manager or quality lead. Sign: the person named
as data protection lead, who must be the owner or a quality lead. Signing needs you to confirm it
is you (step-up).

**Register row**: PLAT-023.

## Name the data protection lead (owner)

1. Go to **Data protection** in the menu.
2. Under **Data protection responsibilities**, choose the **Data protection lead**, and fill in the
   data protection officer, sector, privacy notice link and the date you signed the data
   processing agreement.
3. Choose **Save responsibilities**. Changing the lead asks you to confirm it is you.

## Prepare it

1. Under **Prepare the DPIA**, choose **Save the DPIA** to start a version.
2. Answer each section.
3. For each risk, choose the **Risk remaining after your measures**: low, medium or high. If a risk
   stays high, enter the ICO consultation reference.
4. Choose **Save the DPIA**, then **Mark ready to sign**.
5. Ask the data protection lead to sign it.

## Sign it

1. Open **Data protection** and read the whole version.
2. Under **Sign the DPIA**, type your full name, as it appears on your Harbour account.
3. Choose **Sign the DPIA** and confirm it is you.
4. The page says "DPIA signed" and shows when to review it.

## Good to know

- Your signature is tied to this exact version. If it changes, it must be signed again.
- Review it by the date shown, or sooner if your set-up changes.
- The records-of-processing and sub-processor registers have no screen yet.
