# How to record and handle a data breach

Every personal data breach goes in the breach register, whether or not it must be reported. Harbour
keeps the ICO clock and suggests what to consider. The decision is yours.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality lead, or anyone whose role covers data protection
for the whole organisation.

**Register row**: PLAT-022.

## Record it

1. Go to **Data rights**, then **Breaches**.
2. Under **Record a breach**, describe **What happened** without naming anyone. The register is
   not a care record.
3. Enter **When did the organisation become aware of it?**, in UK time on the 24-hour clock. Add
   when it happened if you know.
4. Choose **Record breach**.

## Follow the ICO clock

Each breach shows the clock in words, with an icon:

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Report to the ICO by 14:00 on 02/11/2026: 20 hours left | The deadline if it must be reported. |
| Overdue: the ICO deadline passed at ... | Report now, and say why it is late. |
| Reported on time / Reported late | The report is recorded. |
| No report needed | The decision is not to report it. |

The hours come from your organisation's settings, counted from when you became aware.

## Work through it

Open the breach. Each step is shown only while it applies.

1. **Assessment**: answer the questions about who and what is affected, then **Save
   assessment**.
2. **Harbour's suggestion**: read it. It is decision support only.
3. **Decision**: choose whether to report to the ICO and whether to tell the people affected, and
   give your reasoning. If you go against the suggestion, tick the box that says so. Then choose
   **Record decision**.
4. **Report to the ICO**: once reported, enter when and the ICO's reference, then **Record
   report**. A late report needs the reason for the delay.
5. **Telling the people affected**: Harbour fills in a notice from its template. Finish every part
   in square brackets, enter the day you told them, then **Record notice**.
6. **Closing**: write what you have learned, then **Close breach**. A closed breach cannot change.

## Notices from Thapsus

If Thapsus, as your processor, tells you of a breach, it appears in the register with its notice
under **Notices from Thapsus**. Read it and acknowledge it.

## Good to know

- Correcting what happened or when, after recording, is not on this screen yet. Ask Harbour support.
- Your breach response procedure says who to tell and when. Harbour supports your judgement and
  never replaces it.
