# How to handle a data rights request

A data rights request is someone asking to see, correct, erase or take their information. This
guide covers recording the request, confirming who is asking and keeping the clock. Finding the
records, reviewing them and sending the response each have their own guide.

**Who can do this**: the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or anyone whose role covers
data protection for the whole organisation. A Registered Manager or deputy whose role covers one
home does not see **Data rights**, and Harbour refuses them if they try.

**Register rows**: PLAT-022, WORK-011 (requests from members of staff).

## Steps

### Record the request

1. Go to **Data rights** in the menu. It opens on **Requests**.
2. Under **Record a request**, choose **What they are asking for** and **Whose information it is**:
   **Person we support** or **Member of staff**. Then, under **Choose who**, choose them.
3. Under **Who is asking**, choose **The person themselves** or **Someone on their behalf**. For
   someone on their behalf, enter the **Name of the person asking on their behalf** and choose
   **Their authority to ask**, for example **Lasting power of attorney for health and welfare**.
4. Choose **How it arrived** and enter the **Day it arrived**.
5. Leave **We need to confirm who is asking before we respond** ticked, unless you already know
   who they are. The clock starts on the day you confirm it.
6. Choose **Record the request**. The request opens and says "The request is recorded."

### Confirm who is asking

1. Under **1. Confirm who is asking**, choose **How you confirmed it**, for example "we saw a
   document that proves who they are".
2. Enter the **Day you confirmed it**.
3. Choose **Confirm and start the clock**.

### Pause or extend the clock

Read the words under **Pause or extend the clock** first.

1. To pause while you wait for the requester to tell you what they want, enter the **Day you asked
   them to clarify** and choose **Pause the clock**.
2. When they reply, enter the **Day they clarified** and choose **Restart the clock**.
3. To extend, for a complex request or several from the same person only, enter the **Months to
   extend by**, choose **Why**, write **Explain the extension** (this is what you tell the
   requester, so keep other people's names out of it), enter the **Day you decided**, then choose
   **Extend the deadline**. Harbour tells you if it is too long or too late.

Then [find and review the records](find-and-review-the-records-for-a-request.md) and
[send the response](send-the-response-to-a-data-rights-request.md). For erasure, correction or
taking information elsewhere, see
[How to handle a request to erase, correct or move information](handle-an-erasure-correction-or-portability-request.md).

## Read the clock

**Where the request stands** shows the clock as an icon, a word and a sentence. The list on
**Requests** shows the same.

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Clock not started | The clock starts when you confirm who is asking. |
| Running | Respond by the day shown, for example "Respond by 01/12/2026." |
| Due soon | The day is close. |
| Overdue | The response was due on the day shown. Respond now. |
| Paused | You are waiting for the requester to clarify. The due date moves on by the days it is paused. |
| Responded | The request is closed, on time. |
| Responded late | The request is closed, after the due date. |

Harbour works the date out each time you open the page, from the dates you record, the bank
holidays and your organisation's settings. Pauses and the extension are listed under the clock.

## Good to know

- **Harbour never sends anything by itself.** It keeps the clock and helps you find and review
  the records. You decide what is sent, and you send it.
- A request is never shown overdue while it is paused, and a late response stays marked late.
- **Members of staff are listed only for people who manage the team.** If you cannot choose a
  member of staff, ask your owner to record the request.
- A request from a person's contact (such as a relative) about their own information is not on
  this screen yet. Ask Harbour support.
- Police, solicitor, coroner and other disclosure requests about a person are not data rights
  requests. They have no screen yet; ask Harbour support.
- The person's communication needs show on the request, so you can reply in a way they can use.
- Your procedure is in the [subject access request procedure](../../compliance/dsar-procedure.md).
  The rules Harbour follows are in the
  [platform specification](../../specs/platform.md#phase-2-the-dsar-workbench-plat-022-work-011)
  and [ADR-0090](../../decisions/0090-dsar-workbench-and-disclosure-collector.md). Harbour supports
  your judgement and never replaces it.
