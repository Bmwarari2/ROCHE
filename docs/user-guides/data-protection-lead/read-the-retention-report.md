# How to read the retention report

See how many records of each kind are past their retention period, held by a legal hold or not yet
due, before any of them are destroyed. The report only counts: it destroys nothing.

**Who can do this**: only the owner and the quality and compliance lead, or someone whose
organisation-wide role has been given retention management. Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is
you with your passkey or authenticator app.

**Register row**: PLAT-012.

## Steps

1. Go to **Records retention** in the menu.
2. Find **Records due for destruction**. It starts with **This report only counts. Nothing has been
   destroyed.**
3. Read whether the report is on:
   - **Report off**: the report is off for your organisation. Ask Thapsus Care support to turn it
     on. There is no setting for it on this screen yet.
   - **Report on** with no date: there is no report yet. The report runs every night, so the first
     one appears the morning after it was turned on.
   - **Report on** with a date and time: that is when the latest report counted, in UK time, for
     example "Counted on 09/11/2026 02:29 (UK time)".
4. Read the totals: **Due for destruction**, **Held**, **Not yet due**, **Never offered**, **Not
   evaluated** and **Total**.
5. Read the table **Records by kind and retention status**: one row for each **Kind of record**
   (for example care records or training records), with the same columns. The kinds with the most
   records due come first. **Not read by kind yet** counts records in tables the report does not
   read by kind yet.
6. Under the table, the report says how many tables it read, and how many it counted without
   reading their dates.

## Good to know

- **Due for destruction** means past its retention period with no legal hold. **Held** means under
  a legal hold, so kept. **Not yet due** means still inside its retention period, or the date it
  counts from has not come yet (for example, the person is still receiving care). **Never
  offered** means kept while current, kept for good, or kept until your organisation sets a period.
  **Not evaluated** means Thapsus Care cannot work out these dates yet.
- The report counts holds on a person or on one record. Holds on a member of staff, a kind of
  record, a location or a date range come later, so the report can show more as due than would be
  offered for destruction.
- Only documents can be destroyed today: see
  [How to destroy documents past their retention period](destroy-documents-past-their-retention-period.md).
  Destroying other records comes later, kind by kind, after your data protection officer has
  checked this report.
- Opening the report is recorded in the audit trail. The report holds counts only: no names and no
  record ids.
- The Registered Manager does not see this report: it covers the whole organisation.
