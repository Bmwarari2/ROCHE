# How to reconcile medicines

Reconciliation compares the MAR with the latest documents, such as a discharge letter after a
hospital stay, and records a person's decision about every medicine. It is due after an admission,
a transfer or a return from hospital, and whenever a new list of medicines arrives.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners. Completing it needs current medicines competence.

**Register row**: MEDS-001.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Medicines**.
2. Start the reconciliation:
   - Under **Reconciliations due**, choose, for example, **Start the return from hospital
     reconciliation**.
   - For a new list from a clinic or the GP, under **Reconcile a change**, choose **Start a
     reconciliation**.
   - If one is already open, choose **Open the reconciliation**.
3. Under **1. Sources**, choose **Add a source** for each document. Fill in the **Kind of source**,
   the **Source reference** and **When it was received**. Fill in **Why it is still current** only
   if it was received before the admission, transfer or change. Choose **Add a source**.
4. Under **2. Lines from the sources**, choose **Add a line** for each medicine line. Choose
   **From which source**, copy **The line as written** exactly, choose **What does the line say?**
   (**Start**, **Continue**, **Change**, **Stop** or **Unclear**) and choose **Add a line**.
5. Under **3. Decide each medicine on the MAR**, for each medicine choose **Continue as it is**,
   **Change it**, **Stop it** or **Ask a question first**, fill in what it asks for, tick the
   **Lines this decision is based on**, and choose **Save the decision about** the medicine.
   - **Change it** asks for the medicine again, as on the add form.
   - **Stop it** asks **Why it stops**.
   - **Ask a question first** asks **Your question**: who you are asking, and what.
6. Under **4. Decide each new line**, for each line that is not already on the MAR, choose **Start
   it** (which asks for the medicine, as on the add form), **Do not start it** (with **Why it is not
   started**) or **Ask a question first**, and save the decision.
7. Under **5. Complete the reconciliation**:
   - Compare the medicines with the person's allergies, then tick **I compared the medicines with
     the person's allergies**.
   - Answer **Was the person involved?**. If **No**, fill in **Why they were not involved**.
   - If there is no discharge letter among the sources, fill in **Why there is no discharge
     letter** when asked.
8. Choose **Complete the reconciliation**. The page says "Reconciliation completed. The MAR is
   updated."

## Important

- **Thapsus Care decides nothing here.** A line that says stop stops nothing until a person decides
  it, and a medicine missing from a letter is never stopped for you. Nothing changes on the MAR
  until the reconciliation is completed (HZ-121).
- **Medicines started or changed by a reconciliation are "Not yet checked"** until a second person
  checks them (see [How to second-check a medicine](second-check-a-medicine.md)) (HZ-005).
- **Thapsus Care never matches allergies.** You compare the sources' allergies with the person's
  and confirm it (HZ-007).
- It cannot be completed while a question is open or anything is undecided. If it is refused,
  each problem is listed in Thapsus Care's words.
- If someone else saved a decision since you opened the page, yours is refused. Reload and check
  theirs first (HZ-121).
- If a version of a medicine starts later, continuing or changing it asks **Why the version
  starting later still stands**.
