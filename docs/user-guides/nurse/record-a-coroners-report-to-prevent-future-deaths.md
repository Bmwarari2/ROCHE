# How to record a coroner's report to prevent future deaths

Record when a coroner sends your organisation a Regulation 28 report to prevent future deaths, and
when your response went, so the legal deadline is not missed.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner, for people at the homes where they work.

**Register row**: SAFE-016.

## Steps

1. Choose **Disclosure requests** in the menu and open the coroner's request about the person. If
   there is none, log one first (see [How to log a disclosure request](log-a-disclosure-request.md)).
2. Under **Regulation 28 report**, enter the date on the coroner's report (the day it was sent),
   not the day it reached you, in **Date the report was sent**. The response is due 56 days after
   that date.
3. Leave **When the response was sent** empty until the response has gone. If the coroner agrees
   a later date for the response, enter it in **New date for the response, if the coroner extended
   it**; the page then shows that date, marked "extended by the coroner".
4. Choose **Save report**. The page says "Report saved" and shows "Response due by" and the date.
5. When your organisation has sent its response to the coroner, enter **When the response was
   sent** and choose **Save report** again. The page shows "Response sent".

## Important

- The response is due by law (the Coroners (Investigations) Regulations 2013). The page counts it
  for you and says "Overdue" once it has passed.
- Enter a new date only when the coroner has agreed one. The date in the coroner's letter is
  usually the normal deadline, not an extension. Keep the coroner's letter.
- Saving again keeps the dates already recorded unless you change them.
- Thapsus Care never writes or sends the response. Your organisation does, usually the Registered
  Manager or the owner.
- The response cannot be dated before the report was sent.
- The rules: [safety specification, disclosure requests](../../specs/safety.md#12-disclosure-requests-slice-41-safe-016).
