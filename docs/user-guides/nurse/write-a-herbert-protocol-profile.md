# How to write a Herbert Protocol profile

Write down what the police need to find a person quickly if they go missing, and keep it up to
date. The Herbert Protocol is the national scheme police forces use for this.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner write and review the profile. Anyone who can read incidents can read it: care and support
workers, agency or bank workers, team leaders or senior carers, care coordinators and the quality
and compliance lead.

**Register row**: SAFE-015.

## Before you start

- Sit down with the person, and their family or representative if the person wants or needs
  them. The profile is only useful if they have checked it.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Herbert Protocol**.
2. Read the notice at the top: "There is no profile yet.", "Up to date. Review it by" a date, or
   "Out of date since" a date.
3. Under **Write the profile** (or **Review the profile** if there is one), fill in each part, for
   example **What they look like**, **What they usually wear**, **Places they may go**, **People
   they may go to**, **Routines and habits** and **How to approach them**.
4. Write **What they look like** from looking at the person: height, build, hair, glasses. Write
   **Places they may go** from what they and their family tell you: former homes, workplaces,
   places they talk about. Both are needed. Thapsus Care never fills these two in for you, but it
   may show a suggestion from the record in the hint under the field.
5. On a first profile, some parts are already filled in from the person's record, for example
   **How they communicate** or **Medical needs**. The hint under each says where it came from.
   Check every one with the person or their family, and correct or remove anything wrong.
6. Write **Reviewed with**, for example "Ada Example and her daughter".
7. Enter **When it was last reviewed**: the date you checked it with them. It cannot be in the
   future.
8. If parts were filled in from the record, tick **I have checked each prefilled part with the
   person or their family**. Thapsus Care will not save a first profile without it.
9. Choose **Save the profile**. The page says "Profile saved" and the profile shows under
   **Saved profile**.

## Good to know

- The profile goes out of date a set number of months after its last review (your organisation's
  setting). When it does, the nurse in charge gets a task to review it. Saving the profile again
  finishes the task.
- A person with a risk assessment for leaving without staff knowing, and no profile, prompts a task
  for the nurse in charge to write one with them. Thapsus Care prompts, but never forces it.
- The photograph is kept with the person's documents. It cannot be added on this page yet: keep the
  printed profile with a current photograph, as your procedure says.
- When the person is missing, the episode says whether their profile is up to date and links to
  it (see [How to keep a missing person's timeline](../care-worker/keep-a-missing-person-timeline.md)).
  Give the police the profile as your procedure says. Thapsus Care never sends it to them.
- Every time someone reads or saves the profile it is recorded, with who did it.
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-127](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-127-a-missing-persons-escalation-delayed-or-the-herbert-protocol-profile-out-of-date).
