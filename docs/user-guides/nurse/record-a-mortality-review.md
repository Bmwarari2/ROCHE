# How to record a mortality review

Look back at a person's death and their care before it, and record what was found and what the
team learns.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner. Staff who can read death records see the review once it is saved, or "Not recorded yet".

**Register row**: SAFE-014.

## Before you start

- Hold the review as your organisation's procedure says, with the people who cared for the person.
- The review is due a set number of days after the death. The time is your organisation's target,
  not a legal deadline.

## Steps

1. Choose **Deaths** in the menu and open the record. The record's status says **Mortality review
   due**, **Mortality review overdue** or **Mortality review done**.
2. Go to **Mortality review**. It says when the review is due.
3. Under **What the review found**, write what happened in the person's care before they died, in
   at least 20 characters. Write facts.
4. Under **What we learn**, write what the team will do differently, or that there is nothing to
   learn and why, in at least 20 characters.
5. Choose **Save the review**. The page says "Review saved" and "Reviewed" with the date and time.
6. When every step is also done or not needed, close the record (see
   [How to keep a death record](keep-a-death-record.md)).

## Good to know

- Thapsus Care records what the review found in words. It never scores a death or decides whether
  it was avoidable.
- You can save the review again while the record is open, for example after a second meeting. Once
  the death record is closed, the review cannot change.
- The death record cannot close until the review is recorded.
- The review does not replace a LeDeR review (the NHS programme that learns from the deaths of
  people with a learning disability and autistic people), a coroner's inquest or a Safeguarding
  Adults Review. Those are run by others.
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-013](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-013-statutory-notification-missed-or-late).
