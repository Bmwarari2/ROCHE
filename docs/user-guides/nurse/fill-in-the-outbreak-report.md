# How to fill in the outbreak report

Use the figures Thapsus Care counts from the line list to report an outbreak to the health
protection team, the local team of the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA).

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner prepare the report. Only the Registered Manager and the owner record it as submitted or not
required.

**Register row**: SAFE-013.

## Steps

1. Choose **Outbreaks** in the menu and open the outbreak.
2. Under **UKHSA report**, read when the report is due. The report's page in the notification
   centre says this time is Thapsus Care's target, not a legal deadline. Report the outbreak as
   soon as you can all the same.
3. If the page shows **The report still needs:**, do not wait for those details. Report to the
   health protection team now with what you have, and give them the rest when you know it. Add
   the organism under **Organism and reference** and residents to the line list as they become
   known (see [How to keep an outbreak line list](keep-an-outbreak-line-list.md)).
4. Copy the figures into the health protection team's report form: **Setting**, **Organism**,
   **Declared on**, **Resident cases**, **Staff cases**, **First symptoms**, **Latest symptoms**,
   **Tested**, **Positive**, **Waiting for results**, **In hospital** and **Died**. A field with no
   value says **Not recorded**.
5. **Staff cases** says **Not collected on this screen**. Count unwell staff yourself and add them
   to the report.
6. Report the outbreak to the health protection team the way they ask, for example by phone or
   their online form.
7. Choose **Open the UKHSA report in notifications**. Prepare the notification there, and ask the
   Registered Manager to record it as submitted, with the team's reference (see
   [How to prepare a statutory notification](../registered-manager/prepare-a-statutory-notification.md)
   and [How to record a notification as submitted](../registered-manager/record-a-notification-as-submitted.md)).

## Important

- The report time is Thapsus Care's target, not a legal deadline. The health protection team asks
  to be told without delay. The report shows in the notification centre with every other
  notification, and the managers are alerted if it is late.
- Thapsus Care counts the figures from every row on the line list, including residents you cannot
  see. It never guesses a missing figure and never sends the report. What goes in the report is
  yours.
- "Positive" and "In hospital" count everyone who ever was, not only those who are now.
- If the page says **The UKHSA report is not shown to you. Ask the Registered Manager.**, ask them
  to prepare it.
- The outbreak cannot be closed until its report is recorded as submitted or not required (see
  [How to close an outbreak](close-an-outbreak.md)).
- The rules: [safety specification, outbreaks](../../specs/safety.md#11-outbreaks-and-ipc-audits-slice-31-safe-013-migration-0093)
  and hazard [HZ-013](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-013-statutory-notification-missed-or-late).
