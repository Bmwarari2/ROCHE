# How to close a missing person episode

Record the welfare check and the debrief after a missing person is found, then close the episode.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner. Care and support workers, team leaders and senior carers can add to the timeline but cannot
record the welfare check or the debrief, or close the episode.

**Register row**: SAFE-015.

## Before you start

- The person must be recorded as **Found** on the timeline (see
  [How to keep a missing person's timeline](../care-worker/keep-a-missing-person-timeline.md)).
- Check the person straight away when they come back, as your procedure says. Record it afterwards.

## Steps

1. Choose **Missing persons** in the menu and open the episode by the person's name.
2. Under **Add to the timeline**, choose **What happened**: **Welfare check**. Enter **When it
   happened** and say in the **Note** what the welfare check found. Choose **Add to the timeline**.
3. When you have talked it through with the person and the staff involved, choose **What
   happened**: **Debrief**. Enter **When it happened** and say in the **Note** what the debrief
   found and what will change. Choose **Add to the timeline**.
4. Under **Close the episode**, read **Before you can close it:** if it shows. It lists what is
   still missing, for example the welfare check. Record those first.
5. When nothing is missing, choose **Close the episode**. The page says "Episode closed".

## Good to know

- Closing is final: the episode cannot be reopened. If the person goes missing again, open a new
  episode from a new incident.
- A person can have a new episode opened while a found one is still waiting for its welfare check
  and debrief.
- If the police were never called before the person was found, write a **Note** saying why, and
  tell the Registered Manager, before you close.
- Check under **Notifications** that what the incident needs is done in the notification centre.
  Closing the episode does not close any notification.
- After an episode, review the person's Herbert Protocol profile and risk assessment with them or
  their family (see [How to write a Herbert Protocol profile](write-a-herbert-protocol-profile.md)).
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-127](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-127-a-missing-persons-escalation-delayed-or-the-herbert-protocol-profile-out-of-date).
