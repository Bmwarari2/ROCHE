# How to keep a duty of candour record

When something goes wrong in a person's care and causes, or could cause, them harm, the law
(the duty of candour, regulation 20) says the home tells them, apologises and follows it up in
writing. Record each step on the incident.

**Who can do this**: people who manage incidents: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the
Registered Manager and the owner. Everyone who can read the incident sees where the record
stands.

**Register row**: SAFE-007.

## Record the threshold decision

1. Open the incident. Under **Duty of candour**, the page says whether Thapsus Care found the duty
   may apply, for example "Thapsus Care found the duty of candour may apply". This is a prompt, not
   a decision.
2. Under **Record the threshold decision**, choose **Who did it happen to?**: the person affected.
   Tell someone acting lawfully for them instead only when the hint on the page says so.
3. Answer **Is it a notifiable safety incident?** with **Yes** or **No**, as the health care
   professional judged it.
4. Under **When was the decision made?**, choose **Just now** or **Earlier** and enter the date and
   time.
5. Enter **Whose opinion does the decision record?**: the health care professional's name and
   role.
6. If your answer differs from Thapsus Care's finding, the page says so. Tick **I have checked the
   facts with the health care professional, and this is the decision**, and say **Why does the
   decision differ?**.
7. Choose **Save the decision**.
8. Move the record on: **Move on: tell the person**, or, if it is not notifiable, **Record as not
   notifiable** with **Why is it not notifiable?**.

## Tell the person

1. Under **Record telling the person in person**, enter **When were they told?** and **Who told
   them?** (one name and role per line).
2. Answer **Were they told in person?**, and tick **What were they told?**: **A true account of
   all the facts known**, **What further enquiries are planned** and **An apology**.
3. Choose the written record of the conversation, if it is on their record, and choose **Save
   telling them in person**.
4. If they cannot be contacted or decline to speak, use **Record an attempt to contact them** for
   every attempt: **When was the attempt?** and **What happened?**, then **Add the attempt**.
5. Move on: **Move on: written notification**, or, only when every attempt is recorded, **Record
   that they could not be told**.

## The written notification and after

1. Under **Record the written notification**, enter **When was it sent?**, tick **What did it
   include?**, choose the copy of the letter from their record, and choose **Save the written
   notification**. Then **Move on: enquiries open**.
2. Under **Record the results and correspondence**, enter **When were the results of the enquiries
   shared? (optional)**, tick the **Correspondence kept**, add an **Outcome (optional)**, and
   choose **Save the results and correspondence**.
3. When the results are shared and the correspondence is kept, choose **Close the record** and say
   **Why can it close?**.

## Important

- **Tell the person as soon as reasonably practicable.** The page shows the time for the written
  notification as your organisation's target, not a legal deadline.
- The incident cannot close until the duty of candour record is finished, or recorded as not
  notifiable (see [How to review and close an incident](../registered-manager/review-and-close-an-incident.md)).
- Letters and documents are chosen from the person's record. Send the letter from their letters
  page first, or add the document there.
- A record that says it is not notifiable, or that the person could not be told, can be reopened
  if new facts change that, for example a fracture confirmed later, or they can now be contacted.
  Choose **Reopen the record** and say **Why is it reopened?**.
- Thapsus Care never decides whether an incident is notifiable. The decision is the health care
  professional's, recorded with their name.
