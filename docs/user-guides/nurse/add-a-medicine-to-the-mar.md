# How to add a medicine to the MAR

Adding a medicine copies it from its source, such as a prescription or a pharmacy label, onto the
person's MAR. It stays "Not yet checked" until a second person checks it against the source.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners. Senior carers and care workers cannot, unless your organisation's own role adds it.

**Register row**: MEDS-001.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines** and then **Add a medicine**.
2. Check the person's name, date of birth, NHS number and allergies at the top of the form.
3. Under **Medicine**, type part of the name in **Find the medicine** and choose **Search
   medicines**. Under **Choose the medicine**, choose the exact product, form and strength on the
   source, for example "Examplomol 500 mg tablets".
4. Choose the **Route** and the **Dose unit**. Only what the NHS medicines list (dm+d) allows for
   that product is offered.
5. Under **What kind of dose is it?**, choose **An amount**, **A range, for example 1 to 2** or
   **Variable, as directed**.
6. Under **When is it given?**:
   - **At set times**: under **Schedule**, choose **How often**, then for each dose time fill in
     **Time** (24-hour clock, for example 08:00) and the amount. Choose **Add another dose time**
     for more, up to eight.
   - **When required**: fill in the amount, **Most in 24 hours**, and **Minimum time between doses,
     in minutes (optional)** if the prescription gives one. **What it is for** is needed.
7. Fill in **Prescriber** and **Starts**. **Stops**, **Review by**, **What it is for** and
   **Special instructions** are optional unless the source gives them.
8. Under **Where this comes from**, choose the **Kind of source** and fill in the **Source
   reference**, enough to find it again.
9. Fill in **Why doses earlier today may still be needed** only if it starts today and a dose due
   earlier today may still be given.
10. Choose **Add the medicine**. The medicine opens and says "Medicine added. It is not yet
    checked."
11. Ask a second person to check it (see [How to second-check a medicine](second-check-a-medicine.md)).

## Important

- **Copy the source exactly.** Thapsus Care keeps the dose exactly as you type it and never rounds
  or converts it. A number with a leading zero, such as 05, is refused (HZ-005).
- **Thapsus Care never decides a dose, an interaction or an allergy match.** It shows the person's
  allergies beside the form. You compare them (HZ-007).
- If you change the product, the route, unit and doses start again, so a dose typed for one
  strength is never carried to another.
- A dose time with no time is refused, never dropped.
- A when-required medicine needs the most in 24 hours. If the prescription or PRN protocol states
  none, ask the prescriber before adding it.
- If the page says the NHS medicines list (dm+d) is not loaded yet, no medicine can be added. Ask
  Thapsus Care support.
- To change a medicine already on the MAR, use a reconciliation (see
  [How to reconcile medicines](reconcile-medicines.md)). A change is a new version; the old one
  stays in the history.
