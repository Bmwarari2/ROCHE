# How to declare an outbreak

Record that an infection is spreading at your home, so the line list and the report to the health
protection team start. The health protection team is the local team of the UK Health Security
Agency (UKHSA).

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner, at the homes where they work. Care and support workers, team leaders or senior carers and
other staff cannot see outbreaks: tell the nurse or manager in charge when people are unwell.

**Register row**: SAFE-013.

## Before you start

- Follow your infection prevention and control (IPC) procedure first: isolate, use the right
  protective equipment and tell the person in charge.
- Thapsus Care may prompt you that a report looks needed, for example when several people at the
  home have had symptoms of a chest infection within a few days of each other. It never decides
  that there is an outbreak. You and your health protection team decide. If in doubt, report it.

## Steps

1. Choose **Outbreaks** in the menu. It lists outbreaks at your homes, newest first.
2. Go to **Declare an outbreak**.
3. Choose the **Home** if you work at more than one.
4. Under **Which illness**, choose **Acute respiratory infection**, **Flu**, **COVID-19**,
   **Diarrhoea and vomiting** or **Other**.
5. Enter the **Organism** if it is known, for example Norovirus. You can add it later.
6. Enter the **Health protection team reference** if the team has given you one. You can add it
   later.
7. Choose **Declare outbreak**. The page says "Outbreak declared" and the outbreak shows in the
   list as **Declared**.

## What happens next

- Thapsus Care drafts the outbreak's own report, "Outbreak report to the health protection team",
  in the notification centre. It is due without delay (see
  [How to fill in the outbreak report](fill-in-the-outbreak-report.md)).
- Add each resident who is unwell to the line list (see
  [How to keep an outbreak line list](keep-an-outbreak-line-list.md)).
- To add the organism or the reference later, open the outbreak and use **Organism and
  reference**, then **Save**.

## Good to know

- Only one outbreak of each illness can be open at a home at a time.
- Outbreaks can be declared for homes in England only. Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland follow
  later.
- Thapsus Care never reports the outbreak to UKHSA or the health protection team. People do.
- Only nurses, clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the owner at the home
  see the outbreak and its line list.
- The rules: [safety specification, outbreaks](../../specs/safety.md#11-outbreaks-and-ipc-audits-slice-31-safe-013-migration-0093)
  and hazards [HZ-013](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-013-statutory-notification-missed-or-late)
  and [HZ-011](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-011-cross-tenant-or-out-of-scope-record-exposure).
