# How to stop a medicine

Stopping a medicine takes it off the MAR from the time you give, on a prescriber's instruction.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners.

**Register row**: MEDS-001.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines** and choose the medicine.
2. Check the person and their allergies shown with the form.
3. Under **Stop this medicine**, fill in **Stops from**: the date and time it stops.
4. Fill in **Why it stops**.
5. Under **The instruction to stop**, choose the **Kind of source** and fill in the **Source
   reference**, for example the GP's letter and its date.
6. Choose **Stop** followed by the medicine's name, for example **Stop Examplomol 500 mg tablets**.
   The page says "Medicine stopped."

## Important

- **Only stop a medicine on a prescriber's instruction.** Thapsus Care never stops one for you,
  and never stops a medicine because it is missing from a list.
- The stopped medicine stays in the history with when and why it stopped. Nothing is deleted.
- To restart it, add it again (see [How to add a medicine to the MAR](add-a-medicine-to-the-mar.md)).
- If a later version of the medicine is waiting to start, Thapsus Care may ask **Why the later
  version is withdrawn**. Fill it in and choose the button again.
- If someone changed the medicine while you were on the page, the stop is refused. Reload the
  page and check again (HZ-004).
