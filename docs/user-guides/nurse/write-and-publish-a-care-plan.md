# How to write and publish a care plan

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, managers and the owner. Everyone who gives care can
read it.

**Register row**: CARE-001.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Care plan**.
2. Choose **Start a care plan**, or **Start a new draft from the plan in use**, or
   **Continue the draft**.
3. Open a need, for example "Mobility". Fill in the **Need**, the **Outcome, in the person's words**,
   **Strengths** and **If things change**. Choose **Save**.
4. Under **Add an action**, write **What staff do**, and who and when. Tick
   **Evidence is needed each time** if the round should ask for a record. Choose **Add action**.
5. Repeat for each need.
6. Choose **Publish the care plan**.
7. Under **Who agreed the plan?**, choose the person, a representative (with their name and
   authority), or say why no one could agree it.
8. Choose **Publish**. It is now the plan in use.

## Good to know

- A draft is not in use and creates no tasks. Follow the plan in use until the draft is published.
- Publishing replaces the plan in use. Earlier versions stay readable under **Versions** and
  **History**.
- A representative needs the right authority, for example a registered lasting power of attorney
  for health and welfare.
- **Reviews** shows when the next review is due (see [How to record a review](record-a-review.md)).
