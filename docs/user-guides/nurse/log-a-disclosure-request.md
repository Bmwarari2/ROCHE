# How to log a disclosure request

Record a request for a person's records from a coroner, a review, the police, a solicitor or under
the Access to Health Records Act, as soon as it arrives.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner, for people at the homes where they work. Other staff cannot see disclosure requests: pass
any request for records to the Registered Manager the same day.

**Register row**: SAFE-016.

## Before you start

- Check the request is genuine, as your procedure says, for example by calling the police station
  or the solicitor's firm on a number you find yourself.
- A request from the person themselves, or someone acting for a living person, is a data rights
  request, not a disclosure request. Pass it to the data protection lead.

## Steps

1. Choose **Disclosure requests** in the menu, then **Log a disclosure request**.
2. Choose **Who the records are about**. Check it is the right person.
3. Under **What kind of request**, choose **Coroner**, **Safeguarding Adults Review**, **Domestic
   Homicide Review**, **Police**, **Solicitor** or **Access to Health Records request**. The legal
   basis for disclosing follows from this.
4. Enter **Who asked** and **Their organisation**.
5. Enter **Their reference** if the request has one.
6. Under **What they need it for**, write what the request says, in its words. The police and
   solicitors must say what they need it for.
7. Enter **When the request was received**.
8. Choose **Log request**. The request opens, with the state **Requested**.

## Good to know

- The request is titled by its kind, the organisation and when it arrived, never by the person's
  name. The **Legal basis** shows in words under **The request**.
- An Access to Health Records request can only be about a person who has died. The page shows when
  the answer is due by law, counted from when the request was received, as "Answer due by" and
  the date, and "Overdue" once it has passed. Other kinds of request show no due date: answer by
  the date the requester gives, and tell the Registered Manager if you cannot.
- Nothing is released until each record is reviewed, the request is approved by someone who did not
  log it, and the pack is released (see
  [How to review the records for a disclosure request](review-records-for-a-disclosure-request.md)).
- To withdraw a request, open it, write **Why it is withdrawn** under **Withdraw the request** and
  choose **Withdraw the request**. Withdrawing is final: nothing is released.
- Every request, with its legal basis, approver and release, stays in the disclosure log. Opening
  the request and the person's records is recorded, with who did it.
- The rules: [safety specification, disclosure requests](../../specs/safety.md#12-disclosure-requests-slice-41-safe-016).
