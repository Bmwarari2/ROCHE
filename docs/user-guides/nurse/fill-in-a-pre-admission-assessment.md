# How to fill in a pre-admission assessment

Record what the assessment found, section by section, and who gave the information, so the
manager can decide whether the home can meet the person's needs.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner.

**Register row**: PEOP-005.

## Steps

1. Open the person from **Enquiries and pre-admission** and choose the **Pre-admission** tab.
   **Pre-admission assessment** shows the **Service sought**, the **Kind of placement sought** and
   **Progress**: how many required sections are done.
2. Under **Sections**, each section says **Done**, **Not applicable**, **In progress** or **Not
   started**. Choose the section's "Edit:" button.
3. Answer **Where is this section?**: **Done**, **Still finding out** or **Not applicable**.
4. Write **What was found** in plain words, as the team will read them. Say who told you where it
   matters. For **Not applicable**, write **Why this section does not apply**.
5. For **Current medicines**, add each one with **Add a medicine**: the **Medicine**, **Dose**,
   **How it is taken** and **How often**. Tick **Time-critical (for example for Parkinson's
   disease)** where it applies. If the person takes no medicines, say so in **What was found** and
   who confirmed it.
6. Choose **Save section**. The page says "Saved:" and the section's name.
7. Under **Where the information came from**, choose **Record sources and how the person took
   part**. Tick **Who gave the information**, answer **Did the person take part in their
   assessment?** (and, if **No**, **Why the person could not take part**), then choose **Save**.
8. When every required section, the sources and how the person took part are done, the
   assessment says **Ready for a decision**. Tell the manager (see
   [How to record a decision to admit](../registered-manager/record-a-decision-to-admit.md)).

## Important

- **Allergies, resuscitation, and eating and drinking are always recorded as what is known**,
  never marked not applicable. Record that there are none, if so, and who confirmed it
  (HZ-007, HZ-015, HZ-016).
- The allergies recorded here do not appear at the top of the person's record. Staff giving care
  see only what the top of the record shows, so tell the manager admitting the person what you
  recorded (HZ-007).
- Sections marked **Optional** are not needed for a decision.
- The hospital transfer pack prints the medicines list, marked to be checked, until the person's
  medicines record is in Thapsus Care.
