# How to record a ReSPECT plan or DNACPR decision

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, managers and the owner.

**Register row**: CARE-015.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Advance care**.
2. Under **Type of record**, choose **ReSPECT plan**, **DNACPR decision**, **Advance decision to
   refuse treatment (ADRT)** or **Advance care plan**.
3. For a ReSPECT plan, choose the CPR recommendation from section 4 of the form.
4. Enter the **Clinician's name** and **Clinician's role**, for example GP.
5. Enter the **Date completed** and the **Review date**.
6. Choose **Save record**. The header now shows the decision in force.
7. Check the header says what the signed form says.

## Withdraw a record

1. Choose **Withdraw this** record and say why it is withdrawn, and by whom.
2. Confirm. The header then shows "CPR decision not recorded" unless another decision is in force.
   Check it.

## Good to know

- The signed paper form stays with the person. Harbour records it; it does not replace it.
- A new record of the same type replaces the one in force. The old one stays in the history.
- If two records disagree, the header says **CPR records disagree**. Sort it out straight away.
