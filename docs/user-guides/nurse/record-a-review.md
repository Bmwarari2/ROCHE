# How to record a review or a change in need

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, managers and the owner. Care workers and senior
carers can read the dates but cannot record.

**Register row**: PEOP-008.

## See what is due

- For one person: open their record and choose **Reviews**.
- For everyone: go to **Residents** and choose **Reviews due**. Overdue reviews come first.

Harbour works out the due dates from your organisation's settings. You cannot set them by hand.

## Record a change in need

A change brings the care plan review forward.

1. Open the person's **Reviews**.
2. Under **Record a change in need**, choose **What changed**, for example "Fall" or "Return from
   hospital".
3. Enter the **Date of the change**.
4. Choose **Record the change**.

## Record a review

1. Under **Record a review**, choose the **Kind of review**: care plan, local authority, family
   involvement or trial placement.
2. Enter the **Date held**.
3. For family involvement, say how the family or representative was involved.
4. For a local authority review, add the **Council reference** if there is one.
5. Add **Who took part** (roles, not names) and the **Outcome**.
6. Choose **Record the review**.

## Good to know

- One care plan review covers every change waiting for it.
- Reviews are paused while the person is in hospital.
