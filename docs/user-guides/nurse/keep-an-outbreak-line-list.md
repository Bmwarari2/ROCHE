# How to keep an outbreak line list

Add each unwell resident to the outbreak's line list, and update them as their test results and
condition change. The line list feeds the figures for the report to the health protection team.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner, at the home with the outbreak. Care and support workers, team leaders or senior carers and
other staff cannot see the line list: tell the nurse in charge who is unwell.

**Register row**: SAFE-013.

## Add a resident

1. Choose **Outbreaks** in the menu and open the outbreak by its title, for example "Diarrhoea and
   vomiting, declared" and the date.
2. Go to **Add a resident to the line list**.
3. Choose the **Resident**. Check it is the right person.
4. Enter **When symptoms started**.
5. Under **Symptoms**, tick each one they have, for example **Fever**, **Diarrhoea** or **New
   confusion**. Tick at least one.
6. Choose the **Test result**: **Not tested**, **Waiting for the result**, **Positive** or
   **Negative**.
7. If they were tested, choose the **Kind of test**, for example **Lateral flow**, **PCR**
   (a laboratory swab test) or **Stool sample**, and enter **When they were tested**.
8. Answer **Vaccinated against flu this season** and **Vaccinated against COVID-19**: **Yes**,
   **No** or **Not known**.
9. Under **How they are now**, choose **Still unwell**, **Recovered**, **In hospital** or
   **Died**. Unless they are still unwell, enter **When that changed**.
10. Choose **Add to the line list**. The page says "Added to the line list" and the resident shows
    under **Line list** as **Current**.

## Update a resident

1. Under **Line list**, find the resident and choose **Update this case:** followed by their name.
2. Under **What changed**, choose:
   - **Their condition or test result changed**, for example a result came back or they
     recovered; or
   - **The row was wrong and needs correcting**, and write **Why the row was wrong**.
3. Change what is different and choose **Save the update**. The page says "Case updated".
4. The earlier row stays on the list, struck through and marked **Superseded by a later entry**.
   The new row is **Current**.

## Good to know

- Each resident is on the line list once. To change anything, update their row. Thapsus Care
  refuses a second row for the same person, so the report counts each person once.
- Nothing on the line list is deleted. A correction keeps the earlier row, struck through, with
  the reason.
- Nothing can be dated in the future, and a test cannot be dated before symptoms started.
- Only residents living at the home can be added. Members of staff cannot be added on this screen
  yet: tell the Registered Manager about staff who are unwell, and add staff figures to the report
  yourself.
- If the list says rows about residents outside your scope are counted but not listed, the report
  figures include them.
- The line list records what staff saw and what tests showed. Thapsus Care never diagnoses anyone or
  says who is infectious.
