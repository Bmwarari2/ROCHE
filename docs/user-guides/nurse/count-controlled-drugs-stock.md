# How to count controlled drugs stock

A stock count checks what is in the cupboard against the register's balance, with a witness.

**Who can do this**: senior carers (team leader or senior carer), nurses (clinical lead or nurse),
deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners, with a witness.

**Register row**: MEDS-005.

> **If the page says "Controlled drug recording is not turned on for this home"**, the paper
> controlled drugs register is the record at your home. The page still shows what Thapsus Care
> holds, which may be incomplete, but none of the forms in these steps, and Thapsus Care refuses
> anything recorded there. Record every receipt, dose given, transfer, disposal and stock count in
> the paper register, with a witness, as your home's policy says. One register is the record, so
> its balance is never split between paper and Thapsus Care (H-1517).

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Controlled drugs**. Each page shows when the next count is
   due, or **Count overdue since** a date, or **Not counted yet**.
2. Choose the drug.
3. Count what is in the cupboard with your witness watching. Your witness signs first, signed in as
   themselves, choosing **A stock count** under **What you saw** and the number counted.
4. Reload the page. Under **Count the stock**:
   - **How many you counted, in** the page's unit.
   - **If it does not match, why**: needed when the count does not match the register.
   - **Witness**: choose your witness's signature.
5. Choose **Record the count**. Confirm it is you if Thapsus Care asks.
6. Read the result under **Stock counts**: **Balanced**, **Short by** or **Over by** an amount.

## Important

- **A count never changes the balance on the register.** If it does not match, Thapsus Care raises
  a medication incident: "Count recorded. It does not match the register, so a medication incident
  has been raised." Tell the nurse or senior in charge (whoever your home gives medicines record management) or the manager (HZ-115).
- Once the difference is explained, put the register right with a correction (see
  [How to correct a controlled drugs register entry](correct-a-controlled-drugs-register-entry.md)).
- The count needs a different person as witness, and you confirm it is you with your passkey or
  authenticator app (HZ-116).
- A count that is overdue says so as a word and an icon on the register pages list. No task
  reminds anyone of an overdue count yet.
