# How to add, review or close a risk assessment

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, managers and the owner. Others can read them.

**Register row**: CARE-002.

## Add one

1. Open the person's record and choose **Risk assessments**.
2. Under **Add a risk assessment**, choose the **Template**, for example "Bed rails", and choose
   **Start the assessment**.
3. Say **What is the hazard?** and **Who is at risk?**
4. Answer the **Questions from the template**.
5. Choose how likely and how bad the harm would be, without and with the controls. These are your
   judgements: Harbour works out the rating from them.
6. Tick the **Controls in place** and add any others.
7. Write the **Positive risk statement** and the person's views.
8. Set the **Date assessed** and **Review by**, then choose **Save risk assessment**.

## Review or close it

- Choose **Review this risk assessment**, change what is needed and choose **Save the review**.
- If it no longer applies, choose **Close this risk assessment**, say why, and confirm. It stays in
  the history.

## Good to know

- Falls risk is not rated: NICE advises against it (NG249).
- For bed rails, a measurement outside the MHRA figure shows a warning. Check the bed rails now.
- Every earlier version is kept: choose **View the history of this risk assessment**.
