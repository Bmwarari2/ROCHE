# How to change or withdraw a homely remedy protocol

Change a protocol when it has been agreed again, for example at its review. Withdraw it when the
home should stop using it.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners.

**Register row**: MEDS-007.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines stock** in the menu and choose **Homely remedy protocols for this home**.
2. Find the protocol. Its state shows as an icon and words: "In use", "Review overdue: not
   to be used until reviewed" or "Withdrawn".
3. To change it, choose **Change the protocol for** the remedy:
   - Every figure is shown as it stands. Change what was agreed again, including **Review by**
     and **Staff allowed to give it**.
   - Fill in **Why it is changing**. A new reason is needed each time.
   - Choose **Change the protocol**. The list says **Protocol changed**.
4. To withdraw it, choose **Withdraw the protocol for** the remedy:
   - Fill in **Why it is being withdrawn**.
   - Choose **Withdraw the protocol**. The list says **Protocol withdrawn**.

## Important

- Doses already given keep the protocol they were checked against.
- **A withdrawn protocol is never used again.** It cannot be changed. Doses given under it stay on
  each person's record.
- Staff named on the protocol whom you cannot see in the staff list are shown as "A member of staff
  named on the protocol whom you cannot list", so a change never drops them without you seeing it.
- If someone changed the protocol while you were on the page, your change is refused. Reload and
  check first (HZ-120).
