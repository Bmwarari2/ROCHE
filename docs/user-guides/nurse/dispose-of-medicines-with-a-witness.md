# How to dispose of medicines with a witness

Medicines that are refused, spoilt, out of date or stopped are disposed of and recorded, with a
second member of staff as witness.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners. The witness is another of them at the same home.

**Register row**: MEDS-008.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines**, then **Medicines stock**, and choose the
   medicine.
2. Under **Dispose of medicines**:
   - **Quantity in** the medicine's unit.
   - **Why they are disposed of**, for example **Out of date**.
   - **Note**, optional.
   - Under **Who witnessed it**, choose **A witness, who will confirm it** and choose the
     **Witness**. Or, only if your organisation allows it, choose **No one could witness it** and
     fill in **Why no one could witness it**, in at least 10 characters.
3. Choose **Record disposal**. The page says "Disposal recorded and waiting for the witness to
   confirm it".
4. The witness gets a task. They open the medicine's stock, choose **Confirm this disposal as its
   witness** beside the disposal, check what was disposed of and choose **I witnessed this
   disposal**.

## Important

- **The stock leaves as soon as the disposal is recorded.** It cannot be undone; a mistake is put
  right with a stock count.
- The witness is never the person who recorded it. They confirm it themselves, signed in as
  themselves, and only their confirmation closes their task.
- If you cannot see the staff list, the form says to ask a manager to record a witnessed disposal.
- **Controlled drugs are not disposed of here.** Record their disposal or destruction on the
  controlled drugs register, with a witness: the paper register until controlled drug recording
  is turned on for your home (see
  [How to record a controlled drug received, returned, disposed of or destroyed](record-a-controlled-drug-movement.md)).
- Your organisation's policy decides whether a disposal may go without a witness. Thapsus Care
  says so when it may not.
