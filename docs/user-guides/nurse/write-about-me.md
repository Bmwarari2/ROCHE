# How to write About me

Write, with the person, what matters to them and how they like to be supported, in their own
words. About me goes with them to hospital.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner. Everyone who can open the person's record can read it.

**Register row**: PEOP-003.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **About me**. It says how many of the 7 parts are written.
2. Choose **Write About me**, or **Change About me** if it has been written before.
3. Under **Who wrote it**, choose whether the person wrote it in their own words, on their own,
   or with support. If they had support, enter **Supported to write this by**: who helped, and how
   they know them.
4. Fill in the parts in their words, for example **What is most important to me**, **How I
   communicate and how to communicate with me** and **Please do and please do not**. Leave a part
   blank if there is nothing to say. Every part is optional.
5. Choose **Save About me**. The page says "About me saved".

## Good to know

- Pictures and recordings already added to a part are kept when you change the words.
- To share About me through a link, see
  [How to record a sharing decision](../registered-manager/record-a-sharing-decision.md).
