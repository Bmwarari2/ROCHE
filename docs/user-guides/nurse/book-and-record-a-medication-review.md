# How to book and record a medication review

A medication review is a clinician's review of all the person's medicines, such as the annual
review by the GP or pharmacist. Thapsus Care tracks when it is due and records what the clinician
said.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners.

**Register row**: MEDS-013.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines** and then **Medication reviews**.
2. Read **Where the reviews stand**: **Next due**, **Last done**, **Booked for** and **Due by the
   interval**, with the status in words.
3. To book one, under **Book a review**, fill in **Date the review is due** and choose **Book the
   review**.
4. When the review has happened, under **Reviews**, open **Record, move or cancel the review due
   on** its date. Under **What do you want to do?**:
   - **Record it as done**: fill in **Date it was done**, **Clinician**, **Their role** and
     **Outcome** in the clinician's words. Choose **Record the review**.
   - **Move it to another date**: fill in **New date it is due** and **Why is it moving?**. Choose
     **Move the review**.
   - **Cancel it**: fill in **Why is it cancelled?**. Choose **Cancel the review**.
5. To correct a review already done, open **Correct the review done on** its date, change what is
   wrong, fill in **Why are you correcting it?** and choose **Save the correction**.

## Important

- **Thapsus Care suggests no change to a medicine.** The outcome is the clinician's words. Any
  change to a medicine is made on the medicines record, by reconciliation, and checked by a second
  person (see [How to reconcile medicines](reconcile-medicines.md)).
- If the booked date is later than your organisation's review interval allows, the page says
  "The booked review is later than the review interval allows." (HZ-134).
- A review cannot be recorded as done on a date still to come.
- If someone changed the review while you were on the page, your change is refused. Reload and
  check first.
- The manager sees everyone at the home in the **Medication review tracker** (see
  [How to check the medication review tracker](../registered-manager/check-the-medication-review-tracker.md)).
