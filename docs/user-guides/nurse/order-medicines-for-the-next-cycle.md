# How to order medicines for the next cycle

Medicines are ordered from the pharmacy once a cycle, usually every 28 days. Each order lists, for
each person, the medicines on their MAR and how much to order.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners. Staff who give medicines can see the orders but not change them.

**Register row**: MEDS-008.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines stock** in the menu. If Thapsus Care asks, choose your home.
2. Under **Ordering cycle**, check **Cycle length**, **A cycle started on** and **Next cycle starts
   on**. If no cycle is set, fill in **First day of a cycle** with the day any one cycle starts and
   choose **Save cycle**.
3. Under **Orders**, choose **Start an order**.
4. Check **First day of the cycle this order is for**. The next cycle is filled in.
5. Under **Medicines to order**, type a quantity for each medicine to order, in its unit. Leave a
   medicine empty to not order it.
6. Choose **Start order**. The order opens as a draft.
7. Check every line under **Medicines on this order**.
8. Under **Submit the order**, choose **How it is sent**: **Through the pharmacy, if it is
   connected** or **By hand, sent another way**. Choose **Submit order**.
9. The order says when and how it was sent, and the pharmacy's reference when there is one.

## Order steps

Each order shows its step as an icon and a word: **Draft**, **Submitted**, **Part received**,
**Received** or **Cancelled**. Lines fill in as medicines are received against them (see
[How to receive, return and count medicines stock](receive-return-and-count-medicines-stock.md)).

To cancel an order not yet fully received, open it and under **Cancel the order** choose **Cancel
order**. It cannot be undone.

## Important

- **Controlled drugs are never ordered here.** Thapsus Care refuses a controlled drug on an order.
  Their stock is kept in the controlled drugs register.
- Each line must be the person's own medicine at this home.
- Thapsus Care raises a "Medicines order due" task before each cycle starts, unless the home
  already has an order for it.
- The pharmacy receives each medicine, quantity and unit, never a name. If the pharmacy is not
  connected, Thapsus Care says so: send it by hand.
- Submitting needs access to everyone at the home, so no one's medicines are left out unseen. If
  some people's medicines could not be read, the page says they are not listed.
- The cycle length comes from your organisation's settings.
