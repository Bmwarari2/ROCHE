# How to record a controlled drug received, returned, disposed of or destroyed

The controlled drugs register has one page for each controlled drug, form and strength for a
person. Every movement is recorded on it with a second person as witness, and the register keeps
the running balance.

**Who can do this**: senior carers (team leader or senior carer), nurses (clinical lead or nurse),
deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners. The witness is another member of staff who can
witness controlled drugs.

**Register row**: MEDS-005.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Controlled drugs**.
2. Under **Register pages**, choose the drug. If it has no page yet, under **Open a register page**
   choose the **Medicine on the MAR chart** and choose **Open the register page**.
3. Check **In stock** at the top of the page, and read the witness rule for your organisation
   under it.
4. Do the movement with your witness watching. Your witness signs first, in their own session
   (see [How to sign as a controlled drugs witness in the browser](../care-worker/sign-as-a-controlled-drugs-witness.md)).
5. Reload the page. Under **Record a movement**:
   - **What happened**: **Received**, **Returned to the pharmacy**, **Disposed of** or
     **Destroyed**.
   - **How much, in** the page's unit.
   - **When it happened**: leave it empty if it has just happened.
   - **From or to**, for example the pharmacy's name, and the **Reference**, for example the
     delivery note number.
   - **Witness**: choose your witness's signature. It shows their name, what they saw, the
     quantity and how long it lasts.
6. Choose **Record it**. If Thapsus Care asks you to confirm it is you, use your passkey or a code
   from your authenticator app, then come back to the page and try again.
7. The page says "Recorded on the register." Check the new entry and **In stock after**.

## If no witness is available

Where your organisation allows it, you may choose **No witness was available** and fill in **Why
no witness was available**. The entry is flagged "No witness: for manager review". A destruction
always needs a witness.

## Important

- **Controlled drugs need two different people and step-up confirmation.** You cannot use your own
  signature, and a witness signature can be used only by the person it names, once, for a short
  time (HZ-116).
- **The signature must match what you record**: the movement and the quantity. If it does not,
  Thapsus Care refuses the entry (HZ-116).
- If no signature is waiting, the form says "No witness has signed for you on this page yet." Ask
  your witness to open the page, signed in as themselves, and sign for what they see.
- **The balance is the register's.** Thapsus Care keeps the running balance; it is never added up
  on your screen (HZ-115).
- **Corrections are new entries, never edits.** See
  [How to correct a controlled drugs register entry](correct-a-controlled-drugs-register-entry.md)
  (HZ-115).
- Only a controlled drug can have a register page. Thapsus Care says if the medicine is not one.
- Giving a controlled drug to the person is not recorded on the web yet. It is recorded on the
  phone, with its witness. Each dose given that way appears on the register as **Given**.
