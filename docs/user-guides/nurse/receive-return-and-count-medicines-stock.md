# How to receive, return and count medicines stock

Each medicine kept for a person has a stock item with a balance. Doses given take stock out;
medicines received, returned and counted are recorded here.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners. Staff who give medicines can see the balances but not change them.

**Register row**: MEDS-008.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Medicines** and then **Medicines stock**.
2. If the medicine has no stock item yet, under **Open a stock item** choose the **Medicine** and
   choose **Open stock item**.
3. Under **Stock items**, choose the medicine. Its balance and **Latest movements** are shown.
4. To record medicines received, under **Record medicines received**:
   - **Quantity in** the medicine's unit.
   - **Order line it fills**: choose the order line, or **Not for an order**.
   - Choose **Record receipt**.
5. To record medicines returned, under **Return to the pharmacy**, fill in the quantity and choose
   **Record return**.
6. To record a count, under **Count the stock**:
   - **How many** tablets (or the medicine's unit) **you counted**, 0 or more.
   - **Why the count differs from the balance**: needed only when the count is not the balance
     shown.
   - Choose **Record count**.

## Important

- **The balance is Thapsus Care's count**, never added up on your screen. **Below nought, count
  needed** means the balance has gone below 0, so the records and the shelf do not agree.
  Count the stock. Thapsus Care also raises a "Medicines stock count needed" task for the nurse in
  charge (HZ-003).
- A count that differs from the balance changes the balance to your count, with your reason.
  Nothing else is changed.
- **Controlled drugs are not counted here.** They are listed under **Controlled drugs** with the
  register's balance and a link to the register page. Where controlled drug recording is not
  turned on for your home, the list says "This balance is from the controlled drugs register in
  Thapsus Care, which is not the record at this home. Use the paper controlled drugs register for
  this medicine's stock."
- A dose recorded as "Not available" because it is out of stock raises an urgent task for the
  nurse in charge (HZ-003).
- A receipt more than the order line asked for is allowed and shown as "more than ordered".
- Stock has no expiry date in Thapsus Care yet. Check expiry dates on the packs.
- To dispose of medicines, see
  [How to dispose of medicines with a witness](dispose-of-medicines-with-a-witness.md).
