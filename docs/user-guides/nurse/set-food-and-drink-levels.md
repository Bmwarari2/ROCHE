# How to set food and drink levels and the fluid target

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, managers and the owner.

**Register row**: CARE-010.

## Steps

1. Open the person's record and choose **Charts**.
2. Under **Chart settings**, choose the **Prescribed food level (IDDSI)** and
   **Prescribed drink level (IDDSI)**.
3. If a level changes, enter **Who advised the IDDSI levels**, for example the speech and
   language therapist.
4. Enter a **Daily fluid target** if one is set (500 to 4,000 ml).
5. If a clinician has decided NEWS2 should use SpO2 scale 2, tick it and name the clinician.
6. Choose **Save chart settings**.

## Good to know

- The levels show on the person's header, the round and the downtime pack.
- Staff who give a different level must give a reason, and you are alerted if it is less safe.
