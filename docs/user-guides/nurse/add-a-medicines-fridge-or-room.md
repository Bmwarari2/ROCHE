# How to add a medicines fridge or room

Each medicines fridge and each medicines room or cupboard is added once, so staff can record its
temperatures.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners.

**Register row**: MEDS-008.

## Steps

1. Go to **Medicines stock** in the menu. If Thapsus Care asks, choose your home.
2. Under **Storage and temperatures**, go to **Add a fridge or room**.
3. Under **What it is**, choose **Medicines fridge** or **Medicines room or cupboard**.
4. Fill in **Name**, 2 to 80 characters, so staff can find it, for example "Ground floor treatment
   room fridge".
5. Choose **Add storage**. The page says **Storage added**, and it is listed under **Storage and
   temperatures**.

## Good to know

- The temperature range for each kind comes from your organisation's settings, never from the
  name.
- Staff then record readings against it (see
  [How to record a medicines fridge or room temperature](../care-worker/record-a-medicines-fridge-or-room-temperature.md)).
  An out-of-range reading raises an urgent task for the nurse or senior in charge (whoever your home gives medicines record management) (HZ-132).
