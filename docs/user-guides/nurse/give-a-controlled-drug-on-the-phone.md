# How to give a controlled drug on the phone

A controlled drug given on the phone needs two signatures on the same phone: the witness's PIN,
then yours. It works without a connection.

**Who can do this**: staff who may record controlled drugs: team leaders or senior carers, nurses,
deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners, with current medicines competence and a medicines
PIN on this phone. Care or support workers can be the witness but cannot give or record a
controlled drug. Agency or bank workers can do neither.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002, MEDS-005.

> **Where your home still uses the paper controlled drugs register**, the phone says so at the top
> of a controlled drug's dose and does not let you record it as given: record it in the paper
> register and on the paper MAR, with a witness. It says when it last checked; if the home may have
> changed since, connect and open the dose again. If the phone has never been able to check, it
> says so: do not give the dose until the nurse in charge confirms which register to use. If the
> home changed while the phone was offline, Thapsus Care refuses the dose when it arrives and the
> nurse in charge gets an urgent review (H-1517).

## Steps

1. Before the round, while there is a connection, make sure you and your witness each have a
   medicines PIN on this phone: see
   [How to set a medicines PIN on the phone](../care-worker/set-a-medicines-pin-on-the-phone.md).
2. Check the sync banner and open the dose as usual: see
   [How to give medicines on the phone](../care-worker/give-medicines-on-the-phone.md). Choose
   **Given**, do the identity check, scan the pack and enter the **Amount given**.
3. Under **Controlled drug: two signatures**, choose the witness: **Witness:** and their name.
4. Give the dose with the witness watching. The witness types their PIN in **Witness's PIN** and
   chooses **Witness signs now**. The phone says **Witnessed by** their name, then **Now record it
   with your PIN.**
5. Type your own PIN in **Your PIN**.
6. Choose **Record dose** straight away. The phone says **Recorded on this phone. It will be sent
   when there is a connection.**

### When no one is here to witness

This is offered only where the home runs only home care, supported living, live-in care, complex
care or day services. At a care home, or where the phone does not know the home's services, a
witness is always needed and the phone says so.

1. Choose **No one is here to witness**.
2. Fill in **Why no one could witness**.
3. Type **Your PIN** and choose **Record dose**. Choose **A witness is here** to go back.

A manager reviews every dose recorded without a witness.

## Important

- **If you may not give controlled drugs, the phone stops you before you start.** At the top of the
  dose it says **You cannot give or record controlled drugs. Do not give this dose: ask someone who
  can record controlled drugs. If it has already been given, tell the nurse in charge now. You can
  still record that it was refused or not given.** It never gets as far as the signatures. A
  medicine the phone cannot place yet counts as a controlled drug (HZ-116).
- **The witness must be a different person from you.** The phone never offers you as your own
  witness, and Thapsus Care refuses a dose where the witness is the recorder (HZ-116).
- **The witness signs first, as they see the dose given.** That fixes the amount and the time. If
  you change the amount afterwards, the witness signs again. If you change what happened, the
  witness's signature is cleared.
- **Offline**: the dose and both signatures are kept on the phone and sent at the next
  connection. Until then the dose is not on Thapsus Care, and the register is written only when
  Thapsus Care has checked both signatures, the witness's standing and competence. Until then the
  register balance does not show this dose (HZ-115).
- **If Thapsus Care refuses the dose** (for example, the witness could not witness for this person,
  their competence had lapsed, the home needs a witness, or controlled drug recording was turned off
  while the phone was offline), it shows under **Not saved: needs attention**. Thapsus Care keeps a copy and sends the nurse
  in charge an urgent review. The register is not changed, so the next stock count will show a
  difference. The dose has already been given: tell the nurse in charge at once (HZ-115).
- If a PIN does not match, the phone says so. After 6 wrong PINs in a row it makes that person
  wait a little before they try again.
- A controlled drug's record is not corrected on the phone, and the web has no screen for
  correcting a dose record yet: tell the nurse in charge. Stock counts are on the web: see
  [How to count controlled drugs stock](count-controlled-drugs-stock.md).
- A controlled drug taken by the person themselves or given by family is not recorded on the phone
  or the web yet. Tell the nurse in charge, who records it with the register.
- Each dose, and its register entry, is recorded, with who did it.
