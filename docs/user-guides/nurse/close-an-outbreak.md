# How to close an outbreak

Record that an outbreak is over, once the health protection team agrees.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner, at the home with the outbreak.

**Register row**: SAFE-013.

## Before you start

- The health protection team, the local team of the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA), must agree
  that the outbreak is over. Thapsus Care never decides it and sets no waiting period of its own.
- The outbreak's report must be recorded as submitted or not required in the notification centre
  (see [How to fill in the outbreak report](fill-in-the-outbreak-report.md)). Until then, closing is
  refused.

## Steps

1. Choose **Outbreaks** in the menu and open the outbreak.
2. Go to **Close the outbreak**. It says how many people on the line list are still unwell.
3. If anyone is still unwell, check it is right, then tick the box that says you know, for example
   **I know 2 people are still unwell**. Update the line list first if someone has recovered.
4. Enter the **Date the outbreak ended**. It cannot be before the outbreak was declared, before
   the last person's symptoms started, or in the future.
5. Under **Why it is closed**, say who at the health protection team agreed and when.
6. Choose **Close the outbreak**. The page says "Outbreak closed" and shows the date and the
   reason under **Outbreak closed**.

## Good to know

- Closing is final: the outbreak cannot be reopened and its line list cannot change. If people
  become unwell again, declare a new outbreak.
- People still unwell never stop you closing, but you must say you know about them.
- If closing is refused, the page says why, for example that the report is still a draft.
- The rules: [safety specification, outbreaks](../../specs/safety.md#11-outbreaks-and-ipc-audits-slice-31-safe-013-migration-0093)
  and hazard [HZ-013](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-013-statutory-notification-missed-or-late).
