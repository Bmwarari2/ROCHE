# How to record an incident investigation

Record who is looking into an incident, what they found, the lessons learned and the actions that
follow.

**Who can do this**: people who manage incidents: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the
Registered Manager and the owner.

**Register row**: SAFE-010.

## Steps

1. Open the incident and go to **Investigation**. It says **In progress** or **Completed**, or that
   none is recorded yet.
2. Under **Record the investigation**, choose **Who leads it?** and the **Method**, for example
   **Root cause analysis**, **Five whys** or **After action review**.
3. Write the **Findings**: what happened and why, as far as it is known.
4. List the **Contributing factors**, one per line, for example staffing, equipment or the
   environment.
5. Write the **Lessons learned**: what will change, in plain words, so others can learn from it.
6. To add an action, under **Add an action** write **What needs doing?**, choose **Who will do
   it?** and **When is it due?**. The person who will do it must be someone who manages
   incidents at that location. Add one action at a time: save, then add the next.
7. Choose **Save the investigation**. When the findings and lessons are written, choose **Save and
   complete it**.

## Good to know

- An action with an owner and a date becomes a task for them: the action says "A task was made
  for its owner". Each action says **Open**, **Open: past its date**, **Done** or **Cancelled**.
- Tick **Mark the lessons as published in a lessons-learned bulletin** once you have shared them.
  Thapsus Care records the date but does not send the bulletin or track who read it yet.
- An investigation and its actions can carry on after the incident is closed. Choose **Save and
  reopen it** if it needs more work.
- To see how many incidents of each type happened each month, see
  [How to read incident trends](../care-worker/read-incident-trends.md).
