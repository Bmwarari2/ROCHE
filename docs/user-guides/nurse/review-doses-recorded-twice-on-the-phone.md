# How to review doses recorded twice on the phone

> Arrives with the phone release from `feat/mobile-meds-cd-offline` (slices 69 and 70); not on main yet.

When two phones record the same dose, Thapsus Care keeps both records and asks the nurse in charge
to decide what happened.

**Who can do this**: nurses and managers. Care workers and senior carers do not see this screen.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002, MEDS-005.

## Steps

You need a connection for this screen.

1. Go to the **Medicines** tab and choose **Doses recorded on two phones**.
2. Each card shows the medicine and the person, when the dose was due, the "First record" and the
   "Second record", whether the records agree, and whether one member or two recorded them.
3. Find out what happened: ask the staff who recorded it, check the person and the stock.
4. Fill in **What you found out**.
5. Choose one:
   - **Same dose sent twice: the first record stands**, when it was given once.
   - **The dose was given twice**, when it was given twice. This raises a medication incident.
6. Read what the decision does, then choose **Confirm: the first record stands** or **Raise
   medication incident**. Choose **Keep reviewing** to go back.
7. The card says **Resolved**.

If the first record itself is wrong, correct it on the web and resolve the conflict there.

## Important

- **Nothing is merged on its own.** The second record never becomes part of the MAR without your
  decision. Until you decide, the MAR shows only the first record (HZ-004).
- **Both records may say the same thing and the dose may still have been given twice.** Check
  before you decide. The phone never suggests which is likely.
- A review task comes to the nurse in charge for each conflict. It is urgent when the dose is
  time-critical or the records disagree.
- A conflict is decided once and cannot be changed afterwards.
- If you recorded or sent the dose yourself, you can still decide, so a conflict never waits
  overnight. The Registered Manager then gets a review of your decision.
- **Controlled drugs**: someone who had no part in the dose must decide, and Thapsus Care asks for
  a fresh second sign-in step before it takes your decision. If you cannot confirm it on the
  phone, decide on the web instead. When a
  controlled drug was given twice, an urgent task asks for the second dose to be entered on the
  register, witnessed.
- Only doses Thapsus Care has already received show here. A dose still waiting on a phone cannot
  conflict yet.
- If some homes' doses could not be read, the screen says so: check the connection and open it
  again.
