# How to review doses recorded twice on the phone

When two phones record the same dose, Thapsus Care keeps both records and asks the nurse in charge
to decide what happened.

**Who can do this**: nurses, deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners. Care or support
workers, team leaders or senior carers and agency or bank workers do not see this screen.

**Register rows**: MOB-007, MEDS-002.

## Steps

You need a connection for this screen.

1. Go to the **Medicines** tab and choose **Doses recorded on two phones**.
2. Each card shows the medicine and the person, when the dose was due, **First record:** and
   **Second record:** in words, whether the records agree, and whether one member of staff or two
   recorded them.
3. Find out what happened: ask the staff who recorded it, and check the person and the stock.
4. Fill in **What you found out**. You can choose a decision only once it is filled in.
5. Choose one:
   - **Same dose sent twice: the first record stands**, when it was given once.
   - **The dose was given twice**, when it was given twice. This raises a medication incident.
6. Read what the decision does, then choose **Confirm: the first record stands** or **Raise
   medication incident**. Choose **Keep reviewing** to go back.
7. The card says **Resolved**. If Thapsus Care does not accept the decision, the card says why.

## Important

- **Nothing is merged on its own.** The second record never becomes part of the MAR without your
  decision. Until you decide, the MAR shows only the first record (HZ-004).
- **Both records may say the same thing and the dose may still have been given twice.** The card
  says so. Check before you decide. The phone never suggests which is likely: it shows, you decide.
- **The decision must fit the records.** Thapsus Care accepts **The dose was given twice** only
  when both records say the dose was taken, and refuses **Same dose sent twice: the first record
  stands** when the second record says given but the first does not. Then the first record needs
  correcting: the card says to do that on the web, but the web has no screen for it yet. Tell the
  Registered Manager.
- **Controlled drugs are not decided on the phone.** The card says **This is a controlled drug.**
  and to decide in Thapsus Care on the web, where you confirm it is you with your passkey or
  authenticator app. Someone who did not record, give or send the dose must decide, and they need
  permission to record controlled drugs. The web has no screen for this yet: tell the Registered
  Manager. When a controlled drug was given twice, an urgent task asks for the second dose to be
  entered on the register, witnessed.
- A medicine this phone does not hold is treated the same way: **This medicine is not on this
  phone.**
- A review task goes to the nurse in charge for each conflict. It is urgent when the dose is
  time-critical or the records disagree.
- A conflict is decided once and cannot be changed afterwards. The decision is recorded, with who
  did it.
- If you recorded, gave or sent the dose yourself, you can still decide a medicine that is not a
  controlled drug, so a conflict never waits overnight. The Registered Manager then gets a task to
  review your decision (the owner, if you are the Registered Manager).
- Only doses Thapsus Care has already received show here. A dose still waiting on a phone cannot
  conflict yet.
- If some homes' doses could not be read, the screen says **Some homes' doses could not be read.
  Check the connection and open this screen again.**
