# How to keep a death record

Record the clinician's judgement and the steps after a person we support dies, so nothing is
missed, then close the record.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner. Care and support workers, agency or bank workers, team leaders or senior carers, care
coordinators and the quality and compliance lead can read death records for people they can see,
but cannot change them.

**Register row**: SAFE-014.

## Before you start

- The death must be recorded on the person's **Leaving** tab first (see
  [How to record a death](../senior-carer/record-a-death.md)). The death record then opens by
  itself. If it is not under **Deaths** in the menu, go to the person's **Leaving** tab and choose
  **Open the death record**, then **Open the death record** again on the next page.

## Open the record

1. Choose **Deaths** in the menu. Records are titled "Died" and the date and time, with the
   person's name and communication needs beside them, open ones first.
2. Each shows its state, whether the **Mortality review** is due or overdue, and how many steps are
   still to do.
3. Open the record by its title.

## Record the clinician's judgement

1. Under **The clinician's judgement**, answer **Was the death expected?** and **May the cause be
   unnatural or suspicious?** with what the clinician said. Do not answer from your own view.
2. Under **Whose judgement this is**, write the clinician's name and role, for example "Dr Ada
   Example, GP".
3. Choose **Record the judgement**. The page says "Judgement recorded".

Until both answers are recorded, the page says so at the top, and Thapsus Care treats the death as
unexpected and possibly unnatural. That means **Police told** and **Room and equipment left as
they are** stay on the list and can only be recorded as done.

## Record each step

The steps are listed under **Steps**, for example **Verification of death**, **Family told**,
**Police told** and **Room and equipment left as they are**. Each says **Still to do**, **Done**
or **Not needed**.

1. Under the step, choose **What happened**: **Done** or **Not needed**.
2. Enter **When** it happened. It cannot be before the death or in the future.
3. Write a **Note**. If the step was not needed, say why, in at least 20 characters.
4. For **Police told**, enter the **Police reference**.
5. Choose **Record step**. The page says "Step recorded" and the step shows under **History**.

**Death notification to the Care Quality Commission**, **Medical examiner information** and
**LeDeR notification** are not recorded here. (LeDeR is the NHS programme that learns from the
lives and deaths of people with a learning disability and autistic people.) Each says "This is
done in the notification centre, not here." and links to it with its status, for example "draft"
or "submitted" (see
[How to follow up after a death](../registered-manager/follow-up-after-a-death.md)).

## Close the record

1. Record the mortality review first (see [How to record a mortality review](record-a-mortality-review.md)).
2. Under **Close the death record**, read the list under **The record cannot be closed yet:** if it
   shows. Record each thing it names.
3. When nothing is left, choose **Close the death record**. The page says "Death record closed".

## Important

- Whether a death was expected, and whether its cause may be unnatural, is the clinician's
  judgement. Thapsus Care records it and never decides it.
- If the death may be unnatural, or the clinician has not said yet, tell the police and leave the
  room and equipment as they are, as your procedure says.
- Thapsus Care never sends anything to the Care Quality Commission, the medical examiner, the
  coroner, LeDeR or the police. People do, then record it.
- A step cannot be changed or removed once recorded, and once it is **Done** or **Not needed** its
  form goes from the page. Check the time and note before you choose **Record step**. If one was
  recorded wrongly, write a note on the person's record naming the step and what was wrong, and
  tell the Registered Manager.
- Closing is final: the death record cannot be reopened.
- If a death was recorded in error and corrected on the person's record, the death record is
  withdrawn, never deleted, and says why.
- The rules: [safety specification, deaths and missing persons](../../specs/safety.md#deaths-and-missing-persons-plan-slice-37-safe-014-safe-015)
  and hazard [HZ-013](../../clinical-safety/hazard-log.md#hz-013-statutory-notification-missed-or-late).
