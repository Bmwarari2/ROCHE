# How to decide a dose recorded twice

When two phones record the same dose offline, Thapsus Care keeps both records and asks the nurse in
charge what happened. Nothing is merged on its own. The phone sends you here for a controlled
drug's dose, and when the record that stands is itself wrong.

**Who can do this**: nurses (clinical lead or nurse), deputy managers, Registered Managers and
owners: anyone who manages the medicines record. A controlled drug's dose also needs someone who
records controlled drugs.

**Register row**: MEDS-002.

## Steps

1. Open **Medicines round** for the home, then choose **Doses recorded twice**.
2. Each dose shows the person, the medicine, the record that stands and the second record in words,
   and whether they agree. Choose **Review and decide** for one.
3. Find out what happened: ask the staff who recorded it and check the person and the stock.
4. If the record that stands is wrong, choose **Correct the record that stands** first:
   - choose what happened, the amount and when;
   - say why you are correcting it;
   - choose **Save correction**. You come back to the dose with **The record was wrong and has
     been corrected** chosen.
5. Under **Decide what happened**, choose one:
   - **Same dose sent twice: the first record stands**;
   - **The dose was given twice**: Thapsus Care asks again, then raises a medication incident;
   - **The record was wrong and has been corrected**.
6. Under **What you found out**, say what you found out, then choose **Save decision**.

## A controlled drug

- You confirm it is you with your passkey or authenticator app. If Thapsus Care asks, sign in
  again and you come back to the same page.
- Someone who recorded, gave or corrected the dose cannot decide it. Ask a colleague who took no
  part.
- A correction that changes the register needs a witness. The page says what they sign for, for
  example "a dose given of 1 capsule", and gives a link. Your witness opens the link, signed in as
  themselves, and chooses **Sign as witness**. Then choose **Check for the signature**, choose
  their signature and save.
- Until your home's electronic controlled drugs register is turned on, the paper register is the
  record (H-1517).

## Important

- Both records are always kept. A correction is a new record; the record it corrects is kept and
  shown struck through (HZ-004).
- You can also correct a dose record from the person's **Medicines** tab: choose **Dose records**,
  then **Correct this record**.
- If you decide on a dose you recorded or uploaded yourself, the Registered Manager reviews your
  decision later.
