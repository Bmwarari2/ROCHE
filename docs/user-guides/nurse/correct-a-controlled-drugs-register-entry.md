# How to correct a controlled drugs register entry

The controlled drugs register is never changed. A mistake is put right with a correction: a new
entry, witnessed like any other, shown under the entry it corrects.

**Who can do this**: senior carers (team leader or senior carer), nurses (clinical lead or nurse),
deputy managers, Registered Managers and owners, with a witness.

**Register row**: MEDS-005.

**Depends on**: H-1203 (a correction from given puts the dose back in stock), still open (H-1541).

> **Until your home's electronic controlled drugs register is turned on**, keep every controlled
> drug record for your home (receipts, doses given, counts and returns) in the paper controlled
> drugs register, and do not use these screens. One register is the record, so its balance is
> never split between paper and Thapsus Care (H-1517).

## Steps

1. Open the person's record, choose **Controlled drugs** and choose the drug.
2. Find the entry under **Entries**, for example **Entry 4**.
3. Ask your witness to sign first, signed in as themselves, choosing **A correction** under **What you
   saw**. For a wrong quantity they enter the change, for example -2; for a wrong detail, 0.
4. Reload the page. Under **Correct an entry**:
   - **Entry to correct**: choose the entry.
   - **What was wrong?**: **The quantity** or **A detail, such as the time or reference**.
   - For the quantity, **Change in** the page's unit: a plus number adds to stock, for example 2;
     a minus number takes away, for example -2.
   - **What was wrong**: say what was wrong and what is right.
   - **Witness**: choose your witness's signature.
5. Choose **Record the correction**. Confirm it is you if Thapsus Care asks.
6. The page says "Correction recorded under the entry it corrects."

## Important

- **Corrections are new entries, never edits.** The original stays as it was, and says **Corrected
  by entry** with the number. The correction says **Corrects entry** and what it corrects (HZ-115).
- A correction of a detail does not change the stock. A correction of the quantity does.
- A correction needs a witness, and you confirm it is you with your passkey or authenticator app,
  like any other entry (HZ-116).
- A stock count never changes the balance. If a count finds a difference, a correction puts the
  register right (see [How to count controlled drugs stock](count-controlled-drugs-stock.md)).
