# How to review the records for a disclosure request

Find the person's records for a disclosure request, decide for each one what goes in the pack,
and mark the request ready for approval.

**Who can do this**: nurses and clinical leads, deputy managers, the Registered Manager and the
owner, for people at the homes where they work.

**Register row**: SAFE-016.

## Before you start

- Log the request first (see [How to log a disclosure request](log-a-disclosure-request.md)).
- Read **The request**: who asked, **Legal basis** and **What they need it for**. Disclose only
  what that purpose needs.

## Collect the records

1. Choose **Disclosure requests** in the menu and open the request.
2. Under **Records**, choose **Collect the person's records**. Thapsus Care finds the person's
   records across every part of their record. The page says "Records collected".
3. Read the count, for example "12 records found: 12 still to review".
4. Read **Not searched** if it shows. It names the kinds of record Thapsus Care did not search and
   why. Gather those from your other records if the request needs them.

## Review each record

1. Open a record from the list by its title, for example a care note and its date. Each record
   shows **Still to review** until you decide.
2. Read **What the record holds**. Where it names other people, the page says so under the field,
   for example "Names of other people found here: Ben Sample. Consider redacting them."
3. Under **Decision on this record**, choose:
   - **Disclose**: the whole record goes in the pack;
   - **Disclose with parts removed**: tick each field to remove under **Fields to remove**; or
   - **Withhold**: the record is left out. Say why under **Why**.
4. Choose **Save decision**. You go back to the request. Do the same for every record.

## Mark it ready for approval

1. When no record is still to review, **Prepare for approval** shows on the request.
2. Choose **Mark ready for approval**. The state changes to **Ready for approval**.
3. Tell an approver: the Registered Manager, a deputy manager or the owner, who did not log the
   request (see
   [How to approve or refuse a disclosure request](../registered-manager/approve-or-refuse-a-disclosure-request.md)).

## Important

- Thapsus Care never removes anything by itself and never decides what to disclose. A person
  reviews every record.
- If a record changes after you reviewed it, it goes back to **Still to review**. Review it again.
- **Collect again** finds records added since. Review any new ones.
- Opening a record here is recorded as viewing the person's care record, with who did it.
- The police and solicitors must have said what they need it for before the request can be marked
  ready.
- The rules: [safety specification, disclosure requests](../../specs/safety.md#12-disclosure-requests-slice-41-safe-016).
