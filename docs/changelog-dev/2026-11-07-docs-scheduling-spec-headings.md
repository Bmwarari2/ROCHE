# Scheduling spec: one heading per section

A finding of the workforce and scheduling guides (PR #183): `docs/specs/scheduling.md` carried
lone-worker safety twice from a merge, as section 19 and as section 17 (each heading and its
subsections repeated), and two later sections reused 19 and 18.

- Lone-worker safety is section 19 only; its web screens are 19.8 and its test list 19.9.
- Payroll and pension exports are section 21, and shifts, the clock and lone working on the phone
  section 22 (with 22.1 to 22.6).
- Links and section references in the feature register, the human actions, the records of
  processing and the spec itself follow.
